# R2 notes (second pass over payments, billing, statements, fulfilment, marketing)

Paths are under packages/backend. Nothing was run; savings are estimates.

## Changed files

- libs/domains/marketing/ads-aggregation.e2e-spec.ts
  - The helper that reads the aggregate/DLQ topic waited a fixed 2.5 s (about 8 uses). It now ends once the reader has joined its group and 1 s has passed with no new message (the timer restarts at every message; hard cap 20 s). The old fixed wait included the group join, so the new quiet window starts only after the join and the fetch begins right after it. Where the test proves nothing is visible (killed before commit, empty DLQ side) it still reads from the start of the topic and sees nothing for 1 s after joining. Saves about 1 to 1.5 s per use, roughly 8 to 10 s.
  - The two fixed waits that prove a duplicate has no effect (1 s and 1.5 s) are kept: nothing can be polled for an absence.
  - Could break: on a very slow broker the first fetch could take longer than 1 s after the join; raise the default quiet time then. A kafkajs fetch event could not be used (its batch count is always 0 in 2.2.4).
- libs/domains/billing/invoice-charging.e2e-spec.ts: the 50 subscriptions of the "charged once" test are made 10 at a time (clock is the same fixed time for each, nothing charged yet). Saves about 5 to 10 s. Could break: if shop/user creation shares a lock.
- libs/domains/billing/subscription-change.e2e-spec.ts: the 30 "nothing to change" attempts before the 31st (429) are sent at once, every answer still checked 422. Saves about 1 s.
- libs/domains/billing/subscribe.e2e-spec.ts: the ten refused attempts before the eleventh are sent at once, each still 404. Saves under 1 s.
- libs/domains/billing/usage-read.e2e-spec.ts: 60 reads before the 61st sent as two waves of 30, each still 200. Saves about 1 to 2 s.
- libs/domains/fulfilment/delivery-request.e2e-spec.ts: 60 requests before the 61st sent as two waves of 30, each still 404. Saves about 1 to 3 s.
- libs/domains/payments/reconciliation-issues.e2e-spec.ts: the 45 issue rows (each with its own fixed time) are written together; the 30 seed-and-resolve steps before the 31st resolve run at once, each still 200. Saves about 2 to 4 s.
- libs/domains/payments/payment-read.e2e-spec.ts: the 45 payments (each with its own fixed time) are written together; the list keeps its order. Saves about 1 to 2 s.
- libs/domains/marketing/ads-campaigns.e2e-spec.ts: 59 refused (409) actions before the 61st mutation sent as waves of 30 and 29. Saves about 1 to 2 s.
- libs/domains/statements/period-close.e2e-spec.ts: 25 shops and owners created together. Saves about 1 to 2 s.
- Could break (all rate-limit ones): if the limiter counts per second, not per minute, or the test client cannot hold 30 open requests; same risk G4 accepted.

## payments/payout-transfer (not changed)

The 20-round and 50-round races use one shared fake provider (`transferCalls()` and `transfers.size` count everything), one shared clock, `t.run.run({})` that handles every seller, and `assertPayoutBooks` that checks the whole ledger. Separate sellers per round would make the counts of one round leak into another and the "exactly one transfer" proof would no longer be per round. Left as is; only reducing the rounds would save time and that weakens the proof.
