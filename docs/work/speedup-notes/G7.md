# G7 notes (apps/core, apps/worker, apps/sse-gateway, apps/local-monolith, apps/lambdas, rate-limit, realtime, cache, jobs)

## apps/worker/src/webhooks-wiring.e2e-spec.ts
- Changed: before each look at the readiness answer, the test throws away the answer the service remembers (it remembers it for 2 s). Same checks, same limits.
- Saving: about 6 s (4 changes of state, each used to wait up to 2 s for the remembered answer to run out).
- Could break: it reaches into a private field called `cached` of the readiness service. If that field is renamed, the 2 s wait comes back (test still passes, just slow).

## apps/worker/src/public-api-wiring.e2e-spec.ts
- Same change. Saving about 3 s (2 state changes).

## apps/worker/src/order-export-wiring.e2e-spec.ts
- Same change. Saving about 6 s (4 state changes).

## apps/worker/src/media-wiring.e2e-spec.ts
- Changed: the wait for the expiry job looked at the job every 0.5 s; now every 0.1 s, with the same overall limit of 30 s (300 looks instead of 60).
- Saving: about 0.4 s. Could break: nothing expected.

## libs/infrastructure/jobs/jobs-lease.e2e-spec.ts
- Changed: in the database-outage test, a fixed 1.5 s sleep waiting for the first error log is now a wait until that error log shows up (limit 10 s).
- Saving: about 1 s. Could break: nothing expected (the check after it is unchanged).
- Not changed: the 5 s "handler times out" test (AS-25). The smallest lease the job system accepts is 5 s, so that wait is real.

## libs/infrastructure/realtime/realtime-publisher.e2e-spec.ts
- Changed: in "entries older than the retention age are dropped", the retention is 0.8 s instead of 1.5 s (the rule "old entries are dropped and the key disappears when idle" does not depend on the exact length), the matching "time to live is at most the retention" number follows (800), and the two waits look every 50 ms instead of 200 ms.
- Saving: about 1.5 s (two waits of retention length each shrink by 0.7 s, plus less polling lag).
- Could break: on a very slow machine the three first publishes plus the length check must finish within 0.8 s; they are three quick calls.

## Looked at, could not improve (and why)
- All apps/core/src/*-wiring files, apps/core/test/app.e2e-spec.ts, apps/local-monolith/*, apps/sse-gateway/* wiring files, apps/lambdas/*: each starts one app once in beforeAll, tests take 1 to 50 ms, nearly all the time is loading code (measured in specs/.checkpoints/local-sweep.timing.jsonl). Nothing to cut.
- apps/worker other *-wiring files: same as above (one app start, no waits).
- libs/infrastructure/rate-limit/* (all): the tests move a fake clock instead of waiting; the only real waits are 25 ms polls and reconnect waits after cutting the connection, which depend on the client's own reconnect timing.
- libs/infrastructure/cache/* (all): expiry is already done with an injected fake clock; remaining real waits are 50 to 150 ms loaders that the tests need to overlap (stampede, single flight). Not shortened.
- libs/infrastructure/jobs/* except jobs-lease: waits are 100 to 300 ms "nothing more starts" checks and the lease clock is fake. The 5 s handler timeout in jobs-lease is the smallest lease the system accepts.
- libs/infrastructure/realtime/* except realtime-publisher: settings are already millisecond-sized (heartbeat 100, lifetime 1 s, drain 1 s, stall 400 ms). The backplane "heal" waits (up to about 2 s each in realtime-recovery) are the redis client's own reconnect delay, which a test cannot change.
- No describe.concurrent added anywhere: the realtime, cache and jobs groups share one store, count global metrics (MetricsRegistry) or wipe data in setup, so overlapping them is not safe without running them.
