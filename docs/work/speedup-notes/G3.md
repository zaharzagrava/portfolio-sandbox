# G3 notes (developer-platform, orders, auctions, launch-events)

## libs/domains/orders/checkout-stock.e2e-spec.ts
- Change: the test users (200 in two tests) are now made 25 at a time instead of one after another; the 251 waiting orders in the "sweeper takes at most 200" test are also made 25 at a time.
- Saving: about 15 to 40 seconds (about 450 one-by-one database round trips become about 20 rounds of 25).
- Could break: if making orders at the same time hits a database lock on the shared product row (then the reserve step would wait or fail); every check and number is unchanged.

## libs/domains/orders/flash-sale-checkout.e2e-spec.ts
- Change: the shared "open sales" helper now schedules and loads the sales 10 at a time instead of one by one (sales are for different products). Matters most in the "50 repeated runs" test, which opens 50 sales.
- Saving: about 10 to 20 seconds (100 one-by-one calls become about 10 rounds).
- Could break: if the sale-creating route does not like several calls at once for one shop (no lock was found in the code). Checks and numbers unchanged.
- Not changed: the 50 repeated runs themselves use the same 100 buyers one run after another, so they cannot overlap; the product creation sits in a helper file outside the spec.

## libs/domains/developer-platform/testing/webhook-e2e.fixture.ts (helper, shared by many webhook specs)
- Change: `seedWorld` (runs before almost every test) now makes its five people at the same time, then the three shop members at the same time, instead of one by one.
- Saving: about 0.1 to 0.2 seconds per test, over hundreds of webhook tests: roughly 30 to 60 seconds in total.
- Could break: nothing expected (each person is a separate row); if it did, every webhook spec would fail at the start, so it shows at once.

## libs/domains/developer-platform/webhook-endpoints.e2e-spec.ts
- Change 1: the three "20 rounds" tests (two enables at once, three creates at the 20 limit, same address twice) now give each round its own shop and run 5 rounds at the same time. The 18 setup creates in each round also go at the same time.
- Change 2: the "every route x every person" access test now runs the six people of one route at the same time (each with its own endpoint) instead of 60 separate rounds with a shop clean-up each.
- Change 3: the 120 allowed calls of the rate limit test go 20 at a time; the 121st still comes after them.
- Saving: about 60 to 120 seconds in total (the 20-limit test alone made 360 creates one after another).
- Could break: the rate limit test counts calls at the same time (if the counter is not exact under parallel calls it would let one through; still the 121st must be refused); the rounds now run next to each other, so a shared per-address or per-machine limit, if one exists, could show up.

## libs/domains/launch-events/live-delivery.e2e-spec.ts
- Change 1: the "20 times" pin/remove test now runs its 20 rounds five at a time (each round has its own stream and its own viewer).
- Change 2: the comment flood helper sends 125 comments at once instead of 50.
- Saving: about 8 to 15 seconds.
- Could break: five rounds at once share the same seller and the same 20 viewer accounts; if posting is limited per viewer, a round could be refused (the limiter is switched off in this file on purpose). The test already uses fake time, so other waits were already short.
- Looked at and left alone: the 15 second keep-alive wait uses fake time (no real waiting).

## libs/domains/developer-platform/infra/webhook-adapters.e2e-spec.ts
- Change: the six "20 rounds" tests (same address four times, the 20 limit, two enables, secret rotation, two workers claiming, breaker with five failures) now run their rounds five at a time through a small `eachRound` helper. Every round already used its own shop, endpoint or id.
- Saving: about 15 to 30 seconds.
- Could break: nothing shared between rounds was found; if the database pool were very small, the five rounds would just queue.

## libs/domains/launch-events/launch-events-holds.e2e-spec.ts
- Change: the "five parallel holds against a limit of two (20 runs)" test now runs its runs five at a time (each run has its own buyer and its own five seats).
- Saving: about 3 to 6 seconds.
- Could break: the event's seat records are shared but each run only counts its own buyer's rows. The other "20 runs" tests in this file were left alone: the 100-buyer seat race can give one buyer several seats if runs overlap (limit would fire), and the release-while-expiring test moves the shared fake clock.

## libs/domains/launch-events/launch-events-confirm.e2e-spec.ts
- Change: three "20 runs" tests (ten confirms at once, two holds owning one seat after a store fault, last seat sells out the event) now run their runs five at a time. Each run has its own buyer, hold and seats (the sell-out test even has its own event).
- Saving: about 8 to 15 seconds.
- Could break: the shared "any seat booked twice" counter must still be 0 (it counts the whole table, so more overlap makes it a stricter check, not a weaker one). The "confirm racing the expiry job" test was left alone because it moves the shared fake clock for each run.

## libs/domains/launch-events/launch-events-organizer.e2e-spec.ts
- Change: the "two closes at once, 20 times" part runs its rounds five at a time (each round has its own fresh event).
- Saving: about 2 to 4 seconds. Could break: nothing shared found.
- Left alone: the bookings-pages test (13 buyers one after another) because the order of confirm times matters for the paging check.

## libs/domains/launch-events/launch-events-seatmap.e2e-spec.ts

- Change 1: the "20 concurrent removals, 20 times" test runs its rounds five at a time (each round has its own comment).
- Change 2: the 120 allowed snapshot reads go 20 at a time; the 121st (the refused one) still comes after them.

## developer-platform: api-keys, public-api-versions, public-api-resources, public-api-logs-usage
- Change: the "20 repeats" tests that give every repeat its own shop or product now run their repeats five at a time through a small `eachRound` helper.
  - api-keys.e2e-spec.ts: "5 parallel key creations at the limit" and "two parallel rotations" (each repeat has a new shop).
  - public-api-versions.e2e-spec.ts: "two concurrent first-key creations" (new shop each time).
  - public-api-resources.e2e-spec.ts: "two parallel PATCH with one If-Match" (a new product each time).
  - public-api-logs-usage.e2e-spec.ts: "same event from two consumers at once" (each repeat has its own minute and request id).
- Saving: about 5 to 10 seconds per test, roughly 25 to 40 seconds in total.
- Could break: five repeats at the same time put more calls on the API at once; a per-address or per-machine limit could refuse some (the files clear limits in other places, so this is the thing to watch). Left alone: "revoke raced a cold cache" (api-keys, clears the shared limits), "concurrent pins" (versions, one shop for all repeats), timing test in public-api-auth (measures speed, must stay one at a time).

## developer-platform/webhooks.e2e-spec.ts and widget-sites.e2e-spec.ts
- Change: the "two rotations at once, 20 rounds" tests now run their rounds five at a time (each round makes its own endpoint or site, and deletes it at the end; the limit clean-up stays inside each round).
- Saving: about 4 to 8 seconds each.
- Could break: five endpoints or sites exist at once in the shop (the limit is 20 for endpoints; sites limit not checked, it is at least 5 as the old code held one at a time). The shop-wide rate limit is 120 mutating calls a minute: the calls are the same in number as before.
- Left alone (shared state across rounds): webhook-lifecycle "sweep" tests (a sweep disables every failing endpoint, so rounds would steal each other's race), widget-kill-lifecycle (each round deletes every site of the shop first).

## libs/domains/orders/payment-webhook.e2e-spec.ts

## libs/domains/launch-events/live-history.e2e-spec.ts and live-reactions.e2e-spec.ts

## auctions: auction-bidding, auction-anti-sniping, auction-operations, auction-close, auction-public, auctions (.e2e-spec.ts)
- Change: the `bidders(n)` helper (makes n test users, 50 to 100 in the big tests) now makes them 25 at a time instead of one after another. The users keep the same order in the returned list.
- Saving: about 1 to 4 seconds per test that makes 50 or more users; roughly 15 to 40 seconds over the six files.
- Could break: nothing expected (each user is its own row and sign-in).
