# R3 notes (second look at the big files of G2 and G5)

Nothing was run. Savings are estimates from reading.

## Changed
- libs/domains/assistant/assistant.e2e-spec.ts, "50 randomized resumes": the 50 trials ran one after another (each streams 600 chunks, about 1 s). Now 5 trials run side by side, 10 rounds. Each trial has its own user and conversation, and the scripted turns are identical (5 are queued before each round), so it does not matter which trial takes which one. All checks are unchanged. Saving about 30 to 40 s. Could break: under load fewer events may exist when the first read wants its random 2 to 9, but 600 chunks at 1 ms still give about 20 events; if it turns flaky, lower to 3 side by side.
- libs/domains/community/discussions-votes.e2e-spec.ts, "+1 racing -1, 20 times": the 20 races now run side by side (own post each), and the cache is flushed once, after all of them, then every post is read again from the store and compared with what it showed before. Same checks per post, including the cold-cache one. Saving about 3 to 6 s. Could break: the same reader votes in all 20 (40 votes, under the 60 per minute limit).
- libs/domains/media/video-upload.e2e-spec.ts: the 9 pending videos are made at once; the 20 starts before the "21st is 429" check go out at once (their answers were never looked at); the 120 resume calls before the 121st go out 30 at a time, each still must be 409. Saving about 3 to 6 s. Could break: only if the 20 simultaneous starts changed the count of calls the limiter sees (it counts every one).
- libs/domains/asset-library/asset-upload.e2e-spec.ts: the 120 prepare calls before the 121st go 30 at a time, each still must be 200. Saving about 2 to 4 s.
- libs/domains/catalog-sync/integration-manage.e2e-spec.ts: the 7 integrations of the paging test are made at once (their creation times are set by hand, so the order is the same); the 6 rounds of pause-vs-resume run side by side (each has its own integration). Saving about 2 to 4 s (each integration starts a small fake shop server).

## Read and not improved (reason)
- assistant/assistant.e2e-spec.ts, "100 runs" (AS-31): each run needs its three scripted turns in order and the next send must come right after the terminal event; the script queue is shared, so runs cannot overlap without mixing turns, and a pause between sends would weaken the race. Left.
- assistant/assistant-limits: the 20 sends of the per minute test and the other loops depend on order and on the shared clock; the rest are single steps.
- media/video-pipeline: loops (3 rounds, 6 delete races) share one queue stand-in and take turns delivering; the 500 ms wait lets worker A start before the lease is moved; left.
- media/video-upload: the 1.2 s wait is the permission expiry (already shortened by G5); hang/refuse test needs its real 5 s bound.
- catalog-sync/integration-lifecycle, integration-sync, integration-connect: only small loops (5 to 24 products), one 400 ms "still locked" wait that proves nothing finishes while the lock is held, retention drain loop that stops when empty. No safe saving.
- catalog/collab-rooms: sleeps of 100 to 500 ms are "nothing arrives" or reconnect waits (about 6 s in total); left. 260 raw frames are sent without waiting already.
- catalog/drafts-publish and discovery/search-projection: no sleeps or repeated waits found; time is app and index work.
