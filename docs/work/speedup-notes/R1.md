# R1 notes (second pass over orders, developer-platform, launch-events, auctions)

## Changed
- libs/domains/developer-platform/public-api-sandbox.e2e-spec.ts: the "10 parallel first calls of a test key, 20 repeats" test now runs its repeats five at a time (each repeat makes its own shop world and its own key). Saving about 5 to 8 seconds. Could break: more calls at once on the API; nothing shared between repeats was found.
- libs/domains/orders/order-read.e2e-spec.ts: the seeding of 45 + 5 orders (paging test) and 22 orders (limit test) now happens at the same time instead of one by one. Each order has its own time (or none) and id; the returned id list keeps its order. Saving about 1 to 2 seconds. Could break: nothing expected (plain inserts).
- libs/domains/orders/flash-sale-lifecycle.e2e-spec.ts: (1) `buyMany` makes its users and fills their carts at the same time, the buying itself stays one after another in the same order; (2) `setBuckets`, `setRemaining` set the bucket counters at the same time; (3) the sold-out race test fills the 24 carts at the same time. Saving about 3 to 8 seconds over the file. Could break: nothing expected (different users, different Redis keys).
