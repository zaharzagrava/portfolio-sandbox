- **S53.D-01** Should a parked outbox event (one that failed 10 times) stop being retried automatically, even though the customer-facing change it describes (an order, a payment, a refund request) is then not delivered until someone requeues it?
  - default: Park after 10 failed attempts, or at once on a non-retryable broker error. Later events of the same aggregate wait behind a failing one until it is parked. Today a failing row retries forever.
  - alternatives: Keep retrying forever with a growing delay. Park after a longer limit, or after a time window such as 24 hours, with an alert.
  - if wrong: Too low a limit means a longer broker outage leaves orders, payments or refunds unprocessed until someone requeues them by hand. Too high means a poison row delays everything for that aggregate.
