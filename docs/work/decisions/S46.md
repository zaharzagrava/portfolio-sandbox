- **S46.D-01** When billing cannot be read, should the assistant stop answering buyers (no free tokens), or keep going on a local default allowance?

  - alternatives: fall back to a conservative local monthly default when billing is down; fail open only for buyers already under their limit
  - if wrong: too strict means the assistant goes dark for all buyers during any billing outage; too loose means free tokens and uncapped provider spend

- **S46.D-02** What may buyers see and do about moderation: which messages are rejected, and what do they get told?
  - default: new moderation on input (`422 input_rejected` with a category; card numbers and secrets rejected as `sensitive_data`) and on output per chunk with the 256 characters before it; a flagged answer is cut and replaced by a refusal; if the moderator fails, `503 assistant_unavailable`
  - alternatives: moderate output only after the full answer; warn on sensitive data instead of rejecting; fail open when the moderator is down
  - if wrong: too strict blocks legitimate shoppers and shows refusals mid-answer; too loose lets harmful or sensitive content through
  - source: "No moderation exists anywhere in the module" (BREAKING)
  - status: open

- **S46.D-03** Should a failed, cancelled or refused reply leave no trace in the buyer's chat history?
  - default: a turn is saved only if it ends `done`, in one batch; the user's message is not kept for any other outcome, so a refused or failed question disappears from history
  - alternatives: keep the user message and mark the turn as failed; keep refusals visible to the buyer
  - if wrong: buyers lose their own questions after errors and may think the chat is broken; the other way risks a corrupted transcript that permanently breaks a conversation
  - source: "A turn is written in pieces: the user message" (BREAKING)
  - status: open

- **S46.D-04** How do we limit usage per buyer: how many replies at once, how many sends per minute, and what happens when the allowance runs out or a stream is dropped?
  - default: 3 concurrent replies per user (`429 too_many_active_turns`), 20 sends per minute, exhausted allowance gives `429 quota_exceeded` with `used`, `limit`, `resetsAt`; aborted calls are charged an estimate so dropping the connection does not give free answers
  - alternatives: 1 or 2 concurrent replies; no charge for aborted calls; a soft allowance that finishes the current reply
  - if wrong: too low frustrates active shoppers; too high multiplies provider cost and allowance overshoot
  - source: "A per-user cap on concurrent replies does not exist" (BREAKING)
  - status: open

- **S46.D-05** Can the assistant be stopped from answering with fake or unavailable models, and how long may a buyer wait before we switch model or give up?
  - default: startup fails if the real provider has no key or the scripted echo model is used in production; first-token deadline 30 s then retry once on the fallback model; per-model breaker opens after 5 consecutive failures for 30 s; both open gives `503`; whole turn capped at 240 s
  - alternatives: longer first-token wait (e.g. 60 s) before switching; no fallback model, show an error instead; a higher failure threshold before the breaker opens
  - if wrong: too short switches to the fallback (possibly costlier or weaker) on slow answers; too long leaves buyers staring at a blank reply
  - source: "No timeouts besides the 120 s SDK" (BREAKING)
  - status: open
