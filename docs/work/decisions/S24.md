# Decisions needing your call: S24 Product chat: channels, idempotent send, per-channel sequence, sync on reconnect, unread, receipts, presence, offline push

- **S24.D-01** Should anyone who is not an active member of a channel be blocked from reading its messages and details, even though today any signed-in user can read any channel?
  - default: members only; strangers and banned users get `404 channel_not_found`; only the by-product discovery route (public fields) stays open to any signed-in user
  - alternatives: keep reading open to all signed-in users; allow read-only access to a wider group such as the product's buyers
  - if wrong: if too strict, people who could see a conversation before lose it and the web app breaks for them; if too loose, private conversations stay exposed (it is a known leak today)
  - source: "Who may read a channel's history, its metadata" [BREAKING]
  - status: open

- **S24.D-02** Who is allowed to open a chat for a product and to moderate it (ban, mute, delete, archive)?
  - default: only a current member of the product's shop with the `products.write` permission, checked live on every action; the stored `OWNER` role alone no longer gives control, so someone who leaves the shop loses control immediately
  - alternatives: keep the product's original seller as owner regardless of shop membership; allow a separate chat-only admin role
  - if wrong: too strict locks out a seller who is no longer on the shop's team; too loose lets former staff keep moderating customer conversations
  - source: "Channel creation authority → a member of" [BREAKING]
  - status: open

- **S24.D-03** Who can see whether a user is online?
  - default: only users who share an active channel with the caller; everyone else looks offline; more than 100 IDs per query is refused
  - alternatives: keep it open to any signed-in user; remove online status from the product entirely
  - if wrong: too open exposes people's activity to strangers; too closed hides "online" from people who expect it, so the feature feels broken
  - source: "`GET /chat/presence` → only users who share" [BREAKING]
  - status: open

- **S24.D-04** In channels with more than 50 members, should read receipts, online indicators and offline push notifications be switched off?
  - default: above 50 active members there are no read or presence events and no delayed offline pushes
  - alternatives: raise the limit (for example 200 or 500) at higher infrastructure cost; keep the features for all sizes but send only counts or summaries
  - if wrong: too low and customers in busy channels get no notifications or receipts; too high and a large channel floods users and infrastructure with events
  - source: "Read receipts and presence broadcasts are suppressed" [BREAKING]
  - status: open

- **S24.D-05** What does an offline push notification show, and when does it go out?
  - default: it goes 30 seconds after a message if the recipient has not read it and is not online, at most one per recipient per channel every 5 minutes; it shows a preview of up to 120 characters and no sender name or e-mail
  - alternatives: show a sender name once a public display name exists; use a different delay or frequency (for example 60 seconds, or one per 15 minutes)
  - if wrong: too frequent annoys customers; too sparse means missed replies and lost sales; a missing sender name makes pushes less useful
  - source: "Offline push → emits `chat.message_escalated` (outbox)" [BREAKING]
  - status: open

- **S24.D-06** Should a member's own messages count as unread for them?
  - default: never; sending moves the author's read position to their own message, so only others' messages count as unread
  - alternatives: keep today's behaviour where the seller's own 5 messages show as 5 unread
  - if wrong: the unread badge differs from what users expect and the web app test asserting the old count must change
  - source: "A member's own messages are never unread" [BREAKING]
  - status: open

- **S24.D-07** How fast may a user send messages and call chat endpoints before being rate-limited?
  - default: sending is limited to 10 messages per 10 seconds per user (`429` above); sync, read, heartbeat, presence, channel creation and ticket requests also get limits, and all limits fail open if the limiter is down
  - alternatives: looser or stricter limits per action; fail closed so a limiter outage blocks traffic
  - if wrong: too tight blocks legitimate fast chatters and reconnect bursts; too loose allows spam and load
  - source: "New `429` limits on send, sync, read, heartbeat" [BREAKING]
  - status: open

- **S24.D-08** What happens to a shop's chats when the shop is deleted or goes through offboarding?
  - default: deleting the shop permanently purges its channels, members and messages; the start of offboarding does nothing, so no export of other people's messages is offered
  - alternatives: keep messages for a retention period before purging; offer participants or the shop an export during offboarding
  - if wrong: irreversible loss of conversations that may be needed for disputes or legal retention, or a data-retention problem if kept too long
  - source: "Shop lifecycle → S24 consumes `tenancy.shop_deleted` v1" [CONTRACT]
  - status: open
