- **S49.D-05** How long does a duplicate-protection key block repeat jobs?

- **S49.D-06** When a schedule hits a daylight-saving change, how many times does it fire?

  - alternatives: fire twice on the repeated hour. Or skip runs that fall in the gap.
  - if wrong: time-of-day jobs such as daily reports or scheduled publishing run twice or are missed on two nights a year.
  - source: "DST semantics fixed: nonexistent local time fires once after" [BREAKING]
