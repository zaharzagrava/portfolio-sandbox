# Decisions needing your call: S06 Collaborative listing drafts (CRDT rooms, permissions per update, version history, publish)
The file records 30 defaults, and 7 of them need you.

- **S06.D-01** When a collaborative draft is published but the product was changed elsewhere in the meantime, should the publish be blocked unless the seller explicitly confirms?
  - default: Publish is refused with `409 product_version_conflict` unless the publisher sends `acknowledgeProductVersion` for the current product version; the draft records the product version it started from.
  - alternatives: Last publish always wins and overwrites the other changes; or merge the draft and the product changes field by field.
  - if wrong: Too strict means sellers click through an extra confirmation dialog; too loose means a collaborative edit silently erases price or stock changes made elsewhere.
  - source: "Lost-update guard: publish overwrote the product blindly" [BREAKING]
  - status: open

- **S06.D-02** What can a person who only has read access to a shop's drafts do, and what do they see when they try to edit?
  - default: Read-only members can open the draft but cannot change it; their edits are dropped, they get a permission-denied message, the attempt is counted and logged, and the connection stays open. Access is rechecked on every update, and a removed member is cut off within 5 seconds (backstop re-check every 60 s).
  - alternatives: Disconnect the viewer on the first attempted edit; or hide the draft from read-only members entirely.
  - if wrong: Viewers may be confused by silent-looking failures, or lose access they expected; wrong timing leaves removed staff able to see or edit drafts for a while.
  - source: "Read-only writes: silently dropped → dropped, answered" [BREAKING]
  - status: open

- **S06.D-03** Should drafts of a suspended shop be unavailable, including reading them?
  - default: Yes. Every draft route, including read-only ones, is refused with `403 shop_suspended` while the shop is suspended, and open rooms are closed when the shop is no longer active.
  - alternatives: Allow reading and exporting drafts while suspended, and block only writes and publishing.
  - if wrong: Suspended sellers cannot even look at or copy out their work, which may cause support complaints; or the opposite if reads should have been blocked.
  - source: "S03 `ShopScoped` gate on every draft route including" [CONTRACT]
  - status: open

- **S06.D-04** Is the price in a draft now stored as whole minor units (such as cents), and is it acceptable that older drafts with the old price field are not converted?
  - default: The field is renamed `fields.price` → `fields.priceMinor`, whole numbers only; non-integer values are rejected as `listing_invalid`. No data migration, on the basis that there is no production data.
  - alternatives: Migrate existing drafts to the new field; or keep accepting decimal prices and convert them on input.
  - if wrong: If any real drafts exist, their prices are lost or rejected and sellers must re-enter them.
  - source: "Document key `fields.price` → `fields.priceMinor` (integer" [BREAKING]
  - status: open

- **S06.D-05** What are the limits on drafts, versions and editing sessions that sellers will run into?
  - default: 200 open drafts per shop; 200 named versions per draft; 2 MiB per document; 50 connections per room and 5 per user; 200 updates per second; 30 connects per minute per user; the draft list shows 20 items by default (maximum 100). Previously the list was capped at 100 with no paging.
  - alternatives: Higher limits for larger shops or paid plans; or no per-shop draft cap.
  - if wrong: Busy shops hit "limit reached" errors, or limits that are too loose let one abusive client raise costs.
  - source: "New limits and close codes: 2 MiB document, 200" [BREAKING]
  - status: open

- **S06.D-06** What does the publish step require of a listing, and what happens to missing data?
  - default: Brand is required and publishing fails with `422 listing_invalid` otherwise; a draft without a category is published under `uncategorized`; product limits (title, price 1…10,000,000,000, quantity, text lengths) come from S05. Publishing a draft identical to the product succeeds and changes nothing.
  - alternatives: Make category required as well; or make brand optional.
  - if wrong: Products appear in the storefront in a catch-all category, or sellers are blocked from publishing when they expected it to work.
  - source: "S05 validation → `create/update` throw `ProductValidationError`" [CONTRACT]
  - status: open

- **S06.D-07** Is the existing publish behaviour change acceptable, where a repeated publish now returns the stored result and the client must send an idempotency key?
  - default: `Idempotency-Key` is required; a replay within 24 hours returns the original result, a concurrent twin gets `409`, a changed body gets `422`. Publishing an already published draft gives `409 invalid_transition` instead of the old `400`. The response now returns `{productId, productVersion, versionId}`.
  - alternatives: Make the key optional with server-side de-duplication; or keep the old response shape and `400` on repeat.
  - if wrong: Existing clients that don't send the key break at publish, so the web editor and any other caller must be updated together.
  - source: "Publish protocol: single unguarded read-then-write returning" [BREAKING]
  - status: open
