- **S17.D-01** When a customer's payment keeps failing, how long do we keep retrying and when do we cut off access?
  - default: retries at 1, 3 and 7 days after the first failure; the fourth decline makes the invoice uncollectible and the subscription unpaid, which is cancelled after 30 days
  - alternatives: a shorter or longer schedule (for example 1/5/14 days); downgrade to the starter plan instead of cancelling
  - if wrong: lost revenue if too short, unpaid access given away if too long, and customers cut off sooner than they expect
  - source: [BREAKING] Dunning delays are applied from the time of each failure
  - status: open

- **S17.D-02** Can a customer get a free trial, and who decides its length?

- **S17.D-03** Should a customer without a payment method be allowed to subscribe to a paid plan?

  - if wrong: a lost sign-up if too strict, or plans handed out that can never be collected if too loose
  - source: [BREAKING] Subscribe without a payment method creates an `ACTIVE`
  - status: open

- **S17.D-04** When a customer downgrades mid-period, what happens to the unused money?
  - default: it becomes a credit balance on the subscription, applied to later invoices up to their total, never negative, and never paid out as a refund
  - alternatives: refund the difference to the card; no credit, the downgrade takes effect at the period end
  - if wrong: customers feel cheated if credit is lost, or finance owes cash refunds that were not expected
  - source: [BREAKING] Net credit from a downgrade is an `OPEN` negative
  - status: open

- **S17.D-05** How is a mid-period plan change rounded and charged?

  - alternatives: charge the new price at the next renewal; prorate by the second instead of by day
  - if wrong: a cent or two of difference per change, and disputes if customers see invoices that do not match what they expected
  - source: [BREAKING] Proration rounds each line independently on float math
  - status: open
