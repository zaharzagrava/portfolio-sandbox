# S28 - Notification routing, preferences, quiet hours, caps, per-channel queues, provider failover, inbox, suppression

## Purpose
A fact happens on the platform and the right people hear about it exactly once, in their language, on the channels they chose, at a time that respects them. Other capabilities only publish events; this one turns them into an inbox item, a bell update and queued e-mail, SMS or push messages. A provider outage or a marketing campaign never blocks the rest, and bad addresses stop receiving mail for good. See {{concept:projection-and-replay}}, {{concept:rate-limit-policy}}, {{concept:job-scheduler-lease}} and {{concept:realtime-topic-stream}}.

## Entry points
- Event consumers, one per source family over the S53 framework, plus the SQS queue `tenancy-invite-requested`: identity, tenancy, shop onboarding, orders, payments, delivery, auctions, billing, payouts, launch events, chat, discussions, seller insights, developer platform and feed. [proof: S28 AS-01, S28 AS-08]
- `GET /notifications`, `GET /notifications/unread-count`, `POST /notifications/read`: the caller's inbox and bell. [code: packages/backend/libs/domains/notifications/api/notifications.controller.ts#NotificationsController]
- `GET` and `PUT /notifications/preferences`, `PUT /notifications/settings`: preferences and quiet hours. [proof: S28 AS-14, S28 AS-15, S28 AS-16, S28 AS-19, S28 AS-20]
- Phone verification: request a code and verify it, both under the settings path of the same controller (added with the preferences work). [proof: S28 AS-21, S28 AS-22, S28 AS-23]
- `GET` and `POST /notifications/devices`, `DELETE /notifications/devices/:deviceId`: push devices. [proof: S28 AS-28, S28 AS-29]
- `GET` and `POST /notifications/unsubscribe?token=`: one-click unsubscribe, anonymous. [proof: S28 AS-25, S28 AS-26]
- `POST /notifications/webhooks/ses` and `POST /notifications/webhooks/twilio`: provider callbacks. [code: packages/backend/libs/domains/notifications/api/notification-webhooks.controller.ts#NotificationWebhooksController]
- Delivery lines `notify-{email,sms,push}` and `notify-marketing-{email,sms,push}`, the queue `notifications-callbacks` and the jobs `notifications.wake-delivery` and `notifications.reconcile-unread`. [proof: none]
- `PASSWORD_RESET_MAILER` port bound by `TransactionalMailModule`, and `GET /dev/mailbox` outside production. [code: packages/backend/libs/domains/notifications/api/dev-mailbox.controller.ts#DevMailboxController]
- Realtime out: event `notification` on the owner-only topic `user:<userId>`.

## Rules
### Intake and routing
- **S28.R-01** The consumer MUST validate each payload against the schema of its type, map it to zero or more notification requests, ignore a [known-unmapped type](../terms/known-unmapped-type.md) with a counter, and [dead-letter](../terms/dead-letter-topic.md) a malformed payload or an [unsupported version](../terms/unsupported-version.md) with a reason and the [event id](../terms/event-id.md). [proof: S28 AS-04, S28 AS-05, S28 AS-13]
- **S28.R-02** A notification MUST have one [dedupe identity](../terms/dedupe-identity.md): source event id (or the owner's own key), recipient and [channel](../terms/notification-channel.md). Processing it again, in sequence or at once, MUST create no second [inbox item](../terms/inbox-item.md), [delivery](../terms/notification-delivery.md), realtime event or count. [proof: S28 AS-02]
- **S28.R-03** Each [routing step](../terms/routing-step.md) MUST be safe to repeat after a crash at any point, so that the final state equals one uninterrupted run; the [done marker](../terms/done-marker.md) is a shortcut and never the only guard. [proof: S28 AS-03]
- **S28.R-04** A request that cannot be rendered MUST be dead-lettered without [side effects](../terms/side-effects.md) and MUST NOT affect the other requests of its [batch](../terms/consumer-batch.md). [proof: S28 AS-12]
- **S28.R-05** For an [aggregate](../terms/aggregate-id.md) with a [monotonic version](../terms/monotonic-version.md), the consumer MUST drop an event whose version is lower than the highest one already notified for that aggregate and [family](../terms/notification-family.md). [proof: S28 AS-10]
- **S28.R-06** An event older than the [maximum age](../terms/maximum-age.md) of its type MUST NOT cause an external delivery (timeline [`expired`](../terms/delivery-timeline-expired.md)) but MUST still create its inbox item; a delivery held for [quiet hours](../terms/quiet-hours.md) MUST re-check the age at its [wake instant](../terms/wake-instant.md). [proof: S28 AS-11, S28 AS-34]
- **S28.R-07** Recipients MUST be resolved only through the [exported services](../terms/exported-service.md) of [identity](../terms/identity-domain.md) and [tenancy](../terms/tenancy-domain-layer.md), batched once per consumed batch with at most 500 ids per call; an unknown user produces nothing. [proof: S28 AS-06, S28 AS-07]
- **S28.R-08** A notification addressed to an e-mail address (an invitation) MUST go to the [e-mail line](../terms/email-line.md) only, with no inbox item and no [preference lookup](../terms/preference-lookup.md), and stays subject to [suppression](../terms/suppression.md). [proof: S28 AS-08]
- **S28.R-09** A notification MUST be rendered once per recipient in the recipient's locale at [routing time](../terms/routing-step.md); money MUST be formatted from [integer minor units](../terms/minor-units.md) and a currency code. [proof: S28 AS-01]
- **S28.R-10** Per recipient the [router](../terms/notification-router.md) MUST create the inbox item first, then for each other allowed channel apply the [marketing cap](../terms/marketing-cap.md) and quiet hours, then hand the delivery to its line; a failed step leaves the earlier steps intact and the event [retriable](../terms/retryable.md). [proof: S28 AS-03, S28 AS-30, S28 AS-35]
- **S28.R-11** [Follower fan-out](../terms/follower-fan-out.md) of a marketing notice MUST be [resumable](../terms/resumable-fan-out.md) and [idempotent ↗](../sw-external-terms.md#idempotency): a retry walks the followers again and creates only what is missing. [proof: none]
- **S28.R-12** Each delivery MUST have a [timeline](../terms/delivery-timeline.md) with the statuses `queued`, `delayed`, `sent`, `delivered`, [`bounced`](../terms/delivery-bounced.md), `complained`, `failed`, [`suppressed`](../terms/suppression.md), [`capped`](../terms/delivery-capped.md), `skipped` and `expired`; a timeline write failure MUST NOT fail or repeat a send. [proof: none]
- **S28.R-13** Secrets (reset and invite tokens, one-time codes, [unsubscribe tokens](../terms/unsubscribe-token.md)) MUST appear only in the message handed to [the provider](../terms/mail-provider.md), never in logs, timeline, metrics, [dead-letter records](../terms/dead-letter-topic.md) or [inbox items](../terms/inbox-item.md). [proof: S28 AS-09]
- **S28.R-14** The password-reset mail MUST go through the [`PASSWORD_RESET_MAILER` port](../terms/password-reset-mailer-port.md) with the link `<front>/reset-password#token=<token>`, using the [catalog template](../terms/catalog-template.md) and [redaction](../terms/template-redaction.md). [proof: S28 AS-09]

### Preferences, settings, phone, devices
- **S28.R-15** The [preference matrix](../terms/preference-matrix.md) MUST hold one boolean per [category](../terms/notification-category.md) and [channel](../terms/notification-channel.md); a stored value is an [explicit override](../terms/explicit-override.md), and [marketing external channels](../terms/marketing-external-channels.md) and SMS default to off. [proof: S28 AS-14]
- **S28.R-16** `GET` and `PUT` of preferences and [settings](../terms/notification-settings.md) MUST return the full matrix, be [idempotent ↗](../sw-external-terms.md#idempotency), and report [`mandatoryChannels`](../terms/mandatory-channels.md) and a [masked phone](../terms/masked-phone.md). [proof: S28 AS-14, S28 AS-15, S28 AS-21]
- **S28.R-17** A [mandatory type](../terms/mandatory-type.md) MUST ignore category and channel [opt-outs](../terms/opt-out.md) and [quiet hours](../terms/quiet-hours.md), and MUST NOT ignore [suppression](../terms/suppression.md). [proof: S28 AS-17]
- **S28.R-18** Enabling SMS for any category MUST require a [verified phone](../terms/verified-phone.md), otherwise the answer is [`422 phone_not_verified`](../terms/phone-not-verified.md). [proof: S28 AS-18]
- **S28.R-19** [Phone ownership](../terms/verified-phone.md) MUST be proven by a [six-digit code](../terms/phone-verification-code.md) valid 10 minutes, with 5 failed attempts [locking](../terms/phone-code-lockout.md) for 15 minutes and 3 [code requests](../terms/phone-code-request-limit.md) per hour; changing the phone clears the verification. [proof: S28 AS-21, S28 AS-22, S28 AS-23]
- **S28.R-20** After a preference or settings write, no process MUST [route](../terms/notification-routing.md) with the previous values: the [recipient view](../terms/recipient-view.md) is [invalidated on write](../terms/invalidation-only-writes.md) and lives at most 5 minutes. [proof: S28 AS-15, S28 AS-19]
- **S28.R-21** [Quiet hours](../terms/quiet-hours.md) MUST be set and cleared as a pair (`[quietStart](../terms/quiet-hours.md)`, `quietEnd` as `HH:MM`, not equal), the time zone MUST be an [IANA name ↗](../sw-external-terms.md#iana-time-zone), and the locale MUST be `en` or `uk`; the store enforces the pair with a [CHECK constraint ↗](../sw-external-terms.md#check-constraint). [proof: S28 AS-20]
- **S28.R-22** A [device registration](../terms/device-registration.md) MUST move a known token to the latest registering user, keep at most 10 devices per user by evicting the oldest, and never return the token; removal is by device id and owner only. [proof: S28 AS-28, S28 AS-29]
- **S28.R-23** `GET /notifications/unsubscribe` MUST only describe the action. `POST` MUST switch off the e-mail channel of the [category](../terms/notification-category.md) named in a [signed, non-expiring token](../terms/unsubscribe-token.md). Every bad token answers `400 [invalid_unsubscribe_token](../terms/invalid-unsubscribe-token.md)` without a reason. [code: packages/backend/libs/domains/notifications/domain/unsubscribe-token.ts] [proof: S28 AS-25, S28 AS-26]
- **S28.R-24** An e-mail of a [non-mandatory type](../terms/mandatory-notification-type.md) MUST carry the signed unsubscribe URL and the [one-click headers ↗](../sw-external-terms.md#list-unsubscribe-one-click); a mandatory type MUST carry neither. [proof: S28 AS-27]

### Quiet hours and caps
- **S28.R-25** Quiet hours MUST delay push and SMS only, in the recipient's time zone, correct across [daylight-saving changes ↗](../sw-external-terms.md#daylight-saving-time) and overnight windows, using the [injected clock](../terms/injected-clock.md). [code: packages/backend/libs/domains/notifications/domain/quiet-hours.ts#quietHoursEnd] [proof: S28 AS-30, S28 AS-32, S28 AS-33]
- **S28.R-26** A delay up to 900 seconds MUST use the [queue delay](../terms/queue-delay.md); a longer delay MUST use exactly one scheduled job `[notifications.wake-delivery](../terms/notifications-wake-delivery-job.md)` per delivery. [proof: S28 AS-30, S28 AS-31]
- **S28.R-27** At its [wake instant](../terms/wake-instant.md) a delayed delivery MUST be re-checked for [maximum age](../terms/delivery-maximum-age.md), [suppression](../terms/delivery-suppression.md) and the user's current preference. [proof: S28 AS-34, S28 AS-51, S28 AS-52]
- **S28.R-28** [Marketing deliveries](../terms/marketing-delivery.md) MUST obey a [rolling per-user, per-channel cap](../terms/marketing-delivery-cap.md): push 3 per hour, e-mail 2 per 24 hours, SMS 1 per 24 hours; [transactional deliveries](../terms/transactional-delivery.md) MUST NOT be capped. A capped delivery is recorded `capped` and not queued; its [inbox item](../terms/inbox-item.md) stays. [proof: S28 AS-35, S28 AS-38]
- **S28.R-29** The cap decision MUST be [atomic ↗](../sw-external-terms.md#atomicity) under concurrency, final per [delivery id](../terms/delivery-id.md), and [fail closed ↗](../sw-external-terms.md#fail-open-fail-closed) for marketing when the [counting store](../terms/rate-limit-counter-store.md) is down. [proof: S28 AS-36, S28 AS-37, S28 AS-39]

### Lines, providers, failover
- **S28.R-30** E-mail, SMS and push MUST each have a [line](../terms/delivery-line.md), and marketing MUST have its own line per channel with lower [worker capacity](../terms/worker-capacity.md), so a backlog or outage on one line never delays another. [proof: S28 AS-40, S28 AS-41]
- **S28.R-31** Each provider MUST have a [send budget](../terms/send-budget.md) enforced by the [rate limiter](../terms/rate-limiter-service.md); a delivery over budget [returns to its line](../terms/delivery-line.md) with the [retry-after delay](../terms/retry-after-ms.md) and [uses no attempt](../terms/job-attempt.md). With the limiter down, [transactional sends](../terms/transactional-send.md) [fail open ↗](../sw-external-terms.md#fail-open-fail-closed) and marketing sends fail closed. [proof: S28 AS-42, S28 AS-43]
- **S28.R-32** Each channel MUST have an [ordered provider chain](../terms/provider-chain.md). A [transient failure](../terms/transient-failure.md) or a timeout of 10 seconds MUST fail over to the next provider in the same attempt, and a [circuit breaker ↗](../sw-external-terms.md#circuit-breaker) MUST skip an open provider until its [probe](../terms/circuit-breaker-probe.md). [proof: S28 AS-44, S28 AS-45, S28 AS-53]
- **S28.R-33** A [permanent provider failure](../terms/permanent-error.md) MUST NOT fail over or retry: the address is [suppressed](../terms/suppression.md) or the [dead push token](../terms/dead-push-token.md) removed, and the delivery is [`failed`](../terms/delivery-status.md). [proof: S28 AS-46]
- **S28.R-34** A transient failure of the whole chain MUST retry through the line with [exponential backoff ↗](../sw-external-terms.md#exponential-backoff) (base 30 s, factor 2, cap 900 s, [full jitter](../terms/jitter.md)) for at most 5 attempts, then [dead-letter ↗](../sw-external-terms.md#dead-letter-queue) as `failed` with reason [`exhausted`](../terms/dead-letter-reason-code.md). [proof: S28 AS-47, S28 AS-48]
- **S28.R-35** A delivery already sent MUST never be handed to a provider again, including under [concurrent copies](../terms/concurrent-delivery-copies.md); a [malformed queue message](../terms/malformed-queue-message.md) is [dead-lettered without its body](../terms/dead-letter-without-body.md). [proof: S28 AS-49, S28 AS-50]
- **S28.R-36** Before each send the worker MUST check [suppression](../terms/suppression.md), the user's current preference ([non-mandatory types](../terms/mandatory-notification-type.md) only) and the [maximum age](../terms/notification-maximum-age.md). [proof: S28 AS-34, S28 AS-51, S28 AS-52]
- **S28.R-37** In production, [startup MUST fail](../terms/startup-validation.md) when a channel used by the [catalog](../terms/notification-catalog.md) has no provider credentials or [`NOTIFICATION_SECRET`](../terms/notification-secret.md) is missing or shared with another secret; [stand-ins](../terms/provider-stand-in.md) outside production report [`provider: "log"`](../terms/log-provider.md) and never [`delivered`](../terms/delivery-status.md). [proof: S28 AS-54]

### Inbox and bell
- **S28.R-38** [`GET /notifications`](../terms/get-notifications.md) MUST return the caller's items only, newest first by [event time ↗](../sw-external-terms.md#event-time) then id, with [signed cursors bound to the caller](../terms/pagination-cursor.md), limit 1 to 50 (default 20) and [180-day retention](../terms/bounded-retention.md). [proof: S28 AS-55, S28 AS-56, S28 AS-64]
- **S28.R-39** The [unread count](../terms/unread-count.md) MUST equal the number of the caller's unread items [bounded at 1,000](../terms/unread-count-ceiling.md), be read in [constant time ↗](../sw-external-terms.md#constant-time), rebuild after the loss of its store, and be repaired within 5 minutes after a crash [between store and count](../terms/unread-count-repair.md). [proof: S28 AS-57, S28 AS-61, S28 AS-62]
- **S28.R-40** Marking read MUST be [idempotent ↗](../sw-external-terms.md#idempotency), [owner-scoped](../terms/caller-scoping.md) and safe under concurrency with [one decrement per item](../terms/unread-count.md); [mark-all](../terms/mark-all-read.md) MUST use a [watermark](../terms/watermark.md) and leave later arrivals unread. [proof: S28 AS-58, S28 AS-59, S28 AS-60]
- **S28.R-41** A new [inbox item](../terms/inbox-item.md) MUST be announced on the owner's [private realtime topic](../terms/realtime-topic.md), event `notification`, on a [best-effort basis](../terms/best-effort-push.md): a [hub failure](../terms/realtime-hub.md) MUST NOT fail [routing](../terms/notification-routing.md). [proof: S28 AS-01, S28 AS-63]
- **S28.R-42** Inbox text MUST be fixed in the locale of its creation, with titles up to 120 and bodies up to 500 characters and a relative link [built by the catalog](../terms/notification-catalog.md); a user MAY switch the [in-app channel](../terms/notification-channel.md) off per [category](../terms/notification-category.md). [proof: S28 AS-65, S28 AS-66]

### Callbacks and suppression
- **S28.R-43** [Suppression](../terms/suppression.md) MUST be per channel and [normalized address](../terms/normalize-email.md) (e-mail trimmed and lower-cased, phone in [E.164 ↗](../sw-external-terms.md#e164)), [idempotent ↗](../sw-external-terms.md#idempotency), in effect for the next send, and honoured by every type including [mandatory ones](../terms/mandatory-notification.md). [proof: S28 AS-17, S28 AS-76]
- **S28.R-44** A [provider callback](../terms/provider-callback.md) MUST be authenticated by the provider's scheme, acknowledged `2xx` only after [durable acceptance](../terms/durable-acceptance.md), [deduplicated](../terms/callback-deduplication.md) on the provider's event id and applied once by the [worker](../terms/worker-instance.md). An [SNS message ↗](../sw-external-terms.md#sns-message) older or newer than 5 minutes MUST be refused. [proof: S28 AS-69, S28 AS-70, S28 AS-75]
- **S28.R-45** Callback processing MUST NOT call a URL taken from the callback unless it is HTTPS on the [pinned provider host](../terms/pinned-address.md). [proof: S28 AS-73]
- **S28.R-46** A [complaint](../terms/complaint.md), a [permanent bounce](../terms/bounce.md), an [SMS STOP](../terms/sms-stop.md) and a [permanent provider error](../terms/permanent-error.md) MUST suppress the address; a complaint MUST also switch off the user's [marketing e-mail](../terms/notification-category.md) when the user is known. [proof: S28 AS-67, S28 AS-68, S28 AS-74, S28 AS-77]
- **S28.R-47** A [delivery status](../terms/delivery-status.md) MUST only move forward by rank (`queued` 0, `delayed` 1, `sent` 2, `delivered` 3, `bounced` 4, `complained` 5); a [pre-send terminal status](../terms/pre-send-terminal-status.md) (`suppressed`, `capped`, `skipped`, `expired`) is reachable only from `queued` or `delayed`. A callback that arrives before the [provider message id](../terms/provider-message-id.md) is recorded MUST be retried for up to 10 minutes, then [dropped and counted](../terms/dropped-late-event.md). [proof: S28 AS-71, S28 AS-72]

### Templates
- **S28.R-48** Templates MUST be [logic-less ↗](../sw-external-terms.md#logic-less-template) `{{variable}}` text per type and [locale](../terms/locale.md); [HTML values are escaped ↗](../sw-external-terms.md#cross-site-scripting), a missing variable fails the render, and the locale resolves as exact, then language, then English. [proof: S28 AS-78, S28 AS-79, S28 AS-80]
- **S28.R-49** Subjects MUST have [CR and LF ↗](../sw-external-terms.md#crlf-injection) removed, push and inbox text MUST respect their length limits, and links MUST come from the [catalog path templates](../terms/notification-catalog.md) with identifiers made of letters, digits, `_` and `-` only. [proof: S28 AS-82]
- **S28.R-50** The catalog MUST hold every notification type with its category (one of 13), [default channels](../terms/notification-channel.md), [mandatory flag](../terms/mandatory-notification.md), [recipient rule](../terms/recipient-rule.md), [maximum age](../terms/notification-maximum-age.md), [line](../terms/notification-line.md), link and `en` and `uk` templates. [code: packages/backend/libs/domains/notifications/domain/catalog.ts] [proof: S28 AS-87]
- **S28.R-56** Money in a message MUST be formatted from integer [minor units](../terms/minor-units.md) with the currency's own number of decimals in the recipient's locale, and a non-integer amount MUST [fail the render](../terms/render-failure.md). [proof: S28 AS-81]

### Boundaries and operations
- **S28.R-51** The capability MUST read and write only the [tables it owns](../terms/table-ownership.md) (`NotificationPreference`, `NotificationSettings`, `NotificationSuppression`, `PushDevice`) and its own inbox, timeline and counter stores; other data enters through [exported services](../terms/exported-service.md) or [events](../terms/event-backbone.md). [proof: none]
- **S28.R-52** The [public entry point](../terms/entry-point.md) MUST export only the module classes, the [rate-limit policies](../terms/rate-limit-policy.md) and the [in-memory mailbox](../terms/in-memory-mailbox.md) of the dev and test e-mail provider, no router service, projector class, formatter or model. [code: packages/backend/libs/domains/notifications/index.ts] [proof: none]
- **S28.R-53** Every endpoint except [unsubscribe and callbacks](../terms/unauthenticated-endpoint.md) MUST authenticate, take the [principal](../terms/principal.md) from the session, answer errors as [`application/problem+json` ↗](../sw-external-terms.md#problem-json) with a [stable code](../terms/error-code.md), and apply the rate-limit policies of the domain ({{concept:problem-json-errors}}). [code: packages/backend/libs/domains/notifications/rate-limit-policies.ts] [proof: none]
- **S28.R-54** Logs MUST be structured and carry no contact data, token or message body; the metrics listed in the [service contract](../terms/service-contract.md) MUST exist under their fixed names with [bounded labels ↗](../sw-external-terms.md#metric-labels). [proof: S28 AS-85, S28 AS-86]
- **S28.R-55** Transactions MUST go through the [transaction runner](../terms/transaction-runner.md) only ({{concept:transaction-runner}}), and the domain MUST NOT open a transaction on its own. [proof: none]

## Behaviors
- **S28.B-01** Given a paid order, when [`order.paid`](../terms/order-paid-event.md) is consumed, then the buyer has one [inbox item](../terms/inbox-item.md), an [unread count](../terms/unread-count.md) of 1, a queued confirmation e-mail and a live [`notification` event](../terms/realtime-event.md). [proof: S28 AS-01]
- **S28.B-02** Given the same event delivered 20 times at once, then exactly one inbox item, [one delivery](../terms/delivery.md) and one count exist. [proof: S28 AS-02]
- **S28.B-03** Given a queue that [refuses once](../terms/queue-refusal.md), when the event is [replayed](../terms/event-replay.md), then the final state equals one uninterrupted run. [proof: S28 AS-03]
- **S28.B-04** Given a batch with a [malformed payload](../terms/event-payload.md), then it is [dead-lettered](../terms/dead-letter-topic.md) with a [reason](../terms/dead-letter-reason-code.md) and the other requests of the batch complete. [proof: S28 AS-04]
- **S28.B-05** Given an invitation event, then one e-mail with the link `<front>/invites/<token>` is [queued](../terms/notification-queue.md) and no [inbox item](../terms/inbox-item.md) exists. [proof: S28 AS-08]
- **S28.B-06** Given an [outbid event](../terms/outbid-event.md) during [quiet hours](../terms/quiet-hours.md), then the inbox item exists at once and the [push](../terms/push-notification.md) is delayed to the end of the window. [proof: S28 AS-30, S28 AS-31]
- **S28.B-07** Given four [marketing e-mails](../terms/marketing-email.md) in 24 hours for one user, then the third is recorded [`capped`](../terms/capped-delivery.md) and the inbox item is still created. [proof: S28 AS-38]
- **S28.B-08** Given the SMS provider fails, when one event has e-mail and SMS, then the e-mail is sent and the SMS retries with [backoff ↗](../sw-external-terms.md#exponential-backoff). [proof: S28 AS-40, S28 AS-47]
- **S28.B-09** Given the primary e-mail provider fails with a [transient error](../terms/transient-error.md), then the same attempt sends through the [fallback provider](../terms/fallback-provider.md) and the [timeline](../terms/delivery-timeline.md) records [`failover`](../terms/failover-timeline-entry.md). [proof: S28 AS-44]
- **S28.B-10** Given three [unread items](../terms/unread-count.md), when the user marks one read and then all, then the count goes 2 then 0 and a later arrival counts 1. [proof: S28 AS-58, S28 AS-60]

## Owns
- Tables: `NotificationPreference`, `NotificationSettings`, `NotificationSuppression`, `PushDevice`.
- Wide-column store: the inbox, the inbox read watermark, the delivery timeline and the provider-message lookup.
- Redis: done markers, version guard, caps, unread counters, claims, sent markers, one-time codes and the recipient view.
- Queues: the six delivery lines with their dead-letter queues, `notifications-callbacks`, and the consumed `tenancy-invite-requested`.
- Jobs: `notifications.wake-delivery`, `notifications.reconcile-unread`.
- Events: none produced; the consumed events are listed in the service contract.
- Configuration: `NOTIFICATION_SECRET`, `FRONT_BASE_URL`, `SES_*`, `SMTP_*`, `TWILIO_*`, `FCM_*`, `SNS_TOPIC_ARN`, `NOTIFICATIONS_TX_CONCURRENCY`, `NOTIFICATIONS_MARKETING_CONCURRENCY`, `NOTIFICATIONS_SEND_BUDGET_*`.

## Decisions
- Notifications are requested only by publishing events; the router, projector and core module are not exported.
- The done marker only short-circuits; exactly-once comes from conditional inserts keyed by the deterministic delivery id.
- The unread count is derived data: it can be lost and rebuilt from the inbox, and a dirty set repairs it after a crash.
- Mark-all keeps a watermark on arrival time, so later arrivals stay unread whatever their event time.
- The reset mail has no event consumer: it is handed to the mail port after commit so the raw token sits in no table.
- The reset link carries the token in the URL fragment, so it reaches no server log.
