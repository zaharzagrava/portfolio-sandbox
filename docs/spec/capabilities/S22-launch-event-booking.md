# S22 - Launch-event booking (waiting room, admission tokens, seat holds, booking confirm, seat map, ticket limits)

## Purpose
A brand sells a limited number of seats for an event that opens at a fixed time, and thousands of buyers arrive at once. A virtual waiting room orders them fairly (early arrivals get no edge by refreshing) and lets them in at a fixed rate; an admitted buyer receives a ten-minute signed token that opens the hold step. A buyer holds seats for ten minutes (exactly one buyer wins each seat, multi-seat holds are all-or-nothing), then confirms the hold into a booking exactly once. A cheap, ordered seat map shows what is taken; it is a display, never the authority. Staff create, watch and close events. The booking half of the `launch-events` domain only; the live stream is S23.

## Entry points

[code: packages/backend/libs/domains/launch-events/api/holds.controller.ts#HoldsController]
- Public reads: `GET /launch-events/:eventId` (cached 60 s) and `GET /launch-events/:eventId/seatmap` (cached 1 s). [code: packages/backend/libs/domains/launch-events/api/buyer.controller.ts#BuyerController]
- Staff routes: `POST /shops/:shopId/launch-events` and `.../:eventId/close` (`products.write`), `GET .../:eventId/bookings` (`orders.manage`). [code: packages/backend/libs/domains/launch-events/api/organizer.controller.ts#OrganizerController]
- Realtime topics: `queue:<ticket>` (owner only, event `admitted`) and `event:<eventId>:seatmap` (public, event `seats`). [code: packages/backend/libs/domains/launch-events/api/realtime-topics.ts#LaunchEventTopics]
- Published events (topic `launch_events.events`, types `launchevents.event_created`, `.booking_confirmed`, `.event_sold_out`, `.event_closed`) and the command `orders.booking_voucher_requested` on the queue `orders-booking-voucher-requested`.
- Jobs: `launch-events.expire-hold`, `launch-events.open-sales`, `launch-events.close-event`, `launch-events.reconcile-seats` (every minute); the one-second admitter runs in the worker. [code: packages/backend/libs/domains/launch-events/infra/jobs/launch-event.jobs.ts#LaunchEventJobs]

## Rules
- **S22.R-01** A buyer joins the waiting room of a `SCHEDULED` or `ON_SALE` event once: a first join answers `201` with the ticket, position, queue length, wait estimate and a 120-second heartbeat, a repeat join (also twenty at once) answers `200` with the same ticket, and a join touches only the queue store. [proof: S22 AS-01, S22 AS-02] [code: packages/backend/libs/domains/launch-events/application/waiting-room.service.ts#WaitingRoomService]
- **S22.R-02** The queue order is pure: a join before sales open scores uniformly inside the first second after opening whatever the instant, a later join scores the later of "now" and one second after opening, so every early joiner is ahead and later joiners follow join order. [proof: S22 AS-03] [code: packages/backend/libs/domains/launch-events/domain/queue-score.ts#joinScore]
- **S22.R-03** The room admits at most the event's rate per second, in queue order, through a one-second bucket with no catch-up burst after downtime, and only the holder of the event's lease acts; a paused admitter that resumes with an older fence admits nothing. [proof: S22 AS-04, S22 AS-08] [code: packages/backend/libs/domains/launch-events/infra/admitter.ts#Admitter]
- **S22.R-04** An admitted buyer gets an ES256 token (issuer `marketplace`, audience `admission:<eventId>`, subject the buyer, ticket as `jti`, purpose `admission`, ten minutes) from the status call and one push on `queue:<ticket>`; after it ends the buyer joins again at the back. [proof: S22 AS-05] [code: packages/backend/libs/domains/launch-events/infra/admission-token.adapter.ts#PurposeAdmissionTokens]
- **S22.R-05** A ticket's position is its exact rank, never worsens once sales are open, and the estimate is the position over the rate rounded up; a ticket not polled for 120 seconds is removed without using admission capacity. [proof: S22 AS-06, S22 AS-07] [code: packages/backend/libs/domains/launch-events/infra/redis/lua.ts#VIEW]
- **S22.R-06** A ticket is visible and subscribable only to its owner; any other caller, an unknown ticket or a ticket of another event gets the same `404 TICKET_NOT_FOUND` (or refused topic). [proof: S22 AS-10, S22 AS-50] [code: packages/backend/libs/domains/launch-events/api/realtime-topics.ts#LaunchEventTopics]
- **S22.R-07** A full room sheds new joins with `503 WAITING_ROOM_FULL` and `Retry-After: 5` and still serves ticket holders; a down queue store answers `503 WAITING_ROOM_UNAVAILABLE`; neither touches another store, and the problem body carries no store text. [proof: S22 AS-11] [code: packages/backend/libs/domains/launch-events/application/waiting-room.service.ts#WaitingRoomService]
- **S22.R-08** Ten new tickets per buyer per minute are allowed (a repeat join of an existing ticket does not count); the eleventh answers `429` with `Retry-After` and leaves no ticket; a failing limiter lets joins through and is counted. [proof: S22 AS-12] [code: packages/backend/libs/domains/launch-events/application/launch-limits.ts#LaunchLimits]
- **S22.R-09** An event may require a bot-check result: it is verified once through a provider port with a two-second timeout, a missing, malformed, rejected or reused result is `403 HUMAN_CHECK_FAILED`, and a provider failure fails closed with `503` after one attempt. [proof: S22 AS-13, S22 AS-14] [code: packages/backend/libs/domains/launch-events/infra/human-verifier.adapter.ts#HttpHumanVerifier]
- **S22.R-10** The hold route checks the admission token (signature, algorithm, issuer, audience, subject, purpose, expiry) before the idempotency step, the pipes or any store; every failure is `403 ADMISSION_REQUIRED`, and a valid token serves several holds until it ends. [proof: S22 AS-18] [code: packages/backend/libs/domains/launch-events/api/admission.guard.ts#AdmissionGuard]
- **S22.R-11** A hold asks for one to ten distinct seats or a quantity, only on an `ON_SALE` event by its authoritative status; bad input is `400 validation_failed`, an out-of-range seat `422 UNKNOWN_SEAT`, seats on a general-admission event `422 SEATS_NOT_SELECTABLE`, and a refusal changes nothing. [proof: S22 AS-19, S22 AS-20] [code: packages/backend/libs/domains/launch-events/application/hold.service.ts#HoldService]
- **S22.R-12** A hold lasts exactly ten minutes and exactly one buyer wins a seat: the first-filter lock only rejects early, the conditional write in the durable ledger decides, an expired hold counts as free at that instant, and a multi-seat hold is all-or-nothing with a rollback that removes only what its own hold and fence wrote. [proof: S22 AS-15, S22 AS-16, S22 AS-17, S22 AS-27] [code: packages/backend/libs/domains/launch-events/infra/dynamo-hold.ledger.ts#DynamoHoldLedger]
- **S22.R-13** A buyer's held plus booked seats never exceed the event's limit, also under parallel requests and after the counter is wiped (it is rebuilt from bookings and live holds and always expires); a refusal is `422 TICKET_LIMIT_EXCEEDED {limit, remaining}`; a hold ending by release and expiry together credits the buyer once. [proof: S22 AS-21, S22 AS-22, S22 AS-23, S22 AS-24] [code: packages/backend/libs/domains/launch-events/application/hold.service.ts#HoldService]
- **S22.R-14** Hold creation needs an `Idempotency-Key` scoped to the buyer: a replay returns the stored answer with `Idempotent-Replayed: true`, a hold that lost the seat replays its `409`, a reused or invalid key is `422`, a `5xx` frees the key. [proof: S22 AS-25] [code: packages/backend/libs/domains/launch-events/api/holds.controller.ts#HoldsController]
- **S22.R-15** Best-available holds pick adjacent seats of one row when one exists, stay disjoint under any concurrency without waiting on each other, and fail whole with `409 NO_SEATS_AVAILABLE`. [proof: S22 AS-26] [code: packages/backend/libs/domains/launch-events/domain/seat-picker.ts#pickSeats]
- **S22.R-16** Expiry frees the seat records, locks, quota and map bits, is safe late, twice or against a confirm, and never touches a seat another hold took; a down lock store is only a counted degradation, a down ledger is `503 HOLDS_UNAVAILABLE` with nothing left behind, and hold attempts are limited to thirty a minute per buyer (a failing limiter refuses the hold). [proof: S22 AS-28, S22 AS-29, S22 AS-30, S22 AS-31] [code: packages/backend/libs/domains/launch-events/application/hold-lifecycle.service.ts#HoldLifecycleService]
- **S22.R-17** Every booking route needs a session (`401` otherwise); the event page and the seat map are public. [proof: S22 AS-32] [code: packages/backend/libs/domains/launch-events/api/buyer.controller.ts#BuyerController]
- **S22.R-18** Confirm turns a live hold into bookings exactly once: the ledger is sealed (`HELD → CONFIRMING`), then one transaction books the seats, records history, the `booking_confirmed` event and the voucher command without any call to another store, then the ledger and the map follow. [proof: S22 AS-33, S22 AS-35, S22 AS-44] [code: packages/backend/libs/domains/launch-events/application/confirm.service.ts#ConfirmService]
- **S22.R-19** Confirm needs an `Idempotency-Key`; a repeat with the same key replays, a new key on a confirmed hold answers `200` with the same bookings and nothing new is written, and concurrent confirms book once. [proof: S22 AS-34, S22 AS-35] [code: packages/backend/libs/domains/launch-events/api/holds.controller.ts#HoldsController]
- **S22.R-20** Confirm and expiry exclude each other: a hold is either booked or expired, never both, and a confirm at or after the hold's end answers `409 HOLD_EXPIRED` and frees the seats; each illegal move answers its own code. [proof: S22 AS-36, S22 AS-37, S22 AS-56] [code: packages/backend/libs/domains/launch-events/domain/hold-state.ts#assertHoldTransition]
- **S22.R-21** The database is the final guard: at most one confirmed booking exists per event and seat, a conflicting confirm writes nothing and answers `409 SEAT_TAKEN`, and the count of seats with two bookings stays zero. [proof: S22 AS-38] [code: packages/backend/libs/domains/launch-events/infra/sequelize-booking.repository.ts#SequelizeBookingRepository]
- **S22.R-22** A hold or booking of somebody else answers `404 HOLD_NOT_FOUND`, byte for byte as an unknown hold; a confirm needs the session only, and its path and body are validated. [proof: S22 AS-39, S22 AS-40] [code: packages/backend/libs/domains/launch-events/application/confirm.service.ts#ConfirmService]
- **S22.R-23** A fault before the booking is written answers a generic `5xx` and the retry with the same key books once; the reconciler finishes a confirmation a crash cut short and releases a confirmation without a booking after 120 seconds; a refused outbox append rolls the booking back and leaves the hold confirmable. [proof: S22 AS-41, S22 AS-42] [code: packages/backend/libs/domains/launch-events/application/reconcile.service.ts#ReconcileService]
- **S22.R-24** The last booked seat moves the event `ON_SALE → SOLD_OUT` once, with one history row and one event, and refuses new holds and joins. [proof: S22 AS-43] [code: packages/backend/libs/domains/launch-events/application/confirm.service.ts#ConfirmService]
- **S22.R-25** The seat map is one bit per seat (seat `i` is bit `7 - i mod 8` of byte `i div 8`) with a `seq` assigned in the same step as the bit; changes are published as ordered deltas, the client rule is "apply `last + 1`, ignore older, refetch on a gap", a lost map is rebuilt once behind a single flight and always expires, and the reconciler clears stray bits. [proof: S22 AS-45, S22 AS-46, S22 AS-47, S22 AS-48] [code: packages/backend/libs/domains/launch-events/application/seat-map.service.ts#SeatMapService]
- **S22.R-26** Public reads are limited per address (600 a minute, fail open); the event page is cacheable, holds public fields only and is negatively cached for unknown events. [proof: S22 AS-49] [code: packages/backend/libs/domains/launch-events/application/launch-event.service.ts#LaunchEventService]
- **S22.R-27** A shop member with `products.write` creates an event (validated shape, schedule rules `invalid_schedule`, a DTO response, no foreign key to the shop, a creation event, an open-sales job and a close job); other callers are refused (`401`, `403`, `404`, `403 shop_suspended`). ## Behaviors
- **S22.B-01** Given an event on sale at rate 10 and 100 waiting buyers, when the admitter runs for one second, then the first ten in line are admitted and an eleventh is not. [proof: S22 AS-04]
- **S22.B-02** Given a ticket not polled for two minutes and a second ticket behind it, when the admitter runs, then the second is admitted in its place and the first gets `404` when it returns. [proof: S22 AS-07]
- **S22.B-03** Given 100 admitted buyers, when all hold seat 42 at the same time, then exactly one gets `201` and 99 get `409 SEAT_TAKEN`. [proof: S22 AS-16]
- **S22.B-04** Given a limit of two, when a buyer fires five single-seat holds at once, then two succeed and three are `422 TICKET_LIMIT_EXCEEDED`. [proof: S22 AS-22]
- **S22.B-05** Given a hold, when its owner confirms it ten times at once with different keys, then one set of bookings, one event and one voucher command exist and every answer carries the same booking ids. [proof: S22 AS-35]
- **S22.B-06** Given a hold that has lapsed and a seat a second buyer now holds, when the first buyer confirms late, then the answer is `409 HOLD_EXPIRED` and the second buyer's hold is untouched. [proof: S22 AS-27]
- **S22.B-07** Given the ledger lost a seat record so that two holds own one seat, when both confirm at once, then one booking exists and the other confirm is `409 SEAT_TAKEN`. [proof: S22 AS-38]
- **S22.B-08** Given the seat map key is lost, when twenty readers ask, then the map is rebuilt once and every reader gets the same correct picture. [proof: S22 AS-48]
- **S22.B-09** Given an organizer closes the event, when a buyer tries to confirm an open hold, then the answer is `409 EVENT_CLOSED` and within a minute the holds are expired and their seats free. [proof: S22 AS-54]

## Decisions
- The ledger, not Redis, decides who holds a seat; Redis is a first filter, a cache and a display (a lost key is rebuilt from the ledger and the bookings).
- Every state change is `UPDATE … WHERE status = :from` (or the same condition in the ledger), so two racing actors cannot both win; side effects follow only for the winner.
- Confirm seals the ledger before a transaction with no network call and finalises after it; a crash in between is repaired by a one-minute reconciler.
- Event types are emitted as `launchevents.<name>` because the platform's type pattern forbids `_` in the first segment; the topic and aggregate type keep `launch_events`, and the notifications consumer resolves the alias.
- The join limit counts new tickets, so the idempotent repeats of one buyer never use it up; a refused join takes its ticket back.
