# S24 - Product chat

## Purpose
Every product can have **one chat**, opened by the shop that owns it. Buyers join, members talk, and only members see what is said; a stranger learns nothing beyond the channel's public title. A message is sent **exactly once** (a client-generated ID makes a retry harmless) and gets a **per-channel sequence number** that is gap-free and ordered whoever wrote it, this API or the realtime gateway. The push is best effort and **sync on reconnect is the guarantee**: a client sends the highest sequence it holds per channel and receives what it missed. Members have unread counts and read receipts, an ephemeral online mark, and, when they are offline and have not read a message after 30 seconds, one push per chat from the notifications capability. Shop staff and moderators ban, mute, promote and delete by a strictly-higher-rank rule, and a deleted shop takes its chats with it.

## Entry points
- `POST /chat/channels` opens a chat for a product, `GET /chat/channels/by-product/:productId` discovers it, `GET`/`PATCH /chat/channels/:channelId` read and rename or archive it, `POST /chat/channels/:channelId/join` joins. [code: packages/backend/libs/domains/chat/api/chat-channel.controller.ts#ChatChannelController]
- `POST`/`GET /chat/channels/:channelId/messages` send (`201` new, `200` replay) and page the history newest first. [code: packages/backend/libs/domains/chat/api/chat-message.controller.ts#ChatMessageController]
- `POST /chat/sync` returns what a client missed in up to 50 channels. [code: packages/backend/libs/domains/chat/api/chat-sync.controller.ts#ChatSyncController]
- `GET /chat/channels/mine` is the inbox and `GET /chat/channels/:channelId/receipts` the other members' read positions. [code: packages/backend/libs/domains/chat/api/chat-channel.controller.ts#ChatChannelController]
- `GET /chat/unread` and `POST /chat/channels/:channelId/read` are the unread list and the read position. [code: packages/backend/libs/domains/chat/api/chat-read-state.controller.ts#ChatReadStateController]
- `POST /chat/presence/heartbeat` and `GET /chat/presence?userIds=` are presence. [code: packages/backend/libs/domains/chat/api/chat-presence.controller.ts#ChatPresenceController]
- `POST /chat/channels/:channelId/members/ban`, `POST /chat/channels/:channelId/members/unban`, `POST /chat/channels/:channelId/members/mute`, `POST /chat/channels/:channelId/members/unmute`, `POST /chat/channels/:channelId/members/promote`, `POST /chat/channels/:channelId/members/demote`, `DELETE /chat/channels/:channelId/messages/:messageId` and `POST /chat/ws-ticket`. [code: packages/backend/libs/domains/chat/api/chat-moderation.controller.ts#ChatModerationController]
- Jobs `chat.partition-maintenance` (daily) and `chat.backfill-shops` (every 10 minutes until no channel lacks a shop). [code: packages/backend/libs/domains/chat/infra/chat.jobs.ts#ChatJobs]
- Consumer of `chat.events` (schedules the delayed offline checks), of the queue `chat-offline-notify` (decides and escalates) and of `tenancy.events` (`tenancy.shop_deleted`). [code: packages/backend/libs/domains/chat/infra/chat-offline.scheduler.ts#ChatOfflineScheduler]
- Events `chat.message_posted` (written by the insert trigger, deterministic event id) and `chat.message_escalated`, both v1 on `chat.events`. [code: packages/backend/libs/domains/chat/application/events/chat-events.ts#ChatMessageEscalated]
- Realtime hub topic `chat:<channelId>` carries `read`, `presence`, `message`, `message_deleted` and `channel_archived` (live only); the gateway buses `chat:channel:<id>` and `chat:moderation` carry messages and moderation. [code: packages/backend/libs/domains/chat/api/realtime-topics.ts#ChatTopics]

## Rules
- **S24.R-01** Opening a chat needs a live member of the product's shop with `products.write` (the answer is never cached), reads the product through the catalog's batch service, stores the shop and makes the caller the `OWNER` member; a second chat for the product is `409 channel_exists` (two at once give one `201`), an archived product `409 product_archived`, a stranger `403 permission_denied`, an unknown product `404 product_not_found`. [proof: S24 AS-01, S24 AS-02, S24 AS-03, S24 AS-04] [code: packages/backend/libs/domains/chat/application/chat-channel.service.ts#ChatChannelService]
- **S24.R-02** Joining is one conditional insert that never touches the role, status or read position of an existing member: a joiner starts at the channel's current sequence, ten joins at once leave one row, a banned member is `403 banned`, an archived channel `409 channel_archived`. [proof: S24 AS-05, S24 AS-06] [code: packages/backend/libs/domains/chat/infra/membership.repository.ts#SequelizeMembershipRepository]
- **S24.R-03** A channel is read by id only by an active member (a stranger, a banned member and a missing channel are the same `404 channel_not_found`); only the discovery route by product is open to any signed-in user, with public fields and `myRole: null`. [proof: S24 AS-07] [code: packages/backend/libs/domains/chat/infra/channel.repository.ts#SequelizeChannelRepository]
- **S24.R-04** A rename or archive needs manager authority; the state change is a conditional update, so a repeat or a lost race is `409 invalid_transition`, the archive publishes `channel_archived` after the commit, and an active member without authority is `403 permission_denied`. [proof: S24 AS-08] [code: packages/backend/libs/domains/chat/application/chat-channel.service.ts#ChatChannelService]
- **S24.R-05** A send is decided by one statement (active member, not muted, channel open, reply target in the channel) and is idempotent per `(channel, author, clientMessageId)`: an identical replay is `200 duplicate: true` with no second row, event or push, the same key with another body or reply target is `422 idempotency_key_reuse`, another author's key is their own message, and ten identical sends at once store one message. [proof: S24 AS-09, S24 AS-10, S24 AS-11, S24 AS-12, S24 AS-13] [code: packages/backend/libs/domains/chat/infra/message.repository.ts#SequelizeMessageRepository]
- **S24.R-06** The sequence number is assigned by the database for every writer (the API and the gateway's plain insert), gap-free and ordered per channel, and a rejected write consumes none; the author's read position moves to their own message. [proof: S24 AS-14, S24 AS-15, S24 AS-31, S24 AS-58] [code: packages/backend/migrations/20261014140000-chat-s24-expand.js]
- **S24.R-07** A body is trimmed and 1 to 4,000 code points, `clientMessageId` is a UUID, an unknown field is refused, and every violation is `400 validation_failed` with nothing stored; a reply to a message of another channel is `422 reply_target_invalid`, to a deleted message of this channel is accepted. [proof: S24 AS-15, S24 AS-16] [code: packages/backend/libs/domains/chat/domain/message-body.ts#normalizeBody]
- **S24.R-08** A send is refused for a stranger (`404`), a banned member (`403 banned`), a muted member (`403 muted` with `mutedUntil`) and an archived channel (`409 channel_archived`). [proof: S24 AS-17] [code: packages/backend/libs/domains/chat/application/chat-message.service.ts#ChatMessageService]
- **S24.R-09** Sends are limited to 10 per 10 seconds per user and fail open (a limiter outage is counted by policy prefix); the live push follows the commit, is best effort and a failure is counted and never fails the send. [proof: S24 AS-18, S24 AS-19] [code: packages/backend/libs/domains/chat/application/chat-publisher.ts#ChatPublisher]
- **S24.R-10** Every stored message has exactly one `chat.message_posted` v1 event with aggregate id the channel and version the sequence, an event id derived from the message so a repeated relay repeats it, and a preview of at most 120 characters. [proof: S24 AS-20] [code: packages/backend/libs/domains/chat/application/events/chat-events.ts#ChatMessagePosted]
- **S24.R-11** Sync takes at most 50 cursors (`400 too_many_channels` above, `400 validation_failed` for any invalid entry, never a silent drop), returns per channel the messages after the cursor in pages of 200 with `hasMore`, omits channels the caller cannot read without a trace, and runs three statements for any number of channels cold. [proof: S24 AS-21, S24 AS-22, S24 AS-23, S24 AS-24, S24 AS-25] [code: packages/backend/libs/domains/chat/application/chat-sync.service.ts#ChatSyncService]
- **S24.R-12** A deleted message is a tombstone that keeps its sequence and is never served after the delete committed: bodies are read from the rows, and the tail cache is keyed by the last sequence and the channel's delete epoch, which changes in the delete's transaction. [proof: S24 AS-26, S24 AS-53] [code: packages/backend/libs/domains/chat/infra/recent-window.cache.ts#RecentWindowCache]
- **S24.R-13** History is newest first by sequence in keyset pages (`limit` 1 to 100, default 50, an opaque cursor, tombstones included), only for an active member (`404` for a stranger, `403 banned`). [proof: S24 AS-27] [code: packages/backend/libs/domains/chat/application/chat-message.service.ts#ChatMessageService]
- **S24.R-14** `ChatMessage` is partitioned by month on `createdAt` with a chat-owned index that guards uniqueness across months; a job creates the current month and the next three (and moves rows parked for a missing month), is safe to run twice at once, and a message is never refused for want of a partition (it lands in the default partition and is counted). [proof: S24 AS-28, S24 AS-29] [code: packages/backend/libs/domains/chat/application/chat-partition.service.ts#ChatPartitionService]
- **S24.R-15** Unread for a member is the last sequence minus their read position, never negative and never counting their own messages; the list is a keyset page by most recent activity, without archived channels and channels the caller is banned from (`limit` 1 to 200, default 50). - **S24.R-16** Marking read is monotonic, capped at the channel's last sequence and safe under concurrency (`LEAST(GREATEST(position, seq), last)` in one conditional update), and invalid input is `400`, a stranger or a banned member `404`. [proof: S24 AS-32, S24 AS-33, S24 AS-34] [code: packages/backend/libs/domains/chat/infra/read-state.repository.ts#SequelizeReadStateRepository]
- **S24.R-17** An advance of the read position, and only an advance, publishes `read {userId, seq}` after the commit on the hub topic, which only active members may follow; a hub that is down never fails the call, and a channel with more than 50 active members publishes no read or presence event. [proof: S24 AS-35, S24 AS-36] [code: packages/backend/libs/domains/chat/infra/realtime-hub.adapter.ts#RealtimeHubAdapter]
- **S24.R-18** A heartbeat marks the user online for 60 seconds in the ephemeral store only; the first one announces `presence` to the user's 20 most recently active small channels exactly once; a query is truthful only for users who share an active channel with the caller; a store that is down answers `204` to a heartbeat and `503 presence_unavailable` to a query; a heartbeat is limited to 6 a minute. - **S24.R-26** `tenancy.shop_deleted` removes the shop's channels, members, messages and index rows in batches of at most 500 rows, idempotently and safely with two consumers at once; a shop with no chats is counted, and an invalid payload is dead-lettered with nothing deleted. - **S24.R-27** Channels created before shops existed receive their shop in batches of at most 200 (from the product, or the shop provisioned for the seller), a channel whose product is gone is skipped and counted, a run can be repeated, and once none is left the shop is required by the store. [proof: S24 AS-57] [code: packages/backend/libs/domains/chat/application/chat-shop-backfill.service.ts#ChatShopBackfillService]
- **S24.R-28** The domain reads and writes no table of another owner, holds no foreign key to one, exports only its modules, rate-limit policies and event definitions, opens every transaction through the transaction runner, and keeps the gateway's three statements working against its schema. [proof: W05 AS-07] [code: packages/backend/libs/domains/chat/application/chat-channel.service.ts#ChatChannelService]

[proof: W05 AS-23, W05 AS-27] [code: packages/backend/libs/domains/chat/application/chat-publisher.ts#ChatPublisher]

## Behaviors
- **S24.B-01** Given a channel at sequence 4, when a member sends "Is it unlocked?", then `201` with sequence 5 and `duplicate: false`, one row, one `chat.message_posted` event and one live push; sending it again with the same client ID is `200` with the same message. [proof: S24 AS-09, S24 AS-10]
- **S24.B-02** Given messages 1 to 5, when a member syncs with cursor 3, then they get messages 4 and 5 with `lastSeq` 5 and `hasMore: false`, and with a moderator's delete in between the deleted message is already a tombstone. [proof: S24 AS-21, S24 AS-26]
- **S24.B-03** Given a channel with 5 messages by the seller, when the buyer reads up to 3, then unread is 2; reading up to 999 gives 0; the seller's own unread was 0 all along. [proof: S24 AS-30, S24 AS-32]
- **S24.B-04** Given a recipient who is offline and has not read, when 30 seconds pass, then exactly one `chat.message_escalated` event exists, and a burst of 20 messages gives one event and 19 `coalesced`. [proof: S24 AS-43, S24 AS-44]
- **S24.B-05** Given a moderator, when they try to ban another moderator or the shop's staff, then `403 permission_denied` and nothing changes; when staff ban a member, then `204` and the member can no longer send, join or read. [proof: S24 AS-49, S24 AS-50]
- **S24.B-06** Given a shop with two chats, when the shop is deleted, then both chats, their members and their messages are gone, another shop's chats are untouched, and a second delivery changes nothing. [proof: S24 AS-56]
