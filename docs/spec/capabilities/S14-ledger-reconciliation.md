# S14 - Double-entry ledger, balanced journals, hot-account sharding, balance read model, daily PSP reconciliation

## Purpose
Every movement of money on the platform is one balanced, immutable journal, posted once. Sales, refunds and settlements post themselves; the accounts every sale touches are split into shards so they do not serialize the platform; balances are running totals, never a sum over history; and every closed UTC day is proven against the payment provider's statement, with differences handed to finance operators as issues. See {{concept:transaction-runner}}, {{concept:outbox-task-queue}} and {{concept:projection-and-replay}}.

## Entry points
- `LedgerService.postJournal`, `recordPaymentCaptured`, `recordPaymentRefunded`, `getBalances`, `listSellerBalances`: the in-process contract used by payments, payouts and ad billing; every posting joins the caller's transaction. [code: packages/backend/libs/domains/payments/application/ledger.service.ts#LedgerService]
- Topic `orders.events` (`order.paid`): the settlement consumer, group `ledger-settlement`. [code: packages/backend/libs/domains/payments/infra/settlement.consumer.ts#SettlementConsumer]
- Topic `ledger.events` (`ledger.journal_posted` v2): the balance read-model projector, group `ledger-balances`. [code: packages/backend/libs/domains/payments/infra/balance.projector.ts#BalanceProjector]
- `GET /shops/:shopId/balance`: a member with `payouts.read` reads the shop's available balance. [code: packages/backend/libs/domains/payments/api/balance.controller.ts#BalanceController]
- `GET /finance/reconciliation/runs`, `GET /finance/reconciliation/issues`, `POST /finance/reconciliation/issues/:issueId/resolve`: the finance operator API (platform `admin` only). [code: packages/backend/libs/domains/payments/api/reconciliation.controller.ts#ReconciliationController]
- Jobs: `payments.reconcile-daily`, `ledger.sweep-hot-accounts`, `ledger.verify-invariants`, `ledger.ensure-partitions`, `ledger.backfill-balances`, `ledger.settle-order` ({{concept:job-scheduler-lease}}). [code: packages/backend/libs/domains/payments/infra/ledger.jobs.ts#LedgerJobs]

## Rules
- **S14.R-01** A [journal](../terms/ledger-journal.md) MUST have 2 to 200 non-zero [whole-minor-unit](../terms/minor-units.md) lines in one currency whose amounts sum to exactly zero; any other journal is refused with a named reason and nothing is written. [proof: S14 AS-02, S14 AS-04] [code: packages/backend/libs/domains/payments/domain/journal.ts#validateJournal]
- **S14.R-02** [The store](../terms/ledger-store.md) MUST [refuse at commit ↗](../sw-external-terms.md#database-constraint) any journal whose lines do not sum to zero, whoever wrote it. [proof: S14 AS-03]
- **S14.R-03** [Entries](../terms/ledger-entry-table.md) and journal headers MUST be [append-only ↗](../sw-external-terms.md#append-only): the store rejects `UPDATE` and `DELETE` on every [partition ↗](../sw-external-terms.md#table-partitioning) and on the header table; a correction is a new [reversing journal](../terms/reversing-journal.md). [proof: S14 AS-06]
- **S14.R-04** Money MUST be integer minor units within the [safe-integer range ↗](../sw-external-terms.md#safe-integer-range), with a currency on every journal; a [running balance](../terms/running-balance.md) outside that range is refused ([`balance_overflow`](../terms/balance-overflow.md)). [proof: S14 AS-05] [code: packages/backend/libs/domains/payments/domain/lock-order.ts#aggregateRows]
- **S14.R-05** A journal is unique per [(kind, reference)](../terms/journal-kind-reference.md), enforced by the store; its identifier is [deterministic ↗](../sw-external-terms.md#deterministic-build) from them; an identical second posting returns the existing journal with [`created: false`](../terms/enqueue-created-flag.md), a different one raises [`JournalConflict`](../terms/journal-conflict.md). [proof: S14 AS-07, S14 AS-08] [code: packages/backend/libs/domains/payments/domain/journal.ts#journalId]
- **S14.R-06** A [posting](../terms/ledger-posting.md) MUST join the caller's [transaction](../terms/transaction-runner.md), never open or commit one, never do network I/O, and write entries, running balances and the [outbox event](../terms/event-row.md) [atomically ↗](../sw-external-terms.md#atomicity) ({{concept:[transaction](../terms/transaction-runner.md)-runner}}). [proof: S14 AS-09] [code: packages/backend/libs/domains/payments/application/journal-posting.service.ts#JournalPostingService]
- **S14.R-07** All lines of a journal MUST carry one [`postedAt`](../terms/posted-at.md) taken from the [injected clock](../terms/injected-clock.md). [proof: S14 AS-01]
- **S14.R-08** Callers of [`postJournal`](../terms/post-journal.md) may post only [`PAYOUT`](../terms/journal-kind.md), `PAYOUT_REVERSAL`, `AD_CHARGE` and `ADJUSTMENT`; `SALE`, `REFUND`, `SETTLEMENT` and `SWEEP` are posted only by [the ledger](../terms/ledger.md). [proof: S14 AS-04] [code: packages/backend/libs/domains/payments/domain/journal.ts#CALLER_KINDS]
- **S14.R-09** A `PAYOUT` journal that debits a [seller account](../terms/ledger-account.md), and any line on [`PAYOUT_CLEARING`](../terms/ledger-account.md), MUST NOT take the balance below zero, decided atomically with the balance update ([`insufficient_balance`](../terms/insufficient-balance.md)); other kinds may take a seller account negative. [proof: S14 AS-10] [code: packages/backend/libs/domains/payments/domain/account.ts#isGuarded]
- **S14.R-10** The accounts are [`PROVIDER_CLEARING`](../terms/ledger-account.md), `MARKETPLACE_CLEARING` ([short name `CLEARING`](../terms/ledger-account.md)), `PLATFORM_FEES`, `PAYOUT_CLEARING`, `PAYOUTS_SENT` and `SHOP_<uuid>`; any other account id, and any [shard suffix ↗](../sw-external-terms.md#shard), is refused ([`account_unknown`](../terms/account-unknown.md)). [proof: S14 AS-04] [code: packages/backend/libs/domains/payments/domain/account.ts#parseAccountId]
- **S14.R-11** There is one commission rule and it is [S16's](S16-seller-statements.md): the platform's commission is booked at [settlement](../terms/order-settlement.md), per order line, from the rate in force on the order's `paidAt` (`CommissionRateQueryService.getRatesAsOf`), half-up in whole [minor units](../terms/minor-units.md); a [capture](../terms/payment-capture.md) books the full amount to clearing and no fee, so a shop's ledger credit equals the net its statement reports. [proof: S14 AS-11, S14 AS-13] [code: packages/backend/libs/domains/payments/domain/settlement-split.ts#splitSettlement]
- **S14.R-12** The buyer is never a [ledger account](../terms/ledger-account.md); the [payer id](../terms/payer-id.md) is a plain reference on the [journal](../terms/ledger-journal.md). [proof: S14 AS-11]
- **S14.R-13** [`recordPaymentCaptured`](../terms/record-payment-captured.md) posts one [`SALE`](../terms/ledger-journal-kinds.md) journal per payment: [`PROVIDER_CLEARING -A`](../terms/ledger-system-accounts.md), `MARKETPLACE_CLEARING +A` (no fee line), and is [idempotent ↗](../sw-external-terms.md#idempotency) per payment. [proof: S14 AS-11, S14 AS-12, S14 AS-13] [code: packages/backend/libs/domains/payments/application/payment-postings.service.ts#PaymentPostingsService]
- **S14.R-14** [`recordPaymentRefunded`](../terms/record-payment-refunded.md) posts one `REFUND` journal per payment that exactly reverses the sale and, when the order was [settled](../terms/order-settlement.md), the settlement (the [shops' shares](../terms/shop-share.md) and the commission); it refuses a missing sale, another amount or currency, and is idempotent per payment. [proof: S14 AS-14, S14 AS-15, S14 AS-16]
- **S14.R-15** The [settlement consumer](../terms/settlement-consumer.md) posts one `SETTLEMENT` journal per [`order.paid`](../terms/order-paid-event.md): it debits the sale's clearing credit, credits each shop the totals of its lines less their commission (line totals from the event, rate per line as of `paidAt`) and `PLATFORM_FEES` the commission; a payload whose lines do not add up to the captured amount, or whose shop subtotals disagree with its lines, is dead-lettered as `invalid_payload`. [proof: S14 AS-17] [code: packages/backend/libs/domains/payments/domain/settlement-split.ts#splitSettlement]
- **S14.R-16** The consumer validates every payload, tolerates duplicates and [out-of-order delivery ↗](../sw-external-terms.md#out-of-order-delivery), [dead-letters](../terms/dead-letter-topic.md) invalid payloads without effect, and [defers up to 6 attempts](../terms/consumer-attempt-budget.md) in about 10 minutes when the sale is not there yet. [proof: S14 AS-18, S14 AS-19, S14 AS-20] [code: packages/backend/libs/domains/payments/domain/settlement-retry.ts#nextSettlementStep]
- **S14.R-17** Settlement and refund of one payment are [serialized ↗](../sw-external-terms.md#mutual-exclusion): a refunded payment is never settled, a settled-then-refunded payment is fully [clawed back](../terms/clawback.md). [proof: S14 AS-21] [code: packages/backend/libs/domains/payments/application/settlement.service.ts#SettlementService]
- **S14.R-18** `PLATFORM_FEES`, `PROVIDER_CLEARING` and `MARKETPLACE_CLEARING` are split into `N` [shards](../terms/ledger-balance-shard.md) (default 32, setting 1 to 256) by a [stable hash ↗](../sw-external-terms.md#stable-hash) of the journal's [shard key](../terms/shard-key.md); reads present the [logical account](../terms/logical-account.md) as the sum of its shards and never show a shard. [proof: S14 AS-22, S14 AS-23, S14 AS-27] [code: packages/backend/libs/domains/payments/domain/shard.ts#shardOf]
- **S14.R-19** An hourly [sweep](../terms/shard-sweep.md) consolidates non-empty shards into shard 0 with one [balanced](../terms/balanced-journal.md), [deterministic ↗](../sw-external-terms.md#deterministic-build) `SWEEP` journal per account per window. [proof: S14 AS-24] [code: packages/backend/libs/domains/payments/application/hot-account-sweep.service.ts#HotAccountSweepService]
- **S14.R-20** Every posting [locks ↗](../sw-external-terms.md#row-lock) the [balance rows](../terms/balance-row.md) it touches in ascending (account, shard) order, whatever the line order. [proof: S14 AS-25] [code: packages/backend/libs/domains/payments/domain/lock-order.ts#compareRows]
- **S14.R-21** A [posting](../terms/ledger-posting.md) bounds its waits ([lock 2 s](../terms/transaction-timeouts.md), statement 5 s) and fails with the [retryable](../terms/retryable.md) [`ledger_busy`](../terms/ledger-busy.md). [proof: S14 AS-26, S14 AS-69]
- **S14.R-22** The ledger keeps a [running balance](../terms/running-balance.md) and a [version](../terms/balance-version.md) per (account, [shard ↗](../sw-external-terms.md#shard), currency), updated by [atomic increments ↗](../sw-external-terms.md#atomic-increment) in the posting's transaction; no balance is computed by summing [entries](../terms/ledger-entry-table.md). [proof: S14 AS-28] [code: packages/backend/libs/domains/payments/infra/balance.store.ts#SequelizeBalanceStore]
- **S14.R-23** [`getBalances`](../terms/get-balances.md) returns [authoritative balances](../terms/authoritative-balance.md) for up to 500 accounts honouring the caller's transaction; [`listSellerBalances`](../terms/list-seller-balances.md) pages seller accounts by [keyset ↗](../sw-external-terms.md#keyset-pagination) with a [signed cursor](../terms/pagination-cursor.md). [proof: S14 AS-28, S14 AS-29]
- **S14.R-24** Each [journal](../terms/ledger-journal.md) emits one [`ledger.journal_posted`](../terms/ledger-journal-posted-event.md) v2 event through the [outbox](../terms/inbox-table.md), with each line's account, shard, amount, resulting balance and [balance version](../terms/balance-version.md). [proof: S14 AS-01] [code: packages/backend/libs/domains/payments/application/events/ledger-events.ts#JournalPosted]
- **S14.R-25** The [read model](../terms/read-model.md) applies an event line only when its balance version is newer than the stored one; it is [idempotent ↗](../sw-external-terms.md#idempotency), [order-tolerant](../terms/order-tolerant.md) and [rebuildable](../terms/rebuildable-read-model.md), and [dead-letters](../terms/dead-letter-topic.md) invalid events without effect. [proof: S14 AS-30, S14 AS-31] [code: packages/backend/libs/domains/payments/domain/balance-version.ts#decideVersion]
- **S14.R-26** The read model has a [hot copy](../terms/hot-copy.md) with an expiry and a [durable copy](../terms/durable-copy.md); a read falls back hot, durable, running balance; staleness is visible through [`asOf`](../terms/as-of.md) and a [lag metric](../terms/ledger-lag-metric.md). [proof: S14 AS-33, S14 AS-37] [code: packages/backend/libs/domains/payments/application/balance-query.service.ts#BalanceQueryService]
- **S14.R-27** `GET /shops/:shopId/balance` answers members with [`payouts.read`](../terms/permission.md) only ([`availableMinor`](../terms/available-minor.md) may be negative), with the same [`404`](../terms/identical-not-found.md) for a [non-member](../terms/shop-membership.md) as for an unknown shop, and is [rate limited per user](../terms/rate-limit.md), [failing open ↗](../sw-external-terms.md#fail-open-fail-closed). [proof: S14 AS-32, S14 AS-34, S14 AS-35, S14 AS-36]
- **S14.R-28** [Reconciliation](../terms/reconciliation.md) reads the provider's [statement](../terms/provider-statement.md) of a closed UTC day through a [domain port](../terms/in-process-port.md) and validates every line before use. [proof: S14 AS-46] [code: packages/backend/libs/domains/payments/domain/reconciliation-matcher.ts#parseStatementLine]
- **S14.R-29** The statement is consumed as a paged, [lazily advanced stream](../terms/lazy-statement-stream.md) with at most one page [buffered](../terms/buffered-data.md) and [job heartbeats](../terms/job-heartbeat.md). [proof: S14 AS-44] [code: packages/backend/libs/domains/payments/application/reconciliation-run.service.ts#ReconciliationRunService]
- **S14.R-30** Statement lines are matched to [`SALE` and `REFUND` journals](../terms/ledger-journal.md) by [provider reference](../terms/provider-idempotency-reference.md), producing issues of kind [`MISSING_IN_LEDGER`](../terms/reconciliation-issue-kind.md), `MISSING_AT_PROVIDER`, `AMOUNT_MISMATCH`, `CURRENCY_MISMATCH`, `DUPLICATE_AT_PROVIDER`, `INVALID_PROVIDER_RECORD`. [proof: S14 AS-38, S14 AS-39, S14 AS-40] [code: packages/backend/libs/domains/payments/domain/reconciliation-matcher.ts#classifyLine]
- **S14.R-31** [Reconciliation](../terms/reconciliation.md) never posts [journals](../terms/ledger-posting.md); it only reports. [proof: S14 AS-40]
- **S14.R-32** A [run](../terms/reconciliation-run.md) is unique per (provider, day); a [`COMPLETED`](../terms/reconciliation-run-status.md) run is final; [concurrent attempts run once](../terms/reconciliation-run-uniqueness.md); a crashed run restarts its day with unique [issues](../terms/reconciliation-issue.md) per (run, kind, [reference](../terms/reconciliation-issue-reference.md)); a failed run stores no issues. [proof: S14 AS-41, S14 AS-42, S14 AS-43, S14 AS-50] [code: packages/backend/libs/domains/payments/domain/run-state.ts#nextRunState]
- **S14.R-33** `day` is a [closed UTC day](../terms/closed-utc-day.md) within 90 days; the schedule runs the previous UTC day and [catches up](../terms/cron-catch-up.md) days without a `COMPLETED` run in the last 7. [proof: S14 AS-47, S14 AS-48] [code: packages/backend/libs/domains/payments/domain/reconciliation-window.ts#validateDay]
- **S14.R-34** A provider failure ends the run [`FAILED(provider_unavailable)`](../terms/reconciliation-run-status.md); [the adapter ↗](../sw-external-terms.md#infrastructure-adapter) does not retry, and [the job layer](../terms/job.md) makes at most 3 attempts. [proof: S14 AS-49] [code: packages/backend/libs/domains/payments/domain/issue-state.ts#resolveIssue]
- **S14.R-36** A reconciled day's [entries](../terms/ledger-entry-table.md) never change; a correction is a new journal in the [current period](../terms/ledger-period.md) that records the day it corrects. [proof: S14 AS-54]
- **S14.R-37** Runs emit [`ledger.reconciliation_completed`](../terms/ledger-reconciliation-completed-event.md) and each new issue [`ledger.reconciliation_issue_opened`](../terms/ledger-reconciliation-issue-opened-event.md) through the [outbox](../terms/outbox-append.md). [proof: S14 AS-68, S14 AS-69] [code: packages/backend/libs/common/config/ledger-config.ts#ledgerConfigKeys]

## Behaviors
- **S14.B-01** Given a balanced three-line journal, when it is posted, then three entries share the journal id and [`postedAt`](../terms/posted-at.md), balances move, and one [`journal_posted` v2](../terms/journal-posted-event.md) is in the [outbox ↗](../sw-external-terms.md#transactional-outbox). [proof: S14 AS-01]
- **S14.B-02** Given an unbalanced journal, then [`JournalUnbalanced`](../terms/journal-unbalanced.md) is raised and no entry, balance or outbox row exists. [proof: S14 AS-02]
- **S14.B-03** Given 50 identical [postings](../terms/ledger-posting.md) at once, then one answers `created: true`, 49 [`created: false`](../terms/enqueue-created-flag.md), and balances move once. [proof: S14 AS-08]
- **S14.B-04** Given two [debits](../terms/ledger-posting.md) of 80 against a balance of 100 at once, then exactly one succeeds and the balance never goes below zero. [proof: S14 AS-10]
- **S14.B-05** Given a [capture](../terms/payment-capture.md) of 2500, then [`PROVIDER_CLEARING -2500`](../terms/ledger-account.md) and `CLEARING +2500` are posted once per payment. [proof: S14 AS-11]
- **S14.B-06** Given a [refund](../terms/refund.md) of an [unsettled sale](../terms/settled-sale.md), then [the three sale accounts](../terms/ledger-account.md) net to zero for the payment; given a settled sale, the shops' shares are reversed. [proof: S14 AS-14, S14 AS-15]
- **S14.B-07** Given [`order.paid`](../terms/order-paid-event.md) for a two-shop order, then one [`SETTLEMENT` journal](../terms/settlement-journal.md) credits each shop its lines less commission and `PLATFORM_FEES` the commission, and the [clearing credit](../terms/ledger-account.md) nets to zero. [proof: S14 AS-17]
- **S14.B-08** Given `order.paid` before the sale exists, then [settlement is deferred](../terms/settlement-journal.md) and runs after the capture, or [dead-letters ↗](../sw-external-terms.md#dead-letter-queue) after 6 attempts. [proof: S14 AS-20]
- **S14.B-09** Given 200 captures at once, then no [deadlock ↗](../sw-external-terms.md#deadlock) or timeout occurs and the [logical balances](../terms/logical-balance.md) of the [hot accounts](../terms/hot-account.md) are exact. [proof: S14 AS-22]
- **S14.B-10** Given a member of a shop, when they read its balance, then they get [`balanceSchema`](../terms/balance-schema.md) with [`source: read_model`](../terms/balance-source.md), or [`ledger`](../terms/balance-source.md) when both stores are down, with the same number. [proof: S14 AS-32, S14 AS-33]
- **S14.B-11** Given a clean [statement day](../terms/statement-day.md), then the run is [`COMPLETED`](../terms/reconciliation-run-status.md) with every line [matched](../terms/statement-line-matching.md) and no issue. [proof: S14 AS-38]
- **S14.B-12** Given a [statement](../terms/statement-day.md) with a missing, an extra, a different amount and a different currency, then [four issues of the named kinds](../terms/reconciliation-issue.md) are opened. [proof: S14 AS-39]
- **S14.B-13** Given a provider outage on page 3, then the run is [`FAILED(provider_unavailable)`](../terms/provider-unavailable-failed-run.md) with no [issues](../terms/issue.md) stored, and [the next attempt](../terms/job-attempt.md) completes the day. [proof: S14 AS-45]
- **S14.B-14** Given an admin who resolves an issue as [`adjusted`](../terms/adjusted-resolution.md), then one [`ADJUSTMENT` journal](../terms/adjustment-journal.md) with [`correctsDay`](../terms/corrects-day.md) is posted with the issue change, [history row](../terms/order-status-history.md) and event [in one transaction ↗](../sw-external-terms.md#database-transaction). [proof: S14 AS-54]
- **S14.B-15** Given a journal in an [uncovered month](../terms/uncovered-month.md), then it posts through the [catch-all partition](../terms/catch-all-partition.md) and [maintenance](../terms/jobs-partition-maintenance.md) moves it without changing a total. [proof: S14 AS-61]

## Owns
- Tables: `LedgerEntry`, `LedgerJournal`, `AccountBalance`, `LedgerCheckpoint`, `LedgerBackfillCursor`, `ReconciliationRun`, `ReconciliationIssue`, `ReconciliationIssueHistory`, `ReconciliationStaging`, `ReconciliationSeen`, `ProviderAccount`, `Payout`.
- Events: `ledger.journal_posted` (v2), `ledger.reconciliation_completed`, `ledger.reconciliation_issue_opened`.
- Read model: DynamoDB `LedgerBalanceView`, Redis `ledger:balance:<accountId>`.
- Configuration: `LEDGER_SHARDS`, `LEDGER_LOCK_TIMEOUT_MS`, `LEDGER_STATEMENT_TIMEOUT_MS`, `RECONCILIATION_PAGE_SIZE`, `RECONCILIATION_CATCHUP_DAYS`, `LEDGER_CURSOR_SECRET`.

## Decisions
- One header row per journal, unique on (kind, reference), decides "once"; the partitioned entries table cannot carry that uniqueness.
- A posting never nests a transaction; errors raised after a first write carry `rollbackRequired` and every caller runs inside the transaction runner.
- The read model stores absolute values with versions, so replays and duplicates are harmless.
- Reconciliation stages its issues and promotes them in one transaction, so a failed run leaves none.
- The entries table has no `TRUNCATE` trap: the test cleanup truncates every table, and production denies the application role `TRUNCATE`.

## Known gaps
- Not proven by an automated test (listed in `specs/UNVERIFIED.md`, status `not run`): the balance page under 100 ms at the 99th percentile with 100 million entries (SC-004), and a balance change visible within 5 seconds at the 99th percentile under production-like load (SC-005).
- The Stripe calls behind the statement port (`StripeService.listBalanceTransactions`, `retrieveBalanceTransactionByReference`) are exercised only through the scriptable double; the mapping of a Stripe balance transaction to a statement line is not run against the real API.
- A refund whose provider refund id never reaches the ledger is booked under `refund:<paymentId>` and will not match the provider's `re_…` line.
