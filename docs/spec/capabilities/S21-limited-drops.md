# S21 - Limited-drop auctions

## Purpose
A seller puts one unit of one product up for **English auction**: a starting price, a minimum increment, an optional hidden reserve and a start and an end. Bidders state the most they will pay (a **maximum**); the price shown rises only as far as needed to stay ahead of the others, like a proxy bidder would. A bid that changes the price in the last two minutes pushes the end out, up to a cap, so nobody wins by sniping. At the end the auction closes **once**, whatever number of triggers fire: sold to the leader at the shown price when the reserve is met, or unsold. The winner gets an order from the order capability; if it is not paid the unit is offered once to the runner-up, and otherwise goes back on the shelf. Members of the selling shop never bid. Every accepted bid is durable the moment it is acknowledged; the fast store only caches, limits and pushes.

## Entry points
- `POST /shops/:shopId/auctions` schedules an auction (`201`, `Location`, `Idempotency-Key`, plan feature `auctions`); `GET /shops/:shopId/auctions` pages the shop's auctions by keyset; `GET /shops/:shopId/auctions/:auctionId` reads one with the reserve; `POST /shops/:shopId/auctions/:auctionId/cancel` cancels it before anyone has bid. [code: packages/backend/libs/domains/auctions/api/auction-seller.controller.ts#AuctionSellerController]
- `POST /auctions/:auctionId/bids` places a bid (`Idempotency-Key` required); `GET /auctions/:auctionId/my-bid` returns the caller's own maximum. [code: packages/backend/libs/domains/auctions/api/auction-bid.controller.ts#AuctionBidController]
- `GET /auctions/:auctionId`, `GET /auctions?productIds=` (the BFF's batch) and `GET /auctions/:auctionId/bids` are anonymous reads. [code: packages/backend/libs/domains/auctions/api/auction-public.controller.ts#AuctionPublicController]
- Jobs: `auctions.hold-stock`, `auctions.open` (at the start), `auctions.close` (one per end), `auctions.request-order`, `auctions.return-stock`, the periodic `auctions.sweep-due` (every 30 s) and the daily `auctions.ensure-bid-partitions`. [code: packages/backend/libs/domains/auctions/infra/auction.jobs.ts#AuctionJobs]
- Consumer of `orders.events`: `order.paid` sells the auction, `order.cancelled` offers the unit to the runner-up or returns it. [code: packages/backend/libs/domains/auctions/infra/auction-settlement.consumer.ts#AuctionSettlementConsumer]
- Events on `auctions.events`, keyed by the auction: `auction.created`, `.opened`, `.bid_placed`, `.leader_changed`, `.closed`, `.second_chance_offered`, `.sold`, `.unsold`, `.cancelled`. [code: packages/backend/libs/domains/auctions/application/events/auction-events.ts#AuctionBidPlaced]
- Realtime topic `auction:<id>` carries `price` and `closed` messages with a rising `version`. [code: packages/backend/libs/domains/auctions/infra/auction-realtime.adapter.ts#AuctionRealtimeAdapter]

## Rules
- **S21.R-01** Proxy resolution: with no leader a maximum at or above the starting price makes the bidder leader at the starting price; against another leader a maximum must reach price plus increment; a higher maximum takes the lead at `min(max, leaderMax + increment)`, an equal one leaves the earlier bidder in front at that maximum, a lower one leaves the leader at `min(leaderMax, max + increment)`. The price never decreases and never exceeds the leader's maximum. [proof: S21 AS-11, S21 AS-12, S21 AS-15, S21 AS-53] [code: packages/backend/libs/domains/auctions/domain/proxy-bid.ts#resolveBid]
- **S21.R-02** The leader raising their own maximum (strictly above the current one) changes neither price nor end (`raised`); an equal or lower maximum is `422 bid_not_increasing`; a maximum below the minimum is `422 bid_too_low` with `minimumMaxAmountMinor`. A rejected bid writes no row, no event and does not change the version. [proof: S21 AS-14, S21 AS-15] [code: packages/backend/libs/domains/auctions/application/bid.service.ts#BidService]
- **S21.R-03** When the leader's maximum reaches the reserve the price is at least the reserve; `reserveMet` is exactly "price is at least the reserve". The reserve amount is shown only to the selling shop's members; everyone else sees `hasReserve` and `reserveMet`. [proof: S21 AS-13] [code: packages/backend/libs/domains/auctions/domain/auction-views.ts#toPublicView]
- **S21.R-04** Bids are accepted for `startsAt <= now < endsAt` by the injected clock; a bid at or after the start on a held `SCHEDULED` auction opens it in the same step, a bid before the start is `409 auction_not_started`, at the end or later `409 auction_closed` with the real status in `auctionStatus`. No client time is read. [proof: S21 AS-17, S21 AS-54] [code: packages/backend/libs/domains/auctions/domain/auction-state.ts#biddingDecision]
- **S21.R-05** Each bid is one transaction under the auction's row lock: decide, append the bid with a gapless `sequence`, update the auction under its version, append `bid_placed` (and `leader_changed` when the leader really changed). Fifty concurrent bidders end with the highest maximum leading at second maximum plus increment, in every repeat. Nothing in Redis decides a bid. [proof: S21 AS-19, S21 AS-20, S21 AS-22] [code: packages/backend/libs/domains/auctions/application/bid.service.ts#BidService]
- **S21.R-06** A bid and a create need an `Idempotency-Key`; a replay of the same principal returns the stored answer, a concurrent duplicate is `409 idempotency_in_flight`, another body `422 idempotency_key_reuse`, and another user's replay is a fresh bid. [proof: S21 AS-04, S21 AS-18] [code: packages/backend/libs/domains/auctions/api/auction-bid.controller.ts#AuctionBidController]
- **S21.R-07** A bidder is limited to 10 bids per 10 s (fail closed): the 11th is `429 rate_limited`; a limiter store outage or a failed or slow tenancy lookup is `503 bid_unavailable` with `Retry-After: 2` and records nothing. [proof: S21 AS-21, S21 AS-41] [code: packages/backend/libs/domains/auctions/api/auction.exception-filter.ts#AuctionExceptionFilter]
- **S21.R-08** A refused outbox append rolls the bid back: `500`, no row, unchanged state, nothing pushed; a realtime failure after the commit never changes the answer and is counted. [proof: S21 AS-22, S21 AS-23] [code: packages/backend/libs/domains/auctions/application/auction-publisher.ts#AuctionPublisher]
- **S21.R-09** A price-changing bid placed when at most 2 minutes remain sets `endsAt = min(original + 60 min, max(endsAt, now + 2 min))`; a `raised` or rejected bid never extends; the total extension never exceeds 60 minutes. The close job follows the end: a job of an older end leaves the auction `OPEN`, and one job exists per end. [proof: S21 AS-24, S21 AS-25, S21 AS-26, S21 AS-54] [code: packages/backend/libs/domains/auctions/domain/auction-schedule.ts#extendEnd]
- **S21.R-10** Members of the selling shop (any role) cannot bid: `403 shill_bid_forbidden`, counted. The membership lookup is made outside the transaction; at the close the leader is checked again before the transaction opens, a leader who became a member ends `UNSOLD(leader_ineligible)`, and a bid that changed the leader between the check and the lock sends the close round again. [proof: S21 AS-40, S21 AS-42] [code: packages/backend/libs/domains/auctions/application/auction-close.service.ts#AuctionCloseService]
- **S21.R-11** Scheduling needs a product of the shop (another shop's product is `404 product_not_found`), a start at least a minute ahead, a length of 10 minutes to 30 days, a reserve at least the starting price, an active product and a unit in stock; at most one live auction exists per product (a partial unique index), so two creates at once give one `201` and one `409 auction_exists_for_product`. [proof: S21 AS-01, S21 AS-02, S21 AS-05] [code: packages/backend/libs/domains/auctions/application/auction-schedule.service.ts#AuctionScheduleService]
- **S21.R-12** The unit leaves regular stock once through `auctions:<id>:hold` and returns at most once through `auctions:<id>:return` (a new operation, never a compensation), driven by jobs and never from inside a transaction; an auction opens only when its unit is held, a hold that cannot be done cancels it with `stock_hold_failed`, and a crash between the row and the hold is repaired by the sweeper. [proof: S21 AS-06, S21 AS-08, S21 AS-29] [code: packages/backend/libs/domains/auctions/application/auction-schedule.service.ts#AuctionScheduleService]
- **S21.R-13** An auction is `SCHEDULED → OPEN → CLOSED | UNSOLD`, `CLOSED → SOLD | SECOND_CHANCE | UNSOLD`, `SECOND_CHANCE → SOLD | UNSOLD`, or `CANCELLED` from `SCHEDULED` and from `OPEN` with no bids; every move is a conditional update with a history row and its event in one transaction, and every other pair is refused or ignored. [proof: S21 AS-54] [code: packages/backend/libs/domains/auctions/domain/auction-state.ts#transition]
- **S21.R-14** A seller cancels a scheduled auction, or an open one with no bids; with bids it is `409 auction_has_bids`, in other states `409 auction_not_cancellable`, a repeat is `200`, and a cancel and a first bid race to exactly one winner. [proof: S21 AS-08, S21 AS-09] [code: packages/backend/libs/domains/auctions/application/auction-schedule.service.ts#AuctionScheduleService]
- **S21.R-15** The close is one conditional `OPEN → CLOSED | UNSOLD` taken only when the stored end has passed, serialized with bids, with its history row, `auction.closed` and the follow-up job in one transaction; it is safe to run any number of times, concurrently or early. `UNSOLD` is `no_bids`, `reserve_not_met` or `leader_ineligible`; a lost close job is recovered by the 30 s sweeper. [proof: S21 AS-27, S21 AS-28, S21 AS-29, S21 AS-30, S21 AS-31, S21 AS-32] [code: packages/backend/libs/domains/auctions/application/auction-close.service.ts#AuctionCloseService]
- **S21.R-16** After a `CLOSED` commit the winner's order is requested once through `createFixedPriceOrder` (key `auction:<id>:winner`, the final price, `closedAt` plus 48 h, stock handling `EXTERNALLY_HELD`); a crash, a duplicate delivery and a transient failure leave one order and one stored ID, after 8 attempts the request dead-letters and is counted, and a permanent refusal is handled as an unpaid winner. [proof: S21 AS-27, S21 AS-33] [code: packages/backend/libs/domains/auctions/application/settlement.service.ts#SettlementService]
- **S21.R-17** `order.paid` of the awardee's order sells the auction (`auction.sold`); a duplicate, a lower `orderVersion`, an order that is not ours and any message for a terminal auction change nothing; an invalid payload is dead-lettered; a payment of the first order after the offer began is an anomaly (counted and logged), not a transition. [proof: S21 AS-34, S21 AS-39] [code: packages/backend/libs/domains/auctions/infra/auction-settlement.consumer.ts#AuctionSettlementConsumer]
- **S21.R-18** When the awardee's order is cancelled, lapses or is refused, the unit is offered once, at their own maximum for 24 hours, to the highest bidder other than the winner whose maximum reaches the reserve and who is not a member of the selling shop (ties to the earlier bid); no one eligible, or an unpaid offer, ends `UNSOLD` and returns the unit once; a second cancelled event gives no second offer. [proof: S21 AS-36, S21 AS-37, S21 AS-38, S21 AS-39] [code: packages/backend/libs/domains/auctions/domain/runner-up.ts#rankRunnersUp]
- **S21.R-19** The public view carries only public fields: a per-auction alias for the leader (stable for a bidder in one auction, different across bidders and auctions, not reversible), never a user ID, a maximum or the reserve amount; it is cached for a second, dropped after each commit, and read from the durable record when the cache is down. [proof: S21 AS-43, S21 AS-45] [code: packages/backend/libs/domains/auctions/domain/bidder-alias.ts#bidderAlias]
- **S21.R-20** `GET /auctions?productIds=` takes at most 100 distinct ids, returns the live and the recently closed auctions of those products and reads the table once. [proof: S21 AS-44] [code: packages/backend/libs/domains/auctions/application/auction-query.service.ts#AuctionQueryService]
- **S21.R-21** The bid history lists price-changing bids newest first by keyset with aliases and no maximum, across monthly partitions; `my-bid` returns only the caller's own maximum. [proof: S21 AS-46, S21 AS-47] [code: packages/backend/libs/domains/auctions/infra/bid.repository.ts#SequelizeBidRepository]
- **S21.R-22** A `price` message is pushed after each bid commits (never inside the transaction) and a `closed` message after the close, with a strictly rising `version` and no winner identity. [proof: S21 AS-48] [code: packages/backend/libs/domains/auctions/application/auction-publisher.ts#AuctionPublisher]
- **S21.R-23** Every lifecycle change writes its event through the outbox in the transaction, keyed by the auction; no payload carries a maximum, and the producer's payloads parse with the consumer schemas of the notifications capability. [proof: S21 AS-49] [code: packages/backend/libs/domains/auctions/application/events/auction-events.ts#AuctionClosed]
- **S21.R-24** The monthly bid partitions for the current month and the next three are created by a daily job that is safe to run twice at once; a bid in a month without a partition still succeeds in the default partition and raises a gauge. [proof: S21 AS-50] [code: packages/backend/libs/domains/auctions/infra/bid-partitions.service.ts#BidPartitionService]
- **S21.R-25** Counters, histograms, gauges, JSON log lines with `requestId` and `auctionId` (never a maximum), spans for the bid, the close and the order request, and four alerts with runbooks cover the domain. [proof: S21 AS-51] [code: packages/backend/libs/domains/auctions/domain/auction-metrics.ts#bidsCounter]
- **S21.R-26** The domain reads and writes no table of another domain, holds no foreign key, exports only its modules, rate-limit policies and event definitions, and opens every transaction through the transaction runner. [proof: S21 AS-52] [code: packages/backend/libs/domains/auctions/index.ts]
- **S21.R-27** The startup configuration (window, extension, cap, windows for payment and offer, sweep and hold times, the alias secret) is validated before the app listens, naming keys and never values. (Proven by `libs/common/config/auctions-config.spec.ts`, whose titles carry requirement ids, not scenario ids.) [proof: none] [code: packages/backend/libs/common/config/auctions-config.ts#auctionsRule]

## Behaviors
- **S21.B-01** Given an open auction with price 1000 and increment 500, when bidder one bids a maximum of 10000, then `200 leading` at price 1000 with `minimumNextMaxMinor` 1500, one bid row, one `bid_placed` and no `leader_changed`. [proof: S21 AS-10]
- **S21.B-02** Given the four bids 10000, 5000, 12000 and 11000 in turn, when each is placed, then the prices are 1000, 5500, 10500 and 11500, the leader changes once, and a bid of 10400 in between is `422 bid_too_low` asking for 11000. [proof: S21 AS-11]
- **S21.B-03** Given a bid with 90 s left, when it changes the price, then the end moves to 30 s past the old end and the push, the row and the event all say so. [proof: S21 AS-24]
- **S21.B-04** Given an auction with a reserve of 3000 where the leader's maximum is 4000, when it closes, then the winner is the leader at 3000, `auction.closed` says `winner`, and one order for 3000 is requested and held without moving stock. [proof: S21 AS-27]
- **S21.B-05** Given a closed auction whose winner's order lapses, when the runner-up qualifies, then the auction becomes `SECOND_CHANCE` with one offer at their maximum for 24 hours, and paying it sells the unit once. [proof: S21 AS-36, S21 AS-37]
- **S21.B-06** Given a member of the selling shop, when they bid, then `403 shill_bid_forbidden` and nothing is recorded. [proof: S21 AS-40]
