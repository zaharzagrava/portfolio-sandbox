# Decisions needing your call: S13 Payment intents, idempotency, unknown outcomes, PSP circuit breaker, outbox, saga with orders

- **S13.D-01** Which currencies and amount range can a customer pay in, given the old code charged only in USD?
  - default: EUR, USD and GBP, with each payment storing its own currency; amounts from 1 to 99,999,999 in minor units (about 1,000,000 in major units); anything outside that range is rejected with `422 amount_out_of_range`.
  - alternatives: EUR only, matching how S10 prices orders today; or a wider currency list or a higher cap, for example for expensive items.
  - if wrong: orders above the cap cannot be paid. A currency the provider account is not set up for fails at checkout. Both are lost sales or rework.
  - source: "Money: `amount` stored as a BIGINT read" (tagged [BREAKING])
  - status: open

- **S13.D-02** How long do we wait before declaring a payment failed when the payment provider has no record of it?
  - default: Keep the payment `UNKNOWN` for 60 minutes after the attempt began, then mark it `FAILED`. If it is still unresolved after 24 hours it stays `UNKNOWN` and raises an alert. We never re-send the charge.
  - alternatives: Wait longer, up to the provider's 24 h key lifetime, which is safer but leaves the customer waiting. Or fail sooner, which risks a double charge if the provider is only slow.
  - if wrong: too short can double-charge a customer, which means refunds and support cost. Too long leaves customers and held stock stuck.
  - source: "An unknown outcome is settled by redelivering the message" (tagged [BREAKING])
  - status: open

- **S13.D-03** Is a customer who must confirm a payment (3-D Secure) allowed to do that, and how long do we hold the order for them?
  - default: Payments that need customer action stay pending and return a `clientSecret` to the client, which completes the step with the provider's library. The order hold (`reservedUntil`) is the only time limit. If the order is cancelled or the hold expires, the provider intent is cancelled so it cannot be completed later.
  - alternatives: Do not support customer-action cards and fail them (loses European cards that require it). Or keep the intent alive past the hold, which risks a payment for a released order.
  - if wrong: Customers whose card needs 3-D Secure either cannot buy, or pay for an order that was already released.
  - source: "A provider answer other than `succeeded` is treated" (tagged [BREAKING])
  - status: open

- **S13.D-04** Can anyone refund part of an order, or can operators refund from a back-office screen?
  - default: Only full refunds, and only when the order was cancelled. There is no payments endpoint for operators. A refund request for any other reason or amount is dead-lettered and counted, not executed.
  - alternatives: Allow partial refunds, for example for returns or goodwill. Or add an operator refund endpoint with its own roles and permissions.
  - if wrong: Support cannot give goodwill or partial refunds until this is built. That means more cross-team work and manual refunds in the provider dashboard.
  - source: "Operator refunds (S11 says "operators refund through S13/S14")" (tagged [CONTRACT])
  - status: open

- **S13.D-05** How many payment attempts can a buyer make, and how do retries behave?
  - default: 10 payment-intent requests per buyer per minute. If the rate-limit store is down, requests are refused (fail closed). Retries with the same `Idempotency-Key` are remembered for 24 hours, and only successful `202` answers are remembered.
  - alternatives: A higher or lower limit. Fail open when the limiter is down, so payments keep working during an outage. A different retention for keys.
  - if wrong: Too strict blocks real buyers, including during a limiter outage, which means lost sales. Too loose weakens protection against card testing.
  - source: "Payment is keyed by the client's `idempotencyKey`" (tagged [BREAKING])
  - status: open

- **S13.D-06** Do we remove the old payment endpoints and the old Kafka entry point at once, even though existing clients use them?
  - default: Yes. The gateway's Kafka route, `GET /payment/by-key/…`, the unpaginated `GET /payment` list and the `/payment/stream` SSE endpoint are all removed. Clients get `paymentId` in the `202` and use `GET /payments` or `payment.status` push events.
  - alternatives: Keep the old routes for a transition period alongside the new ones. Or keep only `by-key` and the SSE stream until the clients have migrated.
  - if wrong: Any client or app version still on the old routes stops being able to pay or see status until it is updated.
  - source: "Routes `GET /payment/by-key/:idempotencyKey`, `GET /payment/:id`" (tagged [BREAKING])
  - status: open

- **S13.D-07** When a payment fails because the order was cancelled or its hold expired, does the customer still see a "payment failed" result?
  - default: Yes. A `payment_failed` event is also sent for cancelled payments, with reason `order_cancelled` or `order_not_payable`. S10 ignores it for an already cancelled order, but other consumers see it.
  - alternatives: Send no failure event for cancellations, and show the customer only "order cancelled". Or use a separate `payment_cancelled` event.
  - if wrong: A customer may see a confusing "payment failed" message for an order they or the system cancelled. This could generate support contacts or false failure alerts.
  - source: "S13 emits `payment_failed` also for `CANCELLED` payments" (tagged [CONTRACT])
  - status: open
