Seller dashboard, product photos at /dashboard/seller/{shopId}/products/{productId}/media (open it from the inventory row menu, "Photos and video"). THE KNOWN BUG to chase (docs/work/2026-10-20-whats-left-hanging.md section 8): by hand, after an upload the photo is "not properly loaded", it disappears on reload and "Save photos" does nothing, although the scripted test seller-photos.spec.ts passes. Reproduce it with the exact steps of a person: pick a real JPEG of 2 MB (generate one with node or use any file on disk), watch the tile go from sending to processing to ready, press "Save photos", reload. At EACH step record the layer: (1) the request POST /api/shops/{shopId}/media/uploads (status, body has upload.url and fields, no key), (2) the browser POST of the form to upload.url (host and port: is it reachable from the browser, is it blocked by the Content Security Policy connect-src or by missing CORS on the storage; read console errors and failed requests), (3) POST /api/media/{id}/complete (202 or 409 upload_incomplete), (4) polling GET /api/media/{id} until READY (the media worker "media-processing" must be running: check scripts/qa/stack-up.sh started it, its log lives in the stack folder; note if it is not and say so, that is the first suspect), (5) the image tiles: do the READY urls sit on a host listed in the web app's image hosts (MEDIA_HOST, next.config remotePatterns, img-src of the page)? a blocked host shows a broken tile or an empty one, (6) is "Save photos" enabled after the photo is READY (it is enabled only when the draft differs; if it stays disabled after an add, that is the defect), (7) PUT /api/shops/{shopId}/products/{productId}/gallery (body mediaIds and expectedVersion; 200 or 409 version_conflict or 422 media_not_attachable with failures), (8) after a reload, GET the same gallery: is the list there, and what does the database say (media rows, gallery rows, status). Report the bug once, at the layer where it first goes wrong, with the evidence. Then the other cases: a PNG, WebP, AVIF; a GIF and a PDF renamed .jpg (rejected with a clear message, not a stuck tile); a text file; an empty file; a file over 15 MB (refused before sending); a 60-megapixel image; an animated image; a photo with EXIF rotation (the result must be upright); 5 files at once; 21 photos (limit 20, "N of 20 photos"); remove a photo; reorder with the arrow buttons and keyboard, save, reload, order kept; two tabs saving the gallery (409 version_conflict "Load latest"); leaving the page with unsaved changes; losing the network mid-upload (message "The file couldn't be sent. Try again." and a retry works); the same photo uploaded twice; a VIEWER cannot add (controls disabled or hidden, API 403); a suspended shop disables writes. The public product page must show the saved photos (and nothing else) and an archived product's gallery answers 404. Out of scope: video, review photos.
