Seller dashboard, inventory and stock numbers: /dashboard/seller/inventory (shop chooser entry) and /dashboard/seller/{shopId}/inventory with its "Adjust stock" action. Stock status must read "Out of stock" at 0, "Low stock" from 1 to 5, "In stock" above 5 and "Archived" for an archived product whatever its quantity (R-08): check the borders 0, 1, 5, 6 and a negative number, a fractional one, 2147483648, an empty field, letters. Adjust stock: increase, decrease below zero (must be refused with a clear message, never stored), set an absolute number, the same adjustment sent twice by a double click or a retry (one change, not two; the request should carry an idempotency key), adjust in two tabs (stale version gives version_conflict and "Load latest"). After each change compare: the table, the database stock row and its history, the outbox event, the buyer product page (shows in stock or sold out), search with the inStock filter, and a cart that already holds the product. Race it: buy the last unit as a buyer (checkout with the Visa test card) while the seller sets stock to 5 in the other tab; the final number must equal 5 minus what was reserved, never negative, never oversold. Check the inStock filter and sorting by stock if offered, counts above the table, and that archived rows are not counted as in stock. A VIEWER sees stock but has no adjust control; the API refuses a direct adjust with 403. Reload keeps filters in the address. Check empty states (a shop with no products), the loading skeleton shape (no spinner, no word "Loading...", R-27) and the table at 390 px (cards, caption, headers; R-25). Out of scope: orders, photos, bulk import.

You may not: edit repository files, change the database by hand, delete other people's data, or report anything the page lists under "Known gaps".
