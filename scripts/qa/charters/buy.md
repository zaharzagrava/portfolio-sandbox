Buy a product as a brand-new customer, in the browser, from the storefront home page: find a product (search or browse), open its page, add it to the cart, change the quantity, sign up and sign in, check out, pay with the sandbox card, and see the order confirmed. Then look at "my orders". Check, behind each step, that the API, database and queue agree with the screen. Try the same flow once more with a reload in the middle of paying, and once with a double click on Pay. Out of scope: refunds, sellers.
