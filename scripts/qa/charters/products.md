name: products
specs: S05
status: draft
minutes: 15
Manage products as a shop member, with curl against the API at localhost:8000 and the product pages on localhost:3000. As a STAFF member of an active shop, create a product with POST /shops/:shopId/products (check 201, version 1, shop and creator taken from the token, not the body), list the shop with GET /shops/:shopId/products and follow nextCursor through about 45 products (pages of 20, 20 and 5, no duplicates, newest first), read one with GET /shops/:shopId/products/:productId, edit it with PATCH and expectedVersion, then archive and restore it. Then read it anonymously with GET /products/:productId and in the browser: check the ETag and a 304 on If-None-Match, that an archived product answers the same 404 as an unknown ID, and that GET /batch/products?ids=a,b,c keeps request order with null for the invisible one. Try to break it: unknown fields, null values, huge text, negative, fractional or giant prices, a foreign currency (422 currency_not_supported), a malformed product ID (400 before anything else), no token (401), a VIEWER (403), a non-member and an unknown shop (same 404), another shop's product ID, a stale or missing expectedVersion (409 version_conflict with currentVersion), a PATCH with identical values (no version bump, no event), editing an archived product, restoring an active one, and two simultaneous PATCHes with the same version (exactly one wins). Also fire more than 120 writes in a minute (429, reads still work) and many random-ID reads from one address. Behind the screen, check that each change leaves one versioned event on products.events, that the cache entry is dropped after a write and shows the new price on the next read, that a second cold read does not hit the database, and that a stale cache never brings back old data. Out of scope: orders, carts, shop settings, stock operations from other domains.
