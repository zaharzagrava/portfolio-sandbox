# QA report: buy
Tried: home -> product page -> Add to cart -> cart quantity +2 (guest) -> "Sign in to check out" -> register -> sign in (guest cart merged) -> /checkout -> Place order -> pay -> success page -> my orders. Ran it three times with fresh buyers: normal, reload right after pressing Pay, and double click on Place order. After each flow I checked the API calls, the BisOrder and Payment rows, the Outbox, the queues and the API log, and ran doctor-live.sh at the start, between flows and at the end (healthy each time).
Scripts: buy.js (modes normal|reload|double), orders.js, explore.js in SCRIPTS_DIR.

## Defects
DEFECT D1 CONFIRMED medium: `/orders` (my orders) is a 404 page; the history only exists at `/dashboard/orders`
- oracle: W03 spec (the route list says `/orders` is the order history and `/dashboard/orders` redirects permanently to it); also an empty/not-found page where data exists
- steps: 1. Buy anything as a new buyer (node buy.js normal). 2. Signed in, open http://localhost:3000/orders. 3. Compare with http://localhost:3000/dashboard/orders.
- expected: `/orders` lists the buyer's orders, newest first; `/dashboard/orders` redirects to it.
- actual: `/orders` answers 404 "Page not found" (also for an anonymous curl: 404), and the browser logs a 404 resource error. `/dashboard/orders` answers 200 and shows the order table (01A133E6 PAID $449.97), with no redirect. The header has no link to orders. The only way to the history is to know `/dashboard/orders`.
- evidence: `curl -s -o /dev/null -w '%{http_code}' http://localhost:3000/orders` gives 404; orders.js output; screenshots /tmp/tmp.HuYqr45gbw/orders_orders.png and orders_dashboard_orders.png. BisOrder 01a133e6-6da7-77d5-bbd3-e9f5a97dbaaf is PAID and is listed at /dashboard/orders.

DEFECT D2 CONFIRMED low: browser console error flood, inline styles blocked by the Content Security Policy (about 170-200 per flow)
- oracle: CONFIRMED 1 (browser console error)
- steps: 1. Open http://localhost:3000/ in headless Chromium with a `console` listener (explore.js). 2. Read the console errors.
- expected: no console errors.
- actual: dozens of "Applying inline style violates the following Content Security Policy directive 'style-src 'self' 'nonce-...''. ... The action has been blocked." on the home page alone. The count is 173, 203 and 174 across the three full flows. The styles are blocked, so some inline-styled elements may render without their styles. I did not check this visually, so that part is SUSPICIOUS.
- evidence: explore.js output; the "CSP errors" line printed at the end of buy.js. A single 401 on `GET /api/bff/session` for an anonymous visitor also shows as a console "Failed to load resource" error on each page load. That 401 is probably expected for signed-out visitors but is noisy.

## Clean
- Product page, Add to cart: PUT /api/cart/items/:id returned 200, the badge showed 1, and the "Added to cart" toast appeared (B-02).
- Quantity +,+ gave 3 items and $449.97, and the badge showed 3. The three writes were ordered and returned 200 (R-03).
- A guest cart showed "Sign in to check out" (B-05). After register and login, POST /cart/merge returned 200 and the 3 items were kept (B-06).
- /checkout showed the Review step, lines by shop and the estimated total (B-07). Place order led to /checkout/pay/{orderId} (B-09). Pay called POST /api/payments/intents (202). The success page showed "Order confirmed", "Order #01A133E4" and the lines (B-18).
- DB agreed with the screen in all three runs: BisOrder PAID, Payment COMPLETED (44997 = $449.97). Reload in the middle of paying showed "Payment is being processed…" and then settled to confirmed. Double click on Place order and on Pay created exactly one order and one payment (no duplicate rows per order).
- Outbox: all 94 rows are `published`, none parked or stale. Dead-letter queue payments-charge-dlq holds 0 messages. doctor-live.sh was healthy before, between and after the flows. The `HTTP 500 /api/payments/intents` and "queue does not exist" lines in the API log are timestamped 08:04-08:05, before my flows (about 08:09 onwards). I saw no 5xx or unhandled error during my runs.
- Not verified: the "Visa test card" choice was never shown to me. Payment went through automatically (provider ids look like `pi_loadtest_…`), so I could not exercise the pm_card_visa selection.
