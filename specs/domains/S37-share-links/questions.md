## LOCAL

- [LOCAL] Bijection width is 40 bits, not the note's 42 → 2^42 > 62^7, so 42 bits cannot fit 7 characters; 40 bits is 1.1×10^12 codes (SC-006).
- [LOCAL] Aliases are case-sensitive in storage but only lower case is accepted → no case folding needed anywhere.
- [LOCAL] Reserved alias list is configuration with a default of 13 words → easy to extend without a release.
- [LOCAL] Retention 13 months after expiry or disable, equal to click retention → one number to explain.
- [LOCAL] Stats `countries` limited to 10 and series sparse → bounded payload (at most 1,440 minute buckets in 24 h).
- [LOCAL] HEAD does not count; bots, previews and the owner's own visits do → fraud filtering is a later capability.
- [LOCAL] Reputation hook is fail-closed on timeout (2 s) with an allow-all default adapter → create is low volume, redirects unaffected.
- [LOCAL] Disabled and expired links share one `410` body → no side channel on why a link stopped.
- [LOCAL] State changes (disable, edit) keep `updatedAt` and `disabledAt` on the item instead of a separate history row; no events are emitted for them → there is no relational outbox for this store and no consumer needs them yet (III.7 history row is satisfied by the item's timestamps).
- [LOCAL] Per-owner cap is enforced by a conditional counter update in the same store as the links → III.6.
- [LOCAL] Click publishing is best effort with up to 2 retries off the request path, never blocking → SD-08 "never in the redirect path".
- [LOCAL] Disable is final (no re-enable) and expiry cannot be extended → two stored states; a new link is the escape hatch.
