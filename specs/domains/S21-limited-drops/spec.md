## Scope

In scope:

- **Auction lifecycle for sellers**: schedule an auction of one unit of one product (starting price, minimum increment, optional hidden reserve, start and end), see and list their auctions, cancel one that has no bids.
- **Bidding with a maximum (proxy bidding)**: a bidder states the most they will pay; the visible price rises only as far as needed to stay ahead (second-highest maximum plus one increment, never above the leader's maximum). Every accepted bid is durable the moment it is acknowledged.
- **Safe concurrency and retries**: bids on one auction are applied one at a time in a strict order; a retried bid never counts twice (`Idempotency-Key`); a per-bidder rate limit.
- **Anti-sniping**: a price-changing bid in the final minutes pushes the end out, up to a cap. The server's clock decides every time-based question.
- **Reserve price**: a hidden minimum the seller will accept; below it the auction ends unsold.
- **Exactly-once close**: at the end, the auction closes once, whichever of many scheduled or retried triggers fires, and the result (winner and price, or unsold) is final and event-published.
- **Winner settlement**: the winner's order is requested from the order capability exactly once; payment moves the auction to sold; non-payment triggers the **second-chance offer** to the runner-up (once), and finally returns the unit.
- **Shill-bid guard**: members of the selling shop cannot bid; re-checked when the auction is awarded.
- **Unit hold**: the auctioned unit leaves regular stock for the auction's life and returns exactly once if unsold or cancelled.
- **Public reads**: the live state of an auction (price, countdown, server time), the public bid history (price-changing bids, bidders pseudonymised), a bidder's own maximum, and the live price push.
- **Bid history storage**: append-only, partitioned by month, partitions always created ahead (P0318).
- **Observability** of all of the above.

Out of scope (owners named):

- Order creation mechanics, the order state machine, the payment window enforcement, the expiry sweeper and `order.*` events → **S10** (`orders`). This capability asks S10 for an order through a command (see Cross-capability contracts) and reacts to S10's events.
- Payment intents, refunds, payouts → **S13**, **S14**, **S15**. The winner pays through the normal order payment path.
- Product data and stock arithmetic → **S05** (`catalog`, R1). Shops, roles, suspension → **S03**. Plan entitlement `auctions` → **S17/S18** (`billing`, R1 and route guard). Authentication → **S01**.
- Shared platform pieces (jobs S49, limiter S50, realtime S51, cache S52, outbox/inbox/consumers S53, problem+json, idempotency facility, clock, config, metrics S54).
- Notification content and delivery ("you've been outbid", "you won", "second chance") → **S28**, which consumes this capability's events. Feed items such as `auction_started` (community) are consumers of the events too.
- All screens (auction page, bid form, countdown, seller form) → the web capability for auction pages; the product title and image shown next to an auction are composed by the BFF (**S48**, IX.7 R2).
- Fixed-price drops with bucketed stock → **S11**. Queue-based launches → **S22**.
- Multi-unit or Dutch auctions, buy-it-now, auto-extending reserves, tiered increments: not provided.

## User Scenarios & Testing *(mandatory)*

Notation: amounts are integer minor units of the auction's currency (`1000` = 10.00 EUR). `U1…Un` are bidders, `M` is a member of the selling shop. `S1` is the selling shop, `S2` another shop. A **maximum** is the amount a bidder is willing to pay (`maxAmountMinor`); the **price** is what the auction currently shows (`priceMinor`); the **leader** is the bidder who would win now. "Time is frozen" means tests control the clock. Every error is `application/problem+json` with `type`, `title`, `status`, `detail`, `instance`, `requestId` and a stable `code`. Unless said otherwise, a request is authenticated and carries a valid `Idempotency-Key`.

**Dataset DS-1** (used throughout unless a scenario says otherwise). Shop `S1` (plan with `auctions: true`), product `P` of `S1` (price `20000` EUR, stock `3`, `ACTIVE`), auction `A` on `P`: `startingPriceMinor 1000`, `minIncrementMinor 500`, no reserve, `startsAt = T`, `endsAt = T + 60 min`, status `OPEN` at `T`. Bidders `U1, U2, U3` are not members of `S1`; `M` is `STAFF` of `S1`.

### User Story 1 — A seller schedules, inspects and cancels an auction (Priority: P1)

A seller with the auctions feature puts one unit of a product up for auction with a start, an end, a starting price, a minimum increment and an optional hidden reserve. The unit leaves regular stock immediately so it cannot be sold twice. The seller can list and inspect their auctions and cancel one before anyone has bid.

**Why this priority**: nothing else exists without a valid auction; a unit that can be sold twice (auction and shelf) is a direct loss.

**Independent Test**: create an auction, check stock went from 3 to 2, list it, cancel it, check stock is 3 again.

**Acceptance Scenarios**:

1. **AS-01** (happy path) — **Given** DS-1 before `A` exists and time frozen at `T − 5 min`, **When** `S1`'s owner `POST /shops/S1/auctions` with `{productId: P, title: "Signed #001/100", startingPriceMinor: 1000, minIncrementMinor: 500, reservePriceMinor: 3000, startsAt: T, endsAt: T + 60 min}`, **Then** `201` with `Location: /api/shops/S1/auctions/<id>` and a body parsed by `auctionMemberSchema` showing status `SCHEDULED`, `currency: "EUR"`, `reservePriceMinor: 3000`, `bidCount: 0`, `priceMinor: 1000`; and exactly: one auction row (version 1, unit held), one history row (`null → SCHEDULED`), the catalog stock of `P` is `2` (one stock operation `auctions:<id>:hold`, delta `−1`, reason `auction.hold`), one outbox row `auction.created`, two delayed jobs (`auctions.open` at `T`, `auctions.close` at `T + 60 min`), all created together or not at all.
2. **AS-02** (validation classes) — **Given** the same shop, **When** the body has any of: `title` empty or longer than 140 characters; `startingPriceMinor`, `minIncrementMinor` or `reservePriceMinor` equal to 0, negative, fractional, a string, or above `1,000,000,000,000`; `startsAt` or `endsAt` malformed; an unknown property (e.g. `buyNowMinor`); `productId` not a UUID, **Then** each answers `400 validation_failed` naming the field and changes nothing. **When** the body is well formed but: `reservePriceMinor < startingPriceMinor` → `422 reserve_below_starting`; `startsAt < now + 1 min` → `422 starts_too_soon`; `endsAt − startsAt` below 10 min or above 30 days → `422 duration_out_of_range`; `P` `ARCHIVED` → `422 product_unavailable`; stock `0` → `422 insufficient_stock`; `P` belongs to `S2` → `404 product_not_found` (existence hidden); **Then** nothing is created, no job, no outbox row, stock unchanged.
3. **AS-03** (authorization) — **When** the create route is called without credentials → `401`; by a member of `S1` without `products.write` → `403`; by someone who is not a member of `S1` → `404 shop_not_found`; by a member of a shop whose plan lacks the `auctions` entitlement → `403 entitlement_required {feature: "auctions"}`; for a suspended shop → S03's `403 shop_suspended`; **Then** in every case nothing is created and stock is unchanged. The same `401`/`404` rules hold for list, read and cancel.
4. **AS-04** (idempotent create) — **Given** AS-01 completed with key `K`, **When** the same request is replayed with `K`, **Then** `201` with the original body and `Idempotency-Replayed: true`; one auction, stock still `2`. **When** five identical requests with a new key `K2` run at once, **Then** each answers the same `201` body or `409 idempotency_in_flight`, at least one `201`, exactly one new auction exists and stock was lowered once. **When** `K` is reused with a different body → `422 idempotency_key_reuse`; no header → `422 idempotency_key_required`; a key shorter than 8, longer than 128 or with characters outside `[A-Za-z0-9_-]` → `422 idempotency_key_invalid`.
5. **AS-05** (one live auction per product) — **Given** `A` is `SCHEDULED` on `P`, **When** a second create for `P` is sent with a new key, **Then** `409 auction_exists_for_product` and nothing changes. **When** two creates for `P` (different keys) run at once with no auction existing, **Then** exactly one answers `201`, the other `409 auction_exists_for_product`, stock lowered once. **When** `A` becomes `CANCELLED` or any other terminal status, **Then** a new create for `P` is accepted.
6. **AS-06** (unit hold is exactly once and gates bidding) — **Given** a create that crashed after the auction row was written but before the stock operation ran (test crash switch), **When** the hold sweeper runs twice, **Then** the stock operation `auctions:<id>:hold` is applied once (stock `2`) and the auction becomes biddable at its start. **Given** the same crash and `P`'s stock has meanwhile dropped to `0`, **When** the sweeper runs, **Then** the auction becomes `CANCELLED(stock_hold_failed)`, an `auction.cancelled` event is written, stock is unchanged, and a bid on it answers `409 auction_closed`. An auction whose unit is not held never opens: the open step at `startsAt` leaves it `SCHEDULED` until the hold is applied or the sweeper cancels it (never later than 5 min after creation).
7. **AS-07** (seller read, list, IDOR) — **Given** `A` and another auction `B2` of `S1`, and an auction `X` of `S2`, **When** `S1`'s owner `GET /shops/S1/auctions?limit=1`, **Then** `auctionPageSchema` with the newest-starting auction first (order `(startsAt DESC, id DESC)`), `nextCursor` opaque, and following the cursor returns the other one with `nextCursor: null`; `limit` above 100 or 0 → `400 validation_failed`; a tampered or foreign cursor → `400 invalid_cursor`; `?status=OPEN` filters. **When** `GET /shops/S1/auctions/<A>`, **Then** `200` `auctionMemberSchema` including `reservePriceMinor`, `bidCount`, `leaderAlias`, `winnerOrderId` when set. **When** `S1`'s owner asks for `X` through `/shops/S1/auctions/<X>` or `/shops/S2/auctions/<X>`, **Then** `404 auction_not_found` with a body identical to that of a random UUID, nothing disclosed. The seller never sees any bidder's user ID or maximum.
8. **AS-08** (cancel and its guards) — **Given** `A` `SCHEDULED` with stock `2`, **When** `POST /shops/S1/auctions/<A>/cancel`, **Then** `200 auctionMemberSchema` status `CANCELLED(seller_cancelled)`, a history row, `auction.cancelled` outbox row, and the unit goes back through the stock operation `auctions:<id>:return` (stock `3`); the later `auctions.close` job finds a terminal auction and does nothing. **When** the same cancel is repeated, **Then** `200`, no further change, stock still `3`. **Given** `A` `OPEN` with zero bids, **Then** cancel is accepted the same way. **Given** `A` `OPEN` with at least one bid, **Then** `409 auction_has_bids`; **Given** `A` `CLOSED`, `SECOND_CHANCE`, `SOLD` or `UNSOLD`, **Then** `409 auction_not_cancellable`; nothing changes in either case. A user without `products.write` → `403`; another shop's auction → `404 auction_not_found`.
9. **AS-09** (cancel races the first bid) — **Given** `A` `OPEN` with no bids, **When** the cancel and a bid by `U1` run at once (`Promise.all`), **Then** exactly one wins: either cancel `200` and the bid `409 auction_closed` (no bid row, stock `3`), or the bid `200` and cancel `409 auction_has_bids` (one bid row, stock `2`); never a cancelled auction with a bid, and the unit is returned iff the auction is cancelled.

### User Story 2 — Bidders bid with a maximum and the visible price resolves like a proxy bidder would (Priority: P1)

A bidder says "I'll pay up to 100.00". The price shown is only as high as needed to beat the others, and rises automatically on the bidder's behalf when someone challenges, until their maximum is reached.

**Why this priority**: it is the core of an English auction with proxy bids.

**Independent Test**: three bidders with different maximums bid in sequence; check the price, leader and outcome after each.

**Acceptance Scenarios**:

1. **AS-10** (first bid, happy path) — **Given** DS-1 with `A` `OPEN`, time frozen at `T + 10 min`, **When** `U1` `POST /auctions/A/bids` with key `k1-aaaa-0001` and `{maxAmountMinor: 10000}`, **Then** `200` parsed by `bidResultSchema`: `{outcome: "leading", priceMinor: 1000, youAreLeading: true, leaderAlias, endsAt: T + 60 min, version: 1, serverTime, minimumNextMaxMinor: 1500}`; and exactly: one bid row (`maxAmountMinor 10000`, outcome `leading`, `priceAfterMinor 1000`, sequence 1), the auction row (`priceMinor 1000`, leader `U1`, `bidCount 1`, `version 1`), one outbox row `auction.bid_placed`, no `auction.leader_changed` (there was no previous leader), a realtime `price` message after commit.
2. **AS-11** (proxy resolution, the table) — **Given** AS-10 done (`U1` max 10000, price 1000), **When** the following bids are made in order, **Then** each answers as shown and the state after it is exactly:
   | Bid | Result | Price | Leader | Notes |
   |---|---|---|---|---|
   | `U2` max 5000 | `200 outbid` | 5500 (`min(10000, 5000 + 500)`) | `U1` | `U1`'s proxy defended; `auction.bid_placed` written, no `leader_changed` |
   | `U3` max 12000 | `200 leading` | 10500 (`min(12000, 10000 + 500)`) | `U3` | `auction.leader_changed {previousLeaderId: U1, leaderId: U3, priceMinor: 10500}` |
   | `U1` max 10400 | `422 bid_too_low {minimumMaxAmountMinor: 11000}` | 10500 | `U3` | nothing recorded |
   | `U1` max 11000 | `200 outbid` | 11500 (`min(12000, 11000 + 500)`) | `U3` | |
   After the table there are exactly 4 bid rows (`U1` 10000, `U2` 5000, `U3` 12000, `U1` 11000: sequences 1–4, gapless; the rejected bid has none), the auction has `bidCount 4`, `version 4`, and the price never decreased.
3. **AS-12** (a tie goes to the earlier bidder) — **Given** the end state of AS-11 (`U3` leads with maximum 12000, price 11500), **When** `U2` bids `{maxAmountMinor: 12000}`, **Then** `200 outbid`, price `12000` (`min(12000, 12000 + 500)`), leader stays `U3`, no `leader_changed`; **When** `U2` then bids `{maxAmountMinor: 12500}` (the minimum, `12000 + 500`), **Then** `200 leading`, price `12500` (`min(12500, 12000 + 500)`), leader `U2`, and `auction.leader_changed {previousLeaderId: U3, leaderId: U2, priceMinor: 12500}`; **When** `U3` (no longer the leader) then bids 12999, **Then** `422 bid_too_low {minimumMaxAmountMinor: 13000}` (`12500 + 500`).
4. **AS-13** (reserve: hidden, and met when a maximum reaches it) — **Given** DS-1 but `A` has `reservePriceMinor: 3000`, **When** `U1` bids max 2500, **Then** `200 leading`, price `1000`, `reserveMet: false`; **When** `U2` bids max 4000, **Then** `200 leading`, price `3000` (`min(4000, 2500 + 500)` is 3000), `reserveMet: true`; **Given** a fresh auction with the same reserve, **When** `U1` bids max 4000 as the first bid, **Then** price `3000` (the price rises to the reserve because the maximum covers it), `reserveMet: true`; **Given** a fresh one, **When** `U1` bids max 2999, **Then** price stays `1000`, `reserveMet: false`. In every response, view, history, event and realtime message the reserve **amount** never appears; only `hasReserve` and `reserveMet` do (the seller's own read shows the amount).
5. **AS-14** (raising your own maximum; never lowering) — **Given** the state after the first step of AS-12 (`U3` leads with maximum 12000 and price 12000) at `T + 10 min`, **When** `U3` bids max 15000, **Then** `200 raised`, price `12000`, leader `U3`, `endsAt` unchanged, `bidCount` +1, one `auction.bid_placed` (outcome `raised`), no `leader_changed`; **When** `U3` bids 15000 again with a new key, or 14000, **Then** `422 bid_not_increasing {currentMaxAmountMinor: 15000}` and nothing is recorded. A `raised` bid never extends the end (AS-24).
6. **AS-15** (minimum bids) — **Given** DS-1 with no bids, **When** `U1` bids 999, **Then** `422 bid_too_low {minimumMaxAmountMinor: 1000}`; 1000 is accepted (`leading`, price 1000). **Given** `U1` leads at price 1000 (maximum 1000), **When** `U2` bids 1499, **Then** `422 bid_too_low {minimumMaxAmountMinor: 1500}`; **When** `U2` bids 1500, **Then** `200 leading`, price `1500`. Rejected bids create no row, no event, no history, and do not change `version`.
7. **AS-16** (validation, authentication, unknown auction) — **When** `POST /auctions/A/bids` has `maxAmountMinor` 0, negative, 1.5, `"100"`, above `1,000,000,000,000`, missing, or an unknown property (e.g. `clientTime`), **Then** `400 validation_failed` naming the field; a malformed auction ID → `400`; no credentials → `401`; an unknown auction ID → `404 auction_not_found`. Nothing is recorded in any case.
8. **AS-17** (the server's clock decides) — **Given** `A` with `startsAt = T`, `endsAt = T + 60 min`, **When** a bid is made at `T − 1 s`, **Then** `409 auction_not_started {startsAt}`; at `T` accepted even if the open job has not run yet (the bid opens the auction, one `auction.opened`, and the later open job is a no-op); at `endsAt − 1 ms` accepted; at `endsAt` exactly and later `409 auction_closed {status}` with the auction's real status (still `OPEN` until the close runs; the end is exclusive: bidding is open for `startsAt ≤ now < endsAt`); a bid on a `CLOSED`, `UNSOLD`, `SOLD`, `SECOND_CHANCE` or `CANCELLED` auction → `409 auction_closed {status}`. A client-supplied time (header `X-Client-Time` or any body field) is ignored or refused (AS-16) and never changes the outcome; every bid response and the view carry `serverTime`.

### User Story 3 — Bids stay correct under crowds, retries and failures (Priority: P1)

Dozens of people bid in the last seconds, phones retry, the network drops. Every accepted bid is counted once, in one agreed order, and none is lost, even if the fast store (cache, limiter, realtime) loses its data.

**Why this priority**: money changes hands on the result; a lost or doubled bid is a dispute.

**Independent Test**: fire 50 bids at once from 50 users; verify winner, price, gapless sequence; replay one with the same key.

**Acceptance Scenarios**:

1. **AS-18** (idempotent bid) — **Given** `U1` bid with key `K` (AS-10), **When** the same request is replayed with `K` sequentially, **Then** `200` with the original body byte for byte and `Idempotency-Replayed: true`; no new bid row, event or version change, even if the auction has since closed. **When** five identical requests with a new key run at once, **Then** responses are the same `200` or `409 idempotency_in_flight` (with `Retry-After: 1`), at least one `200`, exactly one bid row. **When** `K` is reused with `{maxAmountMinor: 11000}` → `422 idempotency_key_reuse`; header missing → `422 idempotency_key_required`; malformed key → `422 idempotency_key_invalid`; **When** the replay arrives from a different user with `K`, **Then** it is treated as a different principal's request (no stored body is returned to the wrong user) and is a fresh bid.
2. **AS-19** (hot auction: 50 concurrent bidders) — **Given** DS-1 with no bids and 50 bidders `V0…V49` with maximums `10000 + 1000·i` (10000…59000), **When** all 50 bid at once (`Promise.all`), **Then** every response is `200` or `422 bid_too_low`; the final state is leader `V49`, price `58500` (`58000 + 500`), regardless of arrival order, in 50 repeated runs; the accepted bids have unique, gapless sequences `1…N` where `N = bidCount`; `N` equals the number of bid rows and of `auction.bid_placed` outbox rows; each response's `priceMinor` equals the price right after that bid; the price never decreased in sequence order; and the stored sequence order is the order in which the bids were applied.
3. **AS-20** (equal maximums at once) — **Given** `A` with no bids, **When** `U1` and `U2` both bid `{maxAmountMinor: 8000}` at once, **Then** exactly one answers `leading` and the other `outbid`; the leader is the one whose bid was applied first (lower sequence); price `8000`; one `leader_changed` is not emitted when the first bid is applied, and none for the second (the leader never changed).
4. **AS-21** (rate limit and limiter failure) — **Given** policy `auction.bid` (10 bids per 10 s per bidder, fail closed), **When** `U1` sends 11 valid bids within 10 s, **Then** the 11th answers `429 rate_limited` with `Retry-After` and records nothing; **When** the limiter's store is made unavailable (forced fault), **Then** a bid answers `503 bid_unavailable` with `Retry-After: 2`, records nothing, and **reads still work** (AS-45).
5. **AS-22** (durability and degradation) — **Given** three accepted bids, **When** all cache, limiter and realtime data is wiped (store flush) and the realtime transport is made to fail, **Then** the next bid is accepted against the durable state (`200`, correct outcome and price), the view and the close use the durable record, no acknowledged bid is lost (3 + 1 rows), and a realtime publish failure never changes the bid's response (the outbox row is present, a failure counter increments).
6. **AS-23** (an event-write failure rolls the bid back) — **Given** the outbox append is made to fail (forced fault), **When** `U1` bids, **Then** the answer is `500` (generic problem+json, no internals), no bid row exists, the auction's price, leader, version and `bidCount` are unchanged, nothing was published; **When** the same request is retried with the same key after the fault clears, **Then** it is accepted as a first attempt (`200`).

### User Story 4 — Late bids extend the end so nobody wins by sniping (Priority: P1)

A bid that changes the price in the last two minutes gives everyone a further two minutes, up to a cap, so the winner is decided by willingness to pay, not by who clicks last.

**Why this priority**: it is the fairness rule of the format (note §22: server time decides, extend by N minutes on late bids).

**Independent Test**: bid with 90 s left; check the end moved; bid with 10 minutes left; check it did not.

**Acceptance Scenarios**:

1. **AS-24** (extension and its limits) — **Given** `A` with `endsAt = E` (originally `T + 60 min`), **When** `U1` bids a price-changing bid at `E − 90 s`, **Then** `endsAt` becomes `E + 30 s` (`max(E, now + 2 min)`) in the response, the row, the `price` realtime message and the `auction.bid_placed` event (`extended: true`); a bid at `E − 121 s` does not move the end (`extended: false`); a bid at `E − 120 s` does not change it either (`max(E, E) = E`); an own-maximum `raised` bid at `E − 10 s` does not extend; a rejected (`bid_too_low`) bid never extends.
2. **AS-25** (the cap) — **Given** the end has been extended so that `endsAt = originalEndsAt + 59 min 30 s`, **When** a price-changing bid arrives 10 s before it, **Then** `endsAt = originalEndsAt + 60 min` exactly (the cap, never later); a further price-changing bid 5 s before that end is accepted and leaves `endsAt` unchanged; a bid at the capped end exactly is `409 auction_closed`. The total extension is never more than 60 min (table test over many sequences, AS-53).
3. **AS-26** (the close job follows the end) — **Given** AS-24's extension, **When** the `auctions.close` job of the original end `E` runs at `E`, **Then** the auction stays `OPEN`, no event and no history row are written, a new close job exists for `E + 30 s` (idempotency key `auction-close:<id>:<endsAtMs>`; a repeat of the original job creates no second one), and when time reaches `E + 30 s` the close happens (AS-27).

### User Story 5 — The auction closes exactly once and the winner gets an order (Priority: P1)

At the end the system decides once, for all: sold to the leader at the shown price (if the reserve is met), or unsold. A sold auction asks the order capability for the winner's order at that price. An unsold auction returns the unit.

**Why this priority**: closing twice (two winners, double order, double stock return) or never is the costly failure.

**Independent Test**: end an auction with bids, deliver the close trigger three times at once, verify one result, one order request, no stock return.

**Acceptance Scenarios**:

1. **AS-27** (close with a winner, the full effect) — **Given** `A` with reserve 3000, `U1` leading at price 3000 (AS-13, first-bid variant), time frozen at `endsAt` and the close job delivered, **Then** exactly: the auction is `CLOSED` with `winnerId U1`, `finalPriceMinor 3000`, `closedAt = endsAt`; one history row `OPEN → CLOSED(winner)`; one outbox row `auction.closed {status: "CLOSED", winnerId: U1, finalPriceMinor: 3000, currency: "EUR", reserveMet: true, reason: "winner"}`; one `closed` realtime message after commit; one delayed job to request the winner's order; no stock operation (the unit stays held). After that job runs, S10 received one order command with idempotency key `auction:<id>:winner`, price 3000, quantity 1, buyer `U1`, shop `S1`, `reservedUntil = closedAt + 48 h`, stock handling `EXTERNALLY_HELD`; the auction stores `winnerOrderId`; the order is `RESERVED` at `3000` with no discount and no stock movement.
2. **AS-28** (exactly-once close under duplicates) — **Given** AS-27's setup, **When** the close job is delivered three times at once (`Promise.all`), and again sequentially afterwards, **Then** exactly one transition `OPEN → CLOSED` happened: one history row, one `auction.closed`, one winner-order request, one `closed` message; the other deliveries are logged no-ops; `auctionVersion` advanced once.
3. **AS-29** (reserve not met) — **Given** `A` with reserve 3000 where `U1` bid max 2500 and `U2` max 2900 (price 2900, `reserveMet: false`), **When** the auction closes, **Then** the status is `UNSOLD(reserve_not_met)`, no winner, `finalPriceMinor: null`, `auction.closed {status: "UNSOLD", winnerId: null, finalPriceMinor: null, reserveMet: false, reason: "reserve_not_met"}`, no order request, and the unit is returned once through `auctions:<id>:return` (stock `3`); repeating the close or the return changes nothing.
4. **AS-30** (no bids) — **Given** `A` with no bids, **When** it closes, **Then** `UNSOLD(no_bids)`, `auction.closed {status: "UNSOLD", reason: "no_bids", reserveMet: false}`, the unit is returned once, no order.
5. **AS-31** (a lost close job is recovered) — **Given** the close job of `A` was lost (deleted before it ran) and `endsAt` has passed, **When** the due-auction sweeper (every 30 s, single-run across instances) runs, **Then** `A` closes exactly as in AS-27 within 30 s of `endsAt`; running the sweeper twice at once and then the late original job changes nothing further. A sweeper run that finds `OPEN` auctions whose end has not passed does nothing to them.
6. **AS-32** (a bid races the close) — **Given** `U1` leads and `endsAt` is about to pass, **When** a bid by `U1` raising their maximum is applied at `endsAt − 1 ms` (a gate holds the bid inside its decision) and the close is attempted at `endsAt` meanwhile, **Then** the close waits for the bid, then closes with the raised state; the bid is acknowledged `200`; **and When** a bid is applied at or after `endsAt`, **Then** it is `409 auction_closed`. Invariant (asserted in both orders): no `200` is ever returned for a bid whose effect is missing from the closed result, and no bid is recorded after the close committed.
7. **AS-33** (the winner order request survives crashes and failures) — **Given** AS-27 closed but the process crashed before the order request ran (crash switch), **When** the request job is retried (and delivered twice at once), **Then** S10 holds exactly one order for key `auction:<id>:winner` and `winnerOrderId` is set once. **When** S10 fails transiently (timeout) → the job retries with exponential backoff and full jitter, at most 8 attempts, with the same key; after that it dead-letters and the auction stays `CLOSED` with the metric `auction_order_request_failed_total` incremented and an alert. **When** S10 rejects the buyer permanently (e.g. the account is gone), **Then** the auction proceeds exactly as if the winner's order had been cancelled (AS-36/AS-38).
8. **AS-34** (the winner pays) — **Given** `A` `CLOSED` with `winnerOrderId O`, **When** S10's `order.paid {orderId: O, …}` is delivered, **Then** the auction becomes `SOLD`, one history row `CLOSED → SOLD`, one outbox row `auction.sold {buyerId: U1, finalPriceMinor: 3000, orderId: O, viaSecondChance: false}`; **When** the same message is delivered again (VII.4), **Then** nothing changes (inbox plus conditional update). **When** a message with an invalid payload (missing `orderId`, wrong types) is delivered, **Then** it is rejected or dead-lettered with no state change. A message for an order that is not this capability's is ignored without error.
9. **AS-35** (job payloads) — **When** `auctions.close`, `auctions.open` or the order-request job receives an unknown auction ID or a malformed payload, **Then** it is rejected or dead-lettered, no state changes, nothing is published; **When** `auctions.open` is delivered twice, **Then** `SCHEDULED → OPEN` happens once (one history row, one `auction.opened`).

### User Story 6 — If the winner does not pay, the runner-up gets one second chance (Priority: P2)

When the winner's order is cancelled or lapses, the unit is offered once to the runner-up at their own maximum; if that is not paid either, the unit goes back on the shelf.

**Why this priority**: it recovers sales lost to non-payment and returns the unit when no one pays; it depends on the close flow (US5).

**Independent Test**: close with two bidders, cancel the winner's order, check the offer; pay or lapse it.

**Acceptance Scenarios**:

1. **AS-36** (offer to the runner-up) — **Given** no reserve, `U1` max 10000, `U2` max 5000, `U3` max 3000 (price 5500, leader `U1`), the auction closed and `U1`'s order `O1` `RESERVED`, **When** S10 emits `order.cancelled {orderId: O1, reason: "hold_expired"}` (or `user_cancelled`, `payment_failed`), **Then** the auction becomes `SECOND_CHANCE`; history `CLOSED → SECOND_CHANCE`; exactly one order command to S10 with key `auction:<id>:second-chance`, buyer `U2`, price `5000` (the runner-up's own maximum), `reservedUntil = now + 24 h`; the auction stores `winnerId U2`, `finalPriceMinor 5000`, `winnerOrderId O2`; one outbox row `auction.second_chance_offered {offeredToId: U2, priceMinor: 5000, currency, expiresAt}`; the unit stays held.
2. **AS-37** (offer outcomes) — **Given** AS-36, **When** `order.paid {orderId: O2}` arrives, **Then** `SOLD` with `auction.sold {buyerId: U2, finalPriceMinor: 5000, viaSecondChance: true}`; **When** instead `order.cancelled {orderId: O2}` arrives, **Then** `UNSOLD(second_chance_unpaid)`, `auction.unsold {reason: "second_chance_unpaid"}`, the unit is returned once (`auctions:<id>:return`), and there is no third offer (an auction offers at most once).
3. **AS-38** (no eligible runner-up) — **Given** a closed auction whose winner's order is cancelled, **When** the only other bidder's maximum is below the reserve, or there is no other bidder, or the runner-up is a member of the selling shop (shill guard), **Then** the auction becomes `UNSOLD(winner_unpaid)`, `auction.unsold {reason: "winner_unpaid"}`, the unit is returned once, no order command is sent; **When** the best runner-up is ineligible but a lower one is eligible (maximum ≥ reserve, not a member), **Then** the offer goes to the highest eligible bidder (ties on maximum go to the earlier bid) at that bidder's maximum.
4. **AS-39** (duplicate, late and out-of-order messages) — **Given** AS-36 in progress, **When** `order.cancelled {orderId: O1}` is delivered a second time, **Then** one offer only (one `second_chance_offered`, one order command). **When** `order.paid {orderId: O1}` arrives after the offer started (a late payment), **Then** no state transition happens, a `auction_settlement_anomaly_total{kind="late_payment"}` counter increments and a warning with `auctionId` and `orderId` is logged (an operator refunds through S13/S14). **When** `order.paid {orderId: O2}` arrives before the offer's own order ID is stored (the command's reply was slow), **Then** it is held for redelivery (not acknowledged as done) and applied once the ID is stored, giving `SOLD` once. **When** a message carries a lower `orderVersion` than one already applied for the same order, **Then** it is ignored. A message for an auction already `SOLD`, `UNSOLD` or `CANCELLED` is ignored.

**Independent Test**: a staff member bids and is refused; a non-member bids and is accepted.

A member of another shop `S2` may bid.
2. **AS-41** (the guard fails closed) — **Given** the tenancy lookup fails or times out (forced fault), **When** any user bids, **Then** `503 bid_unavailable` with `Retry-After: 2`, nothing recorded (no bid is accepted on an unchecked identity).
3. **AS-42** (awarding re-checks) — **Given** `U1` leads and, before the close, `U1` becomes a member of `S1`, **When** the auction closes, **Then** it is `UNSOLD(leader_ineligible)`, `auction.closed {status: "UNSOLD", reason: "leader_ineligible"}`, the unit is returned once and no order is requested; the tenancy check happens before the close's database transaction opens (no network call inside it); if a bid changes the leader between the check and the transaction, the close re-evaluates and never awards an unchecked leader.

### Edge Cases

- A product with a live auction cannot start a second one (AS-05); its unit is out of regular stock while the auction lives, so the shelf cannot sell it (AS-01); it returns exactly once on unsold or cancelled (AS-08, AS-29, AS-30, AS-37, AS-38).
- Bids of exactly the minimum are accepted; one below is not (AS-15); the first bid may equal the starting price (AS-15).
- The seller edits the product (price, title, archive) during the auction: the auction's own title, prices and currency are fixed at scheduling; archiving the product does not stop the auction (the unit is already held).
- The seller's shop is suspended during an auction: bidding and closing continue; the winner's order follows S10's rules for suspended shops (S10); a suspension never silently cancels bids.
- The winner's account is deleted or blocked: S10 rejects the order permanently → same path as non-payment (AS-33, AS-36).
- A user bids on several auctions: bids are independent; the rate limit is per bidder, not per auction.
- Clock differences between instances: every decision uses the injected clock of the deciding instance with NTP-level skew assumed (≤ 100 ms); bids and closes serialize on the auction, so a skewed instance can at worst accept or refuse a bid within that skew of the end; no client time is ever used (AS-17).
- A very long auction (30 days) with the job store restarted: the sweeper recovers due opens and closes (AS-31).
- Duplicate, reordered and late events: AS-34, AS-35, AS-39.

## Requirements *(mandatory)*

### Functional Requirements

**Auction definition and lifecycle**

- **FR-001**: An auction belongs to one shop and offers exactly one unit of one product, in the product's currency, with a title (1–140 characters), `startingPriceMinor`, `minIncrementMinor` and an optional `reservePriceMinor` (all integers from 1 to `1,000,000,000,000`, reserve ≥ starting price), and a half-open bidding window `[startsAt, endsAt)` with `startsAt ≥ now + 1 min` and a length of 10 minutes to 30 days (AS-01, AS-02).
- **FR-002**: Scheduling reads the product through the catalog's exported read service scoped to the shop (a product of another shop is `404 product_not_found`), refuses non-sellable products (`product_unavailable`) and zero stock (`insufficient_stock`), and fixes title, prices and currency; an auction is immutable except for its status and the state bids change (AS-02).
- **FR-003**: Seller routes require `ShopScoped` with `products.write` (create, cancel) or `products.read` (list, read) and the plan entitlement `auctions` on create; every query puts the shop in its predicate; another shop's auction is `404 auction_not_found` identical to an unknown ID (AS-03, AS-07).
- **FR-004**: `POST /shops/:shopId/auctions` requires an `Idempotency-Key`; replay, in-flight, different-body and key-misuse rules are those of the platform (V.6) (AS-04).
- **FR-005**: At most one non-terminal auction exists per product, enforced by the store so concurrent creates yield exactly one (AS-05).
- **FR-006**: Status is one of `SCHEDULED`, `OPEN`, `CLOSED` (awarded, waiting for payment), `SECOND_CHANCE` (offered to the runner-up, waiting for payment), `SOLD`, `UNSOLD`, `CANCELLED`; each has a `statusReason` where applicable: `UNSOLD`: `no_bids`, `reserve_not_met`, `leader_ineligible`, `winner_unpaid`, `second_chance_unpaid`; `CANCELLED`: `seller_cancelled`, `stock_hold_failed`. Bidding is open iff status is `OPEN` and `startsAt ≤ now < endsAt` by the injected clock; a bid that arrives at or after `startsAt` for a `SCHEDULED` auction whose unit is held performs `SCHEDULED → OPEN` in the same atomic step (history row, `auction.opened`), so the open job's timing never refuses a bid (AS-17, AS-35, AS-54).
- **FR-007**: Allowed transitions, each a conditional update asserting one affected row plus a history entry in the same transaction: `SCHEDULED → OPEN`, `SCHEDULED → CANCELLED`, `OPEN → CLOSED`, `OPEN → UNSOLD`, `OPEN → CANCELLED` (only with no bids), `CLOSED → SOLD`, `CLOSED → SECOND_CHANCE`, `CLOSED → UNSOLD`, `SECOND_CHANCE → SOLD`, `SECOND_CHANCE → UNSOLD`; every other pair is illegal (`409` on routes, a logged no-op for jobs and consumers). `SOLD`, `UNSOLD` and `CANCELLED` are terminal (AS-08, AS-28, AS-54).
- **FR-008**: Cancel is allowed while `SCHEDULED`, or `OPEN` with zero bids; with bids it is `409 auction_has_bids`; in other statuses `409 auction_not_cancellable`; repeating a cancel of a `CANCELLED` auction is `200` with no change; a cancel and the first bid race to exactly one winner (AS-08, AS-09).
- **FR-009**: Lists use keyset pagination with an opaque cursor over `(startsAt DESC, id DESC)` (seller list) and `sequence DESC` (bid history); `limit` 1–100, default 20; a malformed cursor is `400 invalid_cursor` (AS-07, AS-46).

**Unit hold**

- **FR-010**: The auctioned unit is held through the catalog's exported stock command with operation `auctions:<auctionId>:hold` (delta `−1`, reason `auction.hold`) and returned with `auctions:<auctionId>:return` (delta `+1`, reason `auction.return`) when the auction becomes `UNSOLD` or `CANCELLED` after a hold; both operations are idempotent and driven by durable intent (a job written in the transition's transaction), never from inside a database transaction; the return happens at most once and never for `SOLD` (AS-01, AS-06, AS-08, AS-29, AS-37).
- **FR-011**: An auction opens only when its unit is held; a hold not completed within 5 minutes of creation cancels the auction with `stock_hold_failed` (AS-06).

**Bidding (proxy bids)**

- **FR-012**: `POST /auctions/:auctionId/bids` requires authentication, an `Idempotency-Key` and `{maxAmountMinor}` (integer, 1 to `1,000,000,000,000`, no other property). It is applied to the auction's durable state as one atomic step: the bid is accepted, recorded and acknowledged only if it was committed (AS-10, AS-16, AS-22).
- **FR-013**: Resolution rule (pure): with no leader, a maximum ≥ the starting price makes the bidder leader at the starting price; with a leader other than the bidder, a maximum must be ≥ `price + increment` (`422 bid_too_low {minimumMaxAmountMinor}` otherwise); a maximum above the leader's makes the bidder leader at `min(max, leaderMax + increment)`; a maximum equal to the leader's leaves the leader (earlier bid wins) at price `leaderMax`; a lower one leaves the leader at `min(leaderMax, max + increment)`; the leader raising their own maximum (strictly above the current one) changes neither price nor end (`raised`); equal or lower is `422 bid_not_increasing` (AS-11–AS-15, AS-53).
- **FR-014**: When the leader's maximum is at or above the reserve the price is at least the reserve (`max(price, reserve)`); `reserveMet` is exactly "price ≥ reserve" (always false with no reserve; `hasReserve` tells which); the reserve amount is shown only to the selling shop's members (AS-13).
- **FR-015**: Bids on one auction are applied strictly one after another in a single order; each accepted bid gets a unique, gapless `sequence` starting at 1, and the auction's `version` rises with it; the visible price never decreases; the outcome of each bid is decided only by the state produced by the bids before it (AS-19, AS-20).
- **FR-016**: Time: bids are accepted iff `startsAt ≤ now < endsAt` with the server's injected clock; no client time is read; responses carry `serverTime` (P0615) (AS-17).
- **FR-017**: A bid by a user who is a member of the selling shop (any role) is `403 shill_bid_forbidden`; when the membership lookup fails or times out, the bid is `503 bid_unavailable` (fail closed). The lookup is made through the tenancy capability's exported service (AS-40, AS-41).
- **FR-018**: A bidder is rate-limited by policy `auction.bid` (10 per 10 s, token bucket, fail closed → `429 rate_limited` with `Retry-After`, or `503 bid_unavailable` when the limiter store is down) (AS-21).
- **FR-019**: Bid replay is exactly-once per `(bidder, Idempotency-Key)`: a replay returns the stored status and body; a concurrent duplicate is `409 idempotency_in_flight`; a changed body is `422 idempotency_key_reuse`; the stored response is never returned to another user (AS-18).
- **FR-020**: Responses give the bidder: `outcome` (`leading|outbid|raised`), `priceMinor`, `youAreLeading`, `leaderAlias`, `endsAt`, `version`, `serverTime`, `minimumNextMaxMinor`, `reserveMet`, `hasReserve`; never another user's identity or maximum (AS-10, AS-43).
- **FR-021**: A bid that is rejected (`bid_too_low`, `bid_not_increasing`, `auction_closed`, `auction_not_started`, shill, rate limit, validation) changes nothing durable: no bid row, no event, no version change (AS-15, AS-16, AS-40).
- **FR-022**: Every acknowledged bid is durable independent of any cache, limiter or realtime store; losing all of them loses no bid and changes no decision (AS-22).

**Anti-sniping**

- **FR-023**: A price-changing bid (`leading` or `outbid`) accepted when `endsAt − now ≤ 2 min` sets `endsAt = min(originalEndsAt + 60 min, max(endsAt, now + 2 min))`; `raised` bids and rejected bids never extend; the new end is in the response, the stored auction, the realtime `price` message and the `auction.bid_placed` event (`extended`), and the close trigger follows it (AS-24–AS-26).
- **FR-024**: The window (2 min), the extension (2 min) and the cap (60 min) are configuration with these defaults, validated at startup (AS-24, AS-25).

**Close, settlement and second chance**

- **FR-025**: Closing is a conditional transition `OPEN → CLOSED | UNSOLD` taken only when `now ≥ endsAt` (end as currently stored), serialized with bids, asserting one affected row, with its history row, `auction.closed` event, the follow-up jobs (winner order request, stock return) and the realtime message trigger written in the same transaction; it is safe to run any number of times, concurrently, or early (AS-26–AS-28, AS-32).
- **FR-026**: Outcome: `CLOSED` iff there is a leader and `reserveMet` and the leader is not a member of the selling shop at that moment; else `UNSOLD` with reason `no_bids`, `reserve_not_met` or `leader_ineligible`; the winner pays the shown price, never above their maximum and never below the reserve (AS-27, AS-29, AS-30, AS-42).
- **FR-027**: Closing is triggered by a delayed job per end time (re-armed whenever the end moves, one job per `(auction, endsAt)`), with a due-auction sweeper (every 30 s, single-run) as a safety net; the close lag (close time − end) is normally under 5 s and under 60 s after a lost job (AS-26, AS-31).
- **FR-028**: After a `CLOSED` transition the winner's order is requested from the order capability after the transaction commits, exactly once per `(auction, step)` with idempotency key `auction:<id>:winner`, price = final price, quantity 1, `reservedUntil = closedAt + 48 h` (configurable), stock handling `EXTERNALLY_HELD`; retries use exponential backoff with full jitter, at most 8 attempts; a permanent rejection is handled as an unpaid winner (AS-27, AS-33).
- **FR-029**: Reactions to the order capability's events (`order.paid`, `order.cancelled` on `orders.events`, keyed by order ID): inbox-deduplicated, zod-validated, dead-lettered when invalid, version-guarded, and resolved by looking up the order ID in this capability's own records; an event for an unknown order is ignored; a late or out-of-order event never moves a terminal or later state backwards, and a late payment is reported as an anomaly (AS-34, AS-39).
- **FR-030**: When the winner's order is cancelled or lapses (or cannot be created), the unit is offered **once** to the runner-up: the highest eligible maximum among bidders other than the winner, eligible iff maximum ≥ reserve (when set) and not a member of the selling shop (ties to the earlier bid), at that maximum, with an order requested under key `auction:<id>:second-chance` and a payment window of 24 h (configurable); no eligible runner-up → `UNSOLD(winner_unpaid)` (AS-36, AS-38).
- **FR-031**: The offer's payment moves the auction to `SOLD`; its cancellation or lapse moves it to `UNSOLD(second_chance_unpaid)` and returns the unit; there is no further offer (AS-37).
- **FR-032**: Jobs and consumers are safe to run twice, concurrently or in any order; scheduled, single-run work uses leases (VIII.6) (AS-28, AS-31, AS-35).

**Public reads, events, observability, isolation**

- **FR-033**: `GET /auctions/:auctionId` and `GET /auctions?productIds=` are anonymous, read the durable record through a shared cache (1 s, single-flight), expose only public fields, and fall back to the durable record when the cache is down (AS-43–AS-45).
- **FR-034**: Bidders are shown as stable per-auction aliases; no user ID, maximum or reserve amount is exposed to any client except: the bidder's own maximum on `my-bid`, and the reserve amount to the selling shop's members (AS-43, AS-46, AS-47).
- **FR-035**: Price changes and the close are pushed to the topic `auction:<auctionId>` after commit with a `version`; a publish failure never fails or rolls back a bid or a close (AS-22, AS-48).
- **FR-036**: Lifecycle changes publish `auction.created`, `auction.opened`, `auction.bid_placed`, `auction.leader_changed`, `auction.closed`, `auction.second_chance_offered`, `auction.sold`, `auction.unsold`, `auction.cancelled` through the outbox in the transition's transaction, keyed by `auctionId`, each with `eventId`, `type`, `version`, `occurredAt`, aggregate ID and `auctionVersion` (strictly increasing per auction); `auction.leader_changed` is written for every actual leader change (not batched) (AS-49).
- **FR-037**: The bid log is append-only and monthly-partitioned; partitions for the current and next 3 months are created by a daily single-run job; a missing partition never fails a bid (AS-50).
- **FR-038**: Metrics, logs, spans and alerts of AS-51; delays, limits and rates are configuration with this spec's defaults, validated at startup: sweeper 30 s, hold deadline 5 min, window/extension/cap, winner window 48 h, offer window 24 h, public cache 1 s, order-request attempts 8, max amount `1,000,000,000,000`, partition horizon 3 months.
- **FR-039**: This capability reads or writes no table and no model of another domain: product facts through R1 `ProductQueryService.getProductsByIds(ids, {shopId})`, stock only through R1 `ProductStockService.applyStockDelta`, membership through R1 `ShopAccessService.getRole`, the plan entitlement through R1 `EntitlementsService` (route guard), the winner's and runner-up's orders through S10's command, and order outcomes through S10's events; it has no foreign key to another domain's table and exports no model, repository, job class or fast-store class (AS-52).
- **FR-040**: Mandatory API cases of VII.3 hold for every route as the scenarios above: `401` (AS-03, AS-16, AS-47), validation classes (AS-02, AS-16), IDOR `404` (AS-07, AS-08), idempotency (AS-04, AS-18), rate limit `429` (AS-21), illegal transition `409` (AS-08, AS-17), concurrency (AS-05, AS-09, AS-19, AS-20, AS-28, AS-32); VII.4: every job and consumer has a duplicate-delivery test and an invalid-payload test (AS-28, AS-34, AS-35, AS-39).

### Key Entities

- **Auction**: shop ID, product ID (plain IDs, no foreign key), title, currency, starting price, minimum increment, optional reserve, bidding window, original end, current end, status and reason, price, leader and leader's maximum, bid count, version, unit-held marker, winner (current awardee), final price, order ID of the current settlement order, close time. Owned by `auctions` (`Auction`).
- **Bid**: auction ID, bidder ID, maximum, outcome (`leading`/`outbid`/`raised`), price after, sequence, time; append-only, monthly partitions. Owned by `auctions` (`Bid`).
- **Auction history**: append-only record of each status transition: from, to, reason, time.
- **Bidder alias**: a stable pseudonym of a bidder within one auction.
- **Settlement order**: an order held by the order capability for the winner or the runner-up; this capability stores only its ID and reacts to its events.

## Success Criteria *(mandatory)*

### Measurable Outcomes

- **SC-001**: With 200 bidders bidding at once on one auction, the final leader is the highest maximum and the final price is second-highest maximum plus one increment (capped by the leader's maximum) in 100% of 50 repeated runs, with gapless bid sequences and 0 lost or doubled bids.
- **SC-002**: At 100 bids per second on one auction, 95% of bids are acknowledged in under 300 ms and 99% in under 800 ms; at 1,000 viewers refreshing the same auction, 99% of views return in under 150 ms.
- **SC-003**: Under any repeat, reorder or concurrency of the close, order-request, stock-return and settlement steps, each auction closes once, requests each order once and returns its unit at most once, and every unit is accounted for (`held = sold + returned`) in 100% of test runs.
- **SC-004**: 99% of auctions close within 5 seconds after their end, and 100% within 60 seconds even when the close trigger was lost.
- **SC-005**: After a total loss of the cache, limiter and realtime data, 0 acknowledged bids are lost and the next bids are decided correctly, in 100% of runs.
- **SC-006**: After any accepted price-changing bid, either at least 2 minutes remain or the end is at its cap, in 100% of runs; total extension never exceeds 60 minutes.
- **SC-007**: 0 auctions are awarded below their reserve price or to a member of the selling shop.
- **SC-008**: 100% of attempts to read, list or cancel another shop's auction return "not found" with identical bodies and change nothing; 0 responses to any client contain another bidder's identity, another bidder's maximum, or the reserve amount (outside the selling shop).
- **SC-009**: A retried bid or create request (same key) never produces a second bid or auction: 0 duplicates in 1,000 randomized retries.
- **SC-010**: The ownership check reports 0 cross-domain accesses for the auctions code (today 5: 3 raw SQL on `Product`/`BisOrderItem`/`ShopOrder`, 1 raw `Product` in create, 1 model injection).
- **SC-011**: 1,000 concurrent viewers of one auction page cause at most 1 durable read per second for that auction and see data at most 2 seconds old.

## Assumptions

- Decisions marked `[BREAKING]`, `[CONTRACT]`, `[LOCAL]` are listed one per line in `questions.md`; this spec adopts each default stated there. Most visible: the durable store decides every bid (the fast-store/write-behind design is dropped); bid and create require `Idempotency-Key`; `startsAt` is enforced; the reserve raises the price when a maximum covers it; the winner's order is requested after commit through a command, never written by this capability; second chance is event-driven; money fields are `…Minor`; user IDs and maxima are never public.
- **Pattern coverage**: P0110 (discriminated unions + `assertNever` state machines) → FR-006, FR-007, AS-54; P0311 (lost-update prevention: conditional updates, serialized per-auction decisions, idempotent transitions) → FR-007, FR-013, FR-015, FR-025, AS-09, AS-19, AS-20, AS-28, AS-32; P0318 (table partitioning: bids by month, partitions ahead) → FR-037, AS-46, AS-50; P0414 (idempotency keys: replay, in-flight 409, different-body 422, TTL) → FR-004, FR-019, AS-04, AS-18; P0607 (per-key ordering: events keyed by `auctionId`, strictly increasing `auctionVersion`, per-auction bid sequence) → FR-015, FR-036, AS-19, AS-48, AS-49; P0615 (server time authority) → FR-016, FR-023, AS-17, AS-24, AS-25.
- Hot auctions are served by per-auction serialization on the durable record (note §22: row-level locking is fine at hundreds of bids per second; reads are served from cache); a queue partitioned by auction ID is a later option if one auction must exceed that rate, with no change to the contract.
- "The fast store" is the shared Redis used for the public-view cache, the rate limiter and the realtime fan-out; it holds nothing that decides a bid (III.9).
- Idempotency keys are retained 24 hours (platform default); a key older than that is a new request, and a duplicate bid beyond the window is a normal new bid subject to `bid_not_increasing`.
- Auctions are one unit; the currency is the product's currency; bids are in that currency; there is no tax, buyer's premium or shipping in the price.
- The anti-sniping window, extension, cap, windows for payment (winner 48 h, offer 24 h), sweeper (30 s), hold deadline (5 min), minimum lead (1 min), duration limits (10 min–30 days), maximum amount, and the 3-month partition horizon are configuration defaults of this spec.
- The close tolerates NTP-level clock skew between instances (≤ 100 ms); bids and closes serialize on the auction, so skew cannot produce two outcomes.
- Bid rows and auction rows are financial records and are retained for 24 months at least (disputes); archival or detaching of old partitions is out of scope.
- Realtime push is best-effort; the durable record is truth and clients may refresh at any time.
- Notification content and delivery are S28's; this capability only emits the events.
