# Questions and decisions: S17 — Subscriptions

Decisions taken unattended under the decision policy (production-grade, notes and constitution first). BREAKING first, then CONTRACT, then LOCAL. Format: `question → default → why`. Source-note gap: `docs/showcase/sections/SD-24-subscriptions-billing.md` is not in this checkout; design 24 of `10-System-Design/07-commerce-and-transactions.md` and the code were used.

## BREAKING

- [BREAKING] Controllers return Sequelize models (`billing.controller.ts:20-21, 28, 69`, `billing.service.ts:35-37, 120-126`), including `paymentMethodRef` → explicit DTOs with `…Minor` money names, `hasPaymentMethod` instead of the reference, `packages/contracts` schemas (`planCatalogSchema`, `subscriptionSchema`, `invoiceSchema`, …) → V.1, V.2, III.8; today a payment reference leaves the API.
- [BREAKING] Subscribe and change take no `Idempotency-Key` (`billing.controller.ts:31-50`) → required on subscribe and change (replay / in-flight 409 / different body 422) → V.6: both move money; today a client retry can race the unique index or double-apply a change.
- [BREAKING] `SubscribeDto.trialDays` (0-30, client chosen, `billing.dto.ts:8`) → removed; the trial length is a `Price` property, once per subject, payment method required → a client could grant itself a 30-day trial on every re-subscribe.
- [BREAKING] Subscribe without a payment method creates an `ACTIVE` subscription whose first charge fails with "no payment method" (`billing.jobs.ts:125-127`) → `422 payment_method_required` unless the price is 0 → never grant a plan that cannot be collected.
- [BREAKING] Dunning delays are applied from the time of each failure (`DUNNING_DAYS[attempt-1]` from `Date.now()`, `billing.jobs.ts:136-137`, so 1, 4 and 11 days cumulative) while the comment and note 10/07 §24 say day 1/3/7 → retries at `firstFailedAt + 1/3/7 days` regardless of when attempts ran → the note wins; the schedule becomes a pure function.
- [BREAKING] Net credit from a downgrade is an `OPEN` negative `PRORATION` invoice, and a renewal marks all such invoices `PAID` wholesale (`billing.jobs.ts:87, 112-115`), losing any credit above the renewal total → a `creditBalanceMinor` on the subscription, applied up to the invoice's positive subtotal, never negative (check constraint) → III.6/III.8: money must not vanish.
- [BREAKING] Invoice uniqueness is `(subscriptionId, periodStart, kind)` with `findOrCreate` returning `null` when it exists (`billing.service.ts:140-145`, migration `…190000…js:69`): two changes at the same instant keep the second change but silently create no invoice → business key per invoice (`renewal:<periodStart>`, `proration:v<version>`) unique per subscription; a duplicate trigger returns the existing invoice.
- [BREAKING] Proration rounds each line independently on float math, with `Math.round(ms/86400000)` days (`proration.ts:27-33`, `periods.ts:20`): 1000→2000 at 10/30 days gives 334 (`-333`, `+667`), and a change at exactly half a day rounds up → net rounded once (333), lines split by largest remainder, integer arithmetic, whole UTC calendar days (`date(end) − date(changeAt)`) → P0103/P1110/P0104; the customer pays the single-rounded net.
- [BREAKING] A subscription in `UNPAID` is still "live" (`Subscription_one_live` excludes only `CANCELED`) with no exit: the customer can neither subscribe again nor recover → `UNPAID → ACTIVE` when the owed invoice is paid (payment-method update gives one immediate attempt), `UNPAID → CANCELED` after 30 days or on request → notes: dunning "eventually cancels or downgrades".
- [BREAKING] `success` on any invoice returns a `PAST_DUE` subscription to `ACTIVE` (`billing.jobs.ts:131`) → only when no invoice remains in dunning (a paid renewal must not hide a failing proration).
- [BREAKING] Charge job reads `attempts`, calls the provider and writes back without claiming the attempt (`billing.jobs.ts:124-130`); unknown outcomes throw and rely on job retry (`:134`) → the attempt is claimed by a conditional update first; unknown outcomes are settled by lookup by reference with a 60-minute limit and never re-sent as a new attempt (S13 AS-23 to AS-28 pattern) → V.6/III.6; two deliveries must not both act.
- [BREAKING] The provider client hard-codes `currency: 'usd'` and sends only `metadata.idempotencyKey` (`stripe.service.ts:99-105`) while invoices are EUR; `is_load_test` makes it answer "succeeded" without calling the provider (`:87-95`) → the charge takes the invoice currency and metadata `{kind, invoiceId, subscriptionId, attempt}`; the load-test shortcut must not exist in a production code path → money correctness; a billing charge in the wrong currency is a defect.
- [BREAKING] Cancel answers `204` and acts on the subscription ID without the subject in the predicate (`billing.service.ts:115-118`); only "at period end" exists → `200` with the DTO, body `{immediately?}`, `resume` added, `409` on illegal calls → III.4, notes (`cancel_at_period_end`), AS-71 to AS-74.
- [BREAKING] `GET /shops/:id/invoices` returns the last 24 invoices of the **live** subscription and `404`s after cancellation (`billing.service.ts:124-126`, `billing.controller.ts:59-63`) → keyset pagination (default 20, max 100) over the subject's invoices of every subscription; detail routes; buyer invoices route → III.10, customers must read their history after leaving.
- [BREAKING] `GET /plans` returns `Price` rows with an embedded `Plan` (bigint amounts, internal fields) → `{plans: [{…, prices: [...]}]}` with `…Minor` fields and a deterministic order → V.1/V.2.
- [BREAKING] `Price` is "immutable once used" only by a comment (`price.model.ts:5`) → the store rejects any change to commercial fields or re-activation; adds `version` and `trialDays` → III.6: an invariant that must never break is enforced by the store.
- [BREAKING] Subscriptions, status moves and invoice status moves have no history; `setStatus` updates a possibly stale instance without a status condition (`billing.jobs.ts:157-161`); the trial path of `change` updates unguarded (`billing.service.ts:93-97`) → conditional updates plus history tables (`SubscriptionTransition`, `InvoiceTransition`) in the same transaction → III.7.
- [BREAKING] Events are recorded with separate statements and the cache is invalidated inside the transaction before commit (`billing.service.ts:69, 111`, `billing.jobs.ts:138-147, 159-160`); jobs are enqueued without the transaction (`billing.service.ts:147`) → outbox rows and the charge request written in the transaction; cache invalidation after commit → III.3, IV.4 (a charge job must never run before the invoice commits).
- [BREAKING] `billing.invoice_payment_failed` and `billing.subscription_status_changed` payloads (`billing-events.ts`) lack `from`, `planCode`, `subscriptionVersion`, `reason`, amounts → additive fields; plus new `billing.invoice_paid` and `billing.subscription_plan_changed` → IV.4/V.7 additive within v1.
- [BREAKING] Trial anchor: the anchor day is the subscribe day (`billing.service.ts:45`), so a trial ending Feb 4 renews on the 5th → the anchor day is the UTC day on which the first paid period starts.
- [BREAKING] `index.ts` exports the five models and `UsageProjector`, `BillingWorkerModule` (`index.ts:7-11, 14-15`) → models leave the barrel (no external importer found in `packages/backend`; verify with `check:boundaries`); the projector belongs to S18; apps import the worker module → debt D-7, D-8, X.4.
- [BREAKING] `billing.e2e-spec.ts` calls services directly (`:71, 75, 84, 96, 107, 119`) and spies on `UsageService` (`:62-63`) → the e2e suite is rewritten over HTTP (VII.2) with the S18 provider as the only fake → VII.2/VII.3.
- [BREAKING] Plan seeds: `starter` seats 2 and `pro` seats 20, no `enterprise` (`…190000…js:82-83`) → seed `starter` 5, `pro` 25 and allow an operator-created `enterprise` (250) so the plan entitlement and S03's seat limits (5/25/250) agree → S03 FR-006.

## CONTRACT

- [CONTRACT] S03 expects `billing.subscription_plan_changed v1 {shopId, plan: 'STARTER'|'PRO'|'ENTERPRISE', version}` with a per-shop monotonic `version` (S03 questions line 40) → emitted exactly so, shops only, on effective-tier changes (`STARTER` for no live subscription, `UNPAID`, `CANCELED`); `version` from a per-shop counter that survives across subscriptions; shop plan codes limited to `starter|pro|enterprise` → S03 dead-letters other values and needs versions for out-of-order protection (P0610); no rename needed.
- [CONTRACT] S13 says "billing is a separate capability that sends a charge command" (S13 spec line 33) and the domain map says billing depends on payments (charge command), but S13 has no such command and its intents are bound to reserved orders → billing charges through its own port and an adapter over the `stripe` infrastructure lib (anti-corruption, IV.8); the provider reference is `<invoiceId>:<attempt>` with metadata `kind: "subscription_invoice"` → no change to S13; the domain map line "payments (charge command)" should read "provider via infrastructure".
- [CONTRACT] S10's order webhook must ignore payment intents with metadata `kind: "subscription_invoice"` (acknowledge, no effect) → S10 owns the webhook route today (`orders/api/stripe-webhook.controller.ts:54-55`, which maps intents by `metadata.idempotencyKey`) and subscription intents arrive on the same provider account.
- [CONTRACT] S18 (usage and entitlements, not yet written) must provide `UsageInvoiceLinesProvider.linesFor(...)` (≤ 5 s, throws on failure), `EntitlementsService.get/invalidate`, and consume `billing.subscription_status_changed` with `subscriptionVersion` ordering → renewal needs usage lines before its transaction; S17 supplies `SubscriptionBasisService.getLiveBasis` / `getLiveBasisBySubjectIds` for S18's cache load, replacing S18's raw join over `Subscription`/`Price`/`Plan`.
- [CONTRACT] S28 consumes `billing.invoice_payment_failed` (attempt 1 to 4, `nextAttemptAt null` = access withdrawn, `reason`) and `billing.invoice_paid` for mails; recipients are S28's choice (owners via S03 `getMembersByShopIds`) → S17 sends no mail.
- [CONTRACT] S14: subscription revenue is not posted to the ledger in this version; `billing.invoice_paid` is the hook if finance wants it (S14 line 30 says "Subscription charges → S17") → keeps S17 free of a second money store; the invoice is the revenue record.
- [CONTRACT] S03's `ShopQueryService.getShopsByIds` is used for the sandbox flag only; S03 lists S17 as a consumer already → one batch R1 call before the subscribe transaction.
- [CONTRACT] S03 shop lifecycle: S17 reacts to `shop_offboarding_started` (cancel at period end), `shop_offboarding_cancelled`, `shop_deleted` (cancel now, erase payment reference, keep invoices) and contributes no separate export artifact (invoices stay readable by `billing.manage` during offboarding) → S03's "every shop-owning domain" rule; retention of financial records overrides purge.
- [CONTRACT] No web capability owns a billing/plans screen (W04 lists dashboard, inventory and developer settings only) → the API is complete for a screen; UI journeys are none in this wave and the happy path is covered by J02's API journey; a later W04 amendment may add the screen.
- [CONTRACT] Rate-limit policy names and the job names go into S50's and S49's registries → `billing.*` as listed in the spec.

## LOCAL

- [LOCAL] Free-tier fallback seat limit → unchanged: S18 owns FREE_SHOP; tenancy keeps its own label `STARTER`.
- [LOCAL] Preview is not a binding quote → the change charges the instant it is made; the response shows the lines actually charged.
- [LOCAL] Usage provider down → renewal deferred, not invoiced without usage → late usage would be an S18 adjustment anyway; deferral is safer and observable.
- [LOCAL] Catch-up after downtime → one period per run (10 minutes apart) → simplicity; each period still gets one invoice.
- [LOCAL] Immediate cancel keeps credit, does not refund → refunds are S13's executor path and out of scope.
- [LOCAL] Immediate-retry attempts use key `<invoiceId>:manual:<k>` and do not count in the 4 numbered attempts → the customer fixing a card must not shorten the schedule.
- [LOCAL] `UNPAID` expiry 30 days → fixed, not configurable.
- [LOCAL] Provider answers that cannot be classified are "unknown" → never success, never decline.
- [LOCAL] Price version counter is per (plan, interval) → the DTO shows `version`; no semantics beyond ordering.
- [LOCAL] Entitlement key allowlist: `freeShipping`, `earlyAccessDrops`, `maxProducts`, `seats`, `auctions`, `apiCallsPerMonth`, `assistantTokensPerMonth` → matches the type today; S18 may extend.
- [LOCAL] `trialDays` max 90, `unitAmountMinor` max 100,000,000, `quantity` max 1000 → bounds keep every amount inside safe integer range.
- [LOCAL] Operator tools (void, refund, force status) → not in this version.
- [LOCAL] Offboarding `cancelled` ordering uses envelope `occurredAt` against the stored `started` time → events are keyed by shop, so reordering is only possible on replay.
- [LOCAL] Tax invoice numbering, VAT, discounts, interval switching mid-period, price migration of subscribers → out of scope.
