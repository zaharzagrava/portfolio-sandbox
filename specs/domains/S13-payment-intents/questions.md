# Open Decisions for S13 (answered unattended)

Format: `[TAG] question → default taken → why`. BREAKING first, then CONTRACT, then LOCAL. The human reviews BREAKING and CONTRACT lines first. Decision policy: most production-grade option the Interview-Prep notes and the constitution support; no external clients to keep compatible, so correctness, security, observability and clean boundaries beat today's behaviour.

## BREAKING (changes behaviour or an API/UI contract that exists today)

- [BREAKING] Payments are requested by the edge gateway writing a message straight to the `payments.requests` topic with a client-supplied `idempotency_key`, `amount`, `bisOrderId`, `productId`, `quantity` (`packages/edge-be/src/index.ts:425-470`, `payment.controller.ts:24`) → `POST /payments/intents` with a mandatory `Idempotency-Key` header and body `{orderId, paymentMethodId}`, answer `202` + `Location`; amount and currency come from the order copy; the gateway's Kafka route is removed → V.6, V.1, pattern P0407, note 10/07 §19 ("intent with idempotency key = order ID"); a client-chosen amount is a price-tampering hole.
- [BREAKING] The payment handler also decrements `Product.quantity` by raw SQL and refunds when stock ran out (`payment.service.ts:133-152,187-210,311-343`) → payments never touches stock; stock is reserved by S10 before payment and released by S10 on failure; the "refund because stock ran out" path disappears → IX.4, III.6, constitution X boundaries, note 10/07 §19 (reservation holds stock, payment converts it); D-12.
- [BREAKING] Results are published on `payments.responses` with an untyped `{payload, extra: {payment}, error}` shape and consumed by orders' `OrderPaymentListener` → typed events `payments.payment_succeeded | payment_failed | payment_refunded` v1 on `payments.events` keyed by `paymentId`; `payments.responses` and `payments.dlq` for this flow are retired → IV.4, IV.5, S10's `questions.md` (consumer of `payments.events`).
- [BREAKING] Payment is keyed by the client's `idempotencyKey` (unique column, `payment.service.ts:88-104`) and `idempotency_key` doubles as the provider's key → one payment per order (unique order reference); the provider's key is the order ID (refund `refund:<paymentId>`, cancel `cancel:<paymentId>`); the HTTP key is a separate, per-buyer, 24 h facility; the `idempotencyKey` column is dropped by expand/contract → note 10/07 §19 and S10's contract (intent key = `orderId`); a client-chosen provider key lets a client replay or collide.
- [BREAKING] An unknown outcome is settled by redelivering the message, which re-sends the charge with the same provider key (`payment.service.ts:122-126`), and the resolver gives up as `FAILED` after 60 min since creation (`payment-resolution.jobs.ts:56`) → never re-send; look up by our reference (`orderId` metadata) with exponential backoff and full jitter; `FAILED(no_provider_record)` only when the provider confirms absence for ≥ 60 min since the attempt began; stuck > 24 h stays `UNKNOWN` and alerts; a crash after the attempt was recorded becomes `UNKNOWN(crash_recovery)` → note 10/07 §20 ("never blindly resend"), SD-20 "never blind resend"; the provider's key lifetime (24 h) makes a late resend a double-charge risk.
- [BREAKING] The circuit breaker is opossum around create only, counting every thrown error, timeout 15 s, no minimum volume, no per-operation separation, declines counted (`stripe.service.ts:33-37,62-76`) → one breaker per operation (`create_intent`, `retrieve_intent`, `cancel_intent`, `refund`), failures = timeout/connection/5xx/429/slow (> 5 s), ≥ 50% of ≥ 10 calls in 10 s, 30 s open, single probe; call limits 8 s / 4 s / 2 s; declines and `4xx` do not count; an open breaker is "not sent" (stays `PENDING`, retried, never `UNKNOWN`) → note 06/03 §1–§3 ("minimum request volume, failure rate, slow-call counting"; "per dependency and sometimes per endpoint").
- [BREAKING] A provider answer other than `succeeded` is treated as a failure, so "requires customer action" becomes `FAILED` and may throw `Fatal_InternalServerError` when `last_payment_error` is absent (`payment.service.ts:185,276-287`; `confirm: true` with redirects disabled, `stripe.service.ts:99-104`) → `requires_action` keeps the payment `PENDING` with `requiresAction`, the owner reads a `clientSecret`, the result arrives through the status refresh or the lookup → note 10/07 §19 ("client confirms with the provider (3-D Secure)").
- [BREAKING] Money: `amount` stored as a BIGINT read as `number`, currency fixed to `'usd'` in the provider call (`stripe.service.ts:94,100`) → `amountMinor` + `currency` stored per payment (EUR, USD, GBP), integers from 1 to 99,999,999, response and event fields suffixed `Minor` → III.8; S10 prices in EUR.
- [BREAKING] Payment statuses: `REFUNDED` meant "refunded because stock ran out"; `CANCELLED` unused → seven statuses with `REFUND_PENDING` between `COMPLETED` and `REFUNDED`; `CANCELLED` means "cancelled before money moved" → III.7; refund is a two-step saga that must survive provider timeouts.
- [BREAKING] Status changes are `UPDATE … WHERE status IN (…)` with no version and no history (`payment.service.ts:214-223`, `payment-dto.service.ts:147-171`, `payment-resolution.jobs.ts:62-65`) → guarded by status and version, +1 version and one history row per move, in the same transaction as ledger and outbox → III.7, pattern P0311 (OCC), VII.3.
- [BREAKING] Routes `GET /payment/by-key/:idempotencyKey`, `GET /payment/:id`, `GET /payment` (unpaginated list, `apps/core/src/payment-query/payment-query.controller.ts`) and the payment SSE stream `GET /payment/stream?idempotencyKey=` (`apps/sse-gateway/src/payment-stream/payment-stream.controller.ts`) → `GET /payments/:paymentId`, `GET /payments?orderId&status&limit&cursor` (keyset), and the push event `payment.status` on `user:<userId>`; `by-key` is removed (clients get `paymentId` in the `202`) → III.10, V.5, S51; the controllers and services move out of `apps/` into the domain (I.5, X.1).
- [BREAKING] `Payment` has an association and foreign key to the order model (`payment.model.ts:120-129`), and `BisOrder` has `HasMany` payments (`orders/infra/models/bis-order.model.ts:75`); `PaymentModel`, `PaymentStatus`, `PaymentDtoService`, `PaymentDtoModule` are exported to other domains (`index.ts:8,14,21`) → order and user references are plain ID columns (`bisOrderId` renamed `orderId` by expand/contract), no association, none of these leave the entry point; orders' webhook controller, module and checkout spec stop injecting `PaymentModel` → IX.4, D-7, D-11, D-17.
- [BREAKING] The existing spec drives `PaymentService.executePayment` directly and seeds `Payment` through `PaymentModel` (`payment.e2e-spec.ts:57`, `test/seeds/*`) → HTTP e2e through the real module and processor; seeds go through the domain's fixture helper (test-only, IX.6) → VII.2.

## CONTRACT (decides something another capability must provide or consume)

- [CONTRACT] S10's spec says S13 creates an intent only for an order that `OrderQueryService.getPayableOrder` returns → S13 does **not** call it: it keeps an order copy (R3) from `order.reserved`, `order.paid`, `order.cancelled` (version-guarded by `orderVersion`), waits up to 2 s for a copy that has not arrived, and otherwise answers `404 order_not_found` (same answer as a foreign order) → S13 calling S10 (R1) and S10 calling S13 (`getPaymentStatus`, R1) is an `orders ↔ payments` module cycle (IV.2, X.5, debt D-11); the domain map says payments "consumes reservation and paid events, never reads orders"; S10 may keep `getPayableOrder` for others but S13 is no longer its consumer. S10 must keep `userId`, `totalMinor`, `currency`, `reservedUntil` and a rising `orderVersion` in `order.reserved`/`order.paid`/`order.cancelled`.
- [CONTRACT] `getPaymentStatus(paymentRef)` maps `UNKNOWN → PENDING`, `REFUND_PENDING → COMPLETED`, `CANCELLED → FAILED` (S10 knows four statuses) and **refreshes** a non-terminal payment from the provider (2 s, once per payment per 2 s, never throws for provider trouble) → S10's webhook asks for confirmation right after the provider's event; the customer-action path has no other trigger; note 04/03 §5 ("re-fetch the object for critical actions"); S10's spec delegates "re-reading the payment at the provider" to this capability.
- [CONTRACT] `paymentRef` is the provider's intent ID, null until the provider answers; `payments.payment_failed` therefore allows `paymentRef: null` and adds `reasonCode`; every event adds `paymentVersion` → additive to S10's required shape `{paymentId, paymentRef, orderId, userId, amountMinor, currency, occurredAt}`; failures before a provider call have no reference.
- [CONTRACT] S13 emits `payment_failed` also for `CANCELLED` payments (reason `order_cancelled` or `order_not_payable`) → S10's `payment_failed` handler cancels only a `RESERVED` order, so it is a no-op for an already cancelled one (S10 AS-48); consumers still see the final result.
- [CONTRACT] `orders.refund_requested` is executed only for `reason: "order_cancelled"`, full amount, matching `paymentRef` and currency; everything else is dead-lettered with a reason metric → no partial refund path exists in S10's contract; a mismatch is a bug to see, not to guess around.
- [CONTRACT] The refund message for a payment that is still `PENDING` with a charge attempt, or `UNKNOWN`, is redelivered with backoff for up to 24 h (not rejected) → S10 may emit it in the cancel-versus-pay race (S10 AS-51) before the payment settles; if the payment fails, the next delivery acknowledges "nothing to refund".
- [CONTRACT] S13 itself reacts to `order.cancelled`: it cancels a payment that has not started charging, and cancels the provider's intent for a payment waiting for the customer; running or unknown payments are left to finish and are refunded through S10's command → hold expiry must not leave a live 3-D Secure intent that can still be completed; saga compensation owned by the payment side.
- [CONTRACT] S14 must export `LedgerService.recordPaymentCaptured({paymentId, userId, amountMinor, currency}, tx)` and `recordPaymentRefunded({paymentId, amountMinor, currency}, tx)`, in-transaction, balanced, idempotent per `paymentId`, returning `{journalId}`; fees and account IDs move inside S14 → replaces today's `recordMarketplaceSale({buyerAccountId, merchantAccountId, platformRevenueAccountId, totalAmount, feeAmount, tx})` call with ledger IDs built by S13 (`payment.service.ts:251-262`, `payment-resolution.jobs.ts:68-77`); the reversal on refund does not exist today (D4 of the domain map: payment and ledger commit together).
- [CONTRACT] S53 supplies the outbox append, the relay, the consumer framework (zod, idempotency, DLQ) and a single-consumer queue for `orders.refund_requested`; S13 consumes `orders.events` in its own consumer group → IV.4, IV.5.
- [CONTRACT] S54's idempotency facility must make the header mandatory, return `Idempotency-Replayed`, scope keys by principal and not remember failures that happened before a record existed (today `libs/infrastructure/idempotency/idempotency.interceptor.ts` makes the header optional, names the header `Idempotent-Replayed`, remembers `4xx` outcomes and uses `400`/`422` differently) → same codes and header as S10 (`idempotency_key_required | invalid | reuse`, `idempotency_in_flight`, `Idempotency-Replayed`).
- [CONTRACT] Realtime: S13 publishes `payment.status {paymentId, orderId, status, version}` to `user:<userId>` through S51's publisher → replaces the payment SSE endpoint; best effort, polling stays the source of truth.
- [CONTRACT] W03/S48: the pay step uses `paymentId` from the `202`, follows `payment.status` or polls, and for `requiresAction` hands `clientSecret` to the provider's client library; orders-with-payments screens compose `GET /payments?orderId=` (R2) → no card data crosses our servers (PCI scope reduction, note 10/07 §20).
- [CONTRACT] Operator refunds (S11 says "operators refund through S13/S14") → no payments endpoint; the order system issues `orders.refund_requested` for operator actions (S10 may add reasons later; the consumer accepts `order_cancelled` only until then) → avoids inventing an admin RBAC surface here; S14's reconciliation reports provider-side differences.
- [CONTRACT] Provider-initiated refunds and disputes, and a provider that contradicts a final status (`payments_conflicting_provider_state_total`, `payments_provider_mismatch_total`) → reconciliation (S14) owns the finding and the fix; S13 only reports.
- [CONTRACT] The provider SDK client in `libs/infrastructure/stripe` stays a thin client (domain map), and the breaker, classification and anti-corruption validation live in a payments port and adapter → X.3, IV.8; S43 and S46 reuse the generic breaker primitive, not the payment logic.

## LOCAL (this capability's internals)

- [LOCAL] Supported currencies EUR, USD, GBP; amount 1…99,999,999 → two-decimal currencies need no unit conversion at the provider.
- [LOCAL] Order-copy wait 2 s, polling inside the request outside any transaction → hides outbox-to-consumer lag without leaking existence.
- [LOCAL] First unknown lookup `+30 s`, backoff ×2 to a 15 min cap with full jitter; sweep every 60 s; 60 min no-record limit; 24 h stuck limit.
- [LOCAL] Charge retry: base 2 s, cap 60 s, ≤ 6 attempts, ≤ 10 min since creation → inside the 15-minute hold.
- [LOCAL] Breaker: 10 s window, ≥ 50% of ≥ 10 calls, 30 s open, one probe, slow call > 5 s; call limits 8 s create/refund, 4 s lookup/cancel, 2 s refresh and connect.
- [LOCAL] Refresh from the provider at most once per payment per 2 s; refund retry cap 15 min, 24 h window.
- [LOCAL] Rate limits `payments.create.user` 10/min fail closed, `payments.read.user` 120/min fail open.
- [LOCAL] `clientSecret` is kept only while `requiresAction` is true and cleared on any final status; it is never logged or published.
- [LOCAL] The payment method token is kept on the payment only until the charge is final, then cleared.
- [LOCAL] Order copy rows are never deleted; one row per order, version-guarded upsert.
- [LOCAL] The cursor for `GET /payments` is opaque over `(createdAt, id)`; `status` filter accepts one status.
- [LOCAL] Failure codes are a closed list (`card_declined`, `insufficient_funds`, `expired_card`, `declined_other`, `provider_rejected`, `provider_unavailable`, `provider_canceled`, `no_provider_record`, `order_not_payable`, `order_cancelled`); raw provider messages are never exposed.
- [LOCAL] Only full refunds; partial refund and dispute handling are not modelled.
