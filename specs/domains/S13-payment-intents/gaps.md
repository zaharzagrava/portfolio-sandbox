## Sibling-spec follow-ups

Written by `/speckit-plan` on 2026-10-10. Other capabilities' specs are not edited; each bullet is what that capability must adopt because S13 changes a name, shape or contract they rely on.

- **S10**: S13 does **not** call `OrderQueryService.getPayableOrder`; it keeps an order copy from `order.reserved|paid|cancelled` (`questions.md` CONTRACT 1, to avoid an `orders ↔ payments` module cycle). S10 must keep `userId`, `totalMinor`, `currency`, `reservedUntil` and a rising `orderVersion` in those events, and `getPayableOrder` may stay for other consumers. S10 adopts `PaymentQueryService.getPaymentStatus` through the `PaymentQueryStatusAdapter` that S13 ships in `orders/infra/payment-status.adapter.ts` (bound in `orders-core.module.ts`); S10 may restyle it. `payments.payment_failed` can now carry `paymentRef: null`, `reasonCode` and `paymentVersion` (`packages/contracts/src/payments/payment-events.ts`; `orders/payment-events.ts` re-exports it). A failed or cancelled payment is published for an order that may already be cancelled (S10 AS-48 makes that a no-op).
- **S14**: provide `LedgerService.recordPaymentCaptured({paymentId, userId, amountMinor, currency}, tx)` and `recordPaymentRefunded({paymentId, amountMinor, currency}, tx)` natively; S13 ships thin wrappers of these names over `post()` (deterministic journal ids `uuidv5('sale:'+paymentId)` / `uuidv5('refund:'+paymentId)`, idempotent) and S14 replaces their internals. S13 writes the **payment id** into `LedgerEntry.paymentId` for its journals while `settlement.listener.ts` still writes the provider reference (`OrderPaid.paymentRef`); S14 decides which the column holds. `Payment.ledgerEntries` and `LedgerEntry.payment` associations are removed (D-17); the `User` association in `ledger-entry.model.ts` (C4) and the `recordMarketplaceSale` caller in `reconciliation.jobs.ts` are S14's. `settlement.listener.ts` now builds its `OrderPaid` definition from `packages/contracts` instead of importing the orders barrel (no `payments → orders` import).
- **S15**: nothing changes in the payout contract; `finance-worker.module.ts` loses `PaymentResolutionJobs` (moved into the payments processor) and `payout.jobs.ts` still injects `Shop` (C6) until S15 uses tenancy's `ShopQueryService`.
- **S53**: the legacy consume path (G-27: `KafkaConsumerService.consume`, `KafkaTopicGroup.payments.*`, `OutboxService.notify`) has no caller in `payments` after S13; delete it. Topics `payments.requests`, `payments.responses`, `payments.dlq` are unused by S13 and can be retired in the contract release (data-model, migration 6).
- **S54**: the idempotency facility needed no change for S13 (header rules and codes already match); if its 8–128 `[A-Za-z0-9_-]` key rule or "failures before a record exists are not remembered" ever changes, `payment-intent.e2e-spec.ts` (AS-07, AS-09) fails.
- **S43 / S46**: reuse `CircuitBreaker` from `@app/common/resilience`; the payment-specific breaker configuration lives in `payments/infra/stripe-payment-provider.adapter.ts` and is not a shared module.
- **Billing capability (S17)**: `libs/domains/billing/infra/billing-gateway.port.ts` still calls `StripeService.createPaymentIntent` directly. S13 keeps that method on the thin client but removes its circuit breaker and the `is_load_test` branch; billing should send a charge command to payments instead (A28).
- **W03 / S48 (web, BFF)**: the pay step posts `POST /payments/intents` with `Idempotency-Key`, follows `payment.status` on `user:<id>` or polls `GET /payments/:id`, and for `requiresAction` hands `clientSecret` to the provider's client library. `packages/web/lib/types.ts:98-104` (`PENDING | PROCESSING | SUCCESS | FAILED | REFUNDED`) must be replaced by the `packages/contracts` payment schemas; the routes `/payment/by-key/:key`, `/payment/:id`, `/payment`, `/payment/stream` no longer exist.

## Notes from implementation pass 1

- Foundations written before their first run (the repositories, the transition service, the adapter and the accept path) so US1 ran green on its first complete attempt apart from test-side mistakes (a rate limit hit by the test itself, access tokens that expire when the test moves the clock); US5, US2, US3, US4 and US6 had their specs written and run first. Failures worth knowing: the unknown-outcome sweep must not reuse the key of the job it stands in for (a lost or dead job leaves its idempotency key spent); a breaker that opened at a fake-clock time later than the reset clock stays open across specs (`resetBreakers` in the e2e kit); the provider double must change its own record with a scripted answer, or a later lookup contradicts the answer given.
- `AS-44` lists "an unsupported currency" among the payloads the order-copy consumer dead-letters; `AS-13` lists `JPY` as a `422 currency_unsupported` at accept. Both hold: the consumer refuses non-EUR/USD/GBP orders, so the accept-time check can only fire on corrupt data, and `payment-intent.e2e-spec.ts` seeds the `JPY` copy directly.
- `AS-52` says "redelivered with backoff"; `research.md` R-9 implements it as durable wait jobs (the message is acknowledged). The behaviour the scenario specifies (nothing changes while unsettled, refunded once `COMPLETED`, "nothing to refund" on failure, dead letter and alert at 24 hours) is what `payment-refund.e2e-spec.ts` asserts.
- The T002 probe was not written as a separate throwaway: the idempotency rules it was to confirm (mandatory header, 8–128 `[A-Za-z0-9_-]`, `422` codes, `4xx` before a record not remembered, injected-clock expiry) were read in `idempotency.interceptor.ts` and are exercised by AS-07 to AS-09; the in-memory `TaskQueue` and `FakeClock`-driven `CircuitBreaker` are exercised by `payment-charge` (`bodySchema` dead letters) and `payment-breaker`.
